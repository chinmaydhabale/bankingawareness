## Part 1: Financial Markets, Stock Exchanges & Gold Trading

### 1.1 Indian Financial Market Architecture: Two Pillars
Indian Financial Market do main parts mein divide hota hai:

| Feature | Money Market (Mudra Bazaar) | Capital Market (Poonji Bazaar) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration (Tenure)** | Short-term (< 1 year / Upto 364 days) | Long-term (> 1 year / Infinite) |
| **Primary Regulator** | **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** | **Securities & Exchange Board of India (SEBI)** |
| **Core Instruments** | T-Bills, Commercial Paper, CDs, Call/Notice Money | Equities, Debentures, Preference Shares, Bonds |
| **Other Segments Regulated** | G-Secs (Gilt-Edged), Repo, Tri-party Repo, Forex | Bullion, Commodity Derivatives, EGRs, REITs, InvITs |

> [!NOTE]
> **Gilt-Edged Securities (G-Secs) ka Historical Logic:**  
> Government securities ko "Gilt-Edged" kyun kaha jata hai? British colonial era mein British Government dwara issue kiye gaye bond certificates ke edges (kinare) par **pure gold (gilt) ki leaf coating** hoti thi. Yeh gold coating is baat ka prateek thi ki is paper par sovereign guarantee hai, yeh 100% safe hai aur default ka zero risk hai. Tabhi se aaj tak Government Bonds aur Treasury Bills ko "Gilt-Edged Securities" kaha jata hai aur inka regulator strictly **RBI** hai.

### 1.3 Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) on NSE
SEBI ne physical gold trading ko financial system mein formally lana aur gold monetization ko promote karne ke liye **Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR)** frame kiya.

```
[ Physical Gold Deposited ] ---> [ SEBI Registered Vault Manager ] ---> [ Issues EGR into Demat Account ]
                                                                                   |
                                                                   [ Traded on NSE just like Equity Shares ]
                                                                                   |
[ Surrender EGR to Vault ] <--- [ Physical Gold Delivered back ] <-----------------+
```

#### EGR ke Crucial Rules & Exam Parameters:
1. **Regulator:** **SEBI** (Securities and Exchange Board of India).
2. **Trading Platform:** NSE (aur BSE par bhi approved).
3. **Asset Backing:** 100% physical gold SEBI-registered Vault Managers ke paas securely rakha hota hai. Gold ki purity 995 ya 999 fineness hoti hai.
4. **Tax Benefit:** EGRs ko purchase/hold karne par koi physical GST ya handling storage charges normal demat transactions ke alawa alag se nahi lagte, physical gold ki chori ya impurity ka risk zero hota hai.
5. **Trading Hours:**
   - **Monday to Friday:** **9:00 AM to 11:30 PM (Late Night)**.
   - *Logic:* Piyush Sir ne explain kiya ki international bullion markets (jaise US COMEX aur London Bullion Market Association - LBMA) raat ko open rehte hain, isliye domestic investors ko global price movements ka faida mil sake.
6. **Denominations & Slabs:**
   - **Minimum Trading Unit:** **100 mg (milligrams)** (chote se chota retail investor bhi buy kar sakta hai) se lekar **10 kg**.
   - **Physical Delivery / Withdrawal Unit:** Minimum **1 kg** (upto **100 kg** for bulk institutional players). Agar physical delivery chahiye, toh minimum 1 kg EGR surrender karna padega.

### 2.4 Foreign Exchange Remittance Limits (LRS vs Corporate)

#### 1. Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS)
- **Applicability:** Only Resident Individuals (including minors).
- **Annual Limit:** Maximum **USD $250,000** (ya equivalent foreign currency) per Financial Year (April - March).
- **Purposes Permitted:** Private visits abroad, gift/donation, going abroad for employment, emigration, maintenance of close relatives abroad, business travel, medical treatment, studies abroad, and capital account investments (buying shares/properties abroad).
- **THE GOLDEN EXEMPTION RULE:**
  > [!IMPORTANT]
  > **Bhutan aur Nepal ke liye LRS rule apply nahi hota!**  
  > Kyun? Kyunki Bhutan aur Nepal mein Indian Rupee (INR) officially legal tender ki tarah direct circulate hota hai. Vahan kisi foreign exchange conversion (USD) ki zarurat nahi hoti, isliye vahan LRS ki $250,000 limit lagu nahi hoti.

#### 2. Corporate Cross-Border Remittance (AD Category-II)
- Non-individual corporate entities ke cross-border commercial transactions ke liye limit **USD $2.5 Million** per financial year tak authorized hai under specific RBI-approved windows.

### 4.1 NEFT vs RTGS: In-Depth Comparison

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                 CENTRAL BANK SETTLEMENT RAILS                                     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Feature               | NEFT (National Electronic Funds)    | RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)   |
+-----------------------+-------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+

| Settlement Mechanism  | Deferred Net Settlement (DNS)       | Real Time Gross Settlement (Gross)  |
| Batch Frequency       | 48 half-hourly batches daily        | Continuous, immediate, transaction  |
```

#### STP (Straight Through Processing) Mode:
NEFT aur RTGS dono ab **STP (Straight Through Processing)** mode par kaam karte hain. Iska matlab hai ki sender ke mobile/net banking se lekar beneficiary ke account mein credit hone tak kisi bhi human bank employee ke manual intervention ki zarurat nahi hoti; CBS (Core Banking Solution) automated messaging se fund seconds mein credit kar deta hai.

#### Indo-Nepal Remittance Scheme under NEFT:
- **Eligible Scheme:** RBI aur Nepal Rastra Bank ke beech bilateral tie-up.
- **Bank Account Holders in India:** Nepal ke kisi bhi bank account mein **upto ₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh)** per transaction bhej sakte hain (saal mein kitni bhi baar).
- **Non-Account / Walk-in Cash Remitters:** Maximum **₹50,000 per transaction** bhej sakte hain, aur ek calendar year mein maximum **12 remittances** hi allow hoti hain.

### 4.2 BSBDA (Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account) Rules
Financial inclusion ko boost karne ke liye RBI ne BSBDA guidelines issue kiye hain:
1. **Zero Balance Account:** Minimum balance maintain karne ki koi obligation nahi hoti, zero balance par koi penalty nahi lagti.
2. **ATM / Debit Card:** Free Basic RuPay Debit Card issue hota hai bina kisi annual maintenance charge ke.
3. **Cash Withdrawal Quota:** **Minimum 4 free cash withdrawals per month** provide kiye jaate hain (isme own bank ATM, other bank ATM aur branch counter teeno shamil hain).
4. **The Digital Exemption Rule:**
   > [!IMPORTANT]
   > RBI circular ke mutabiq, digital transfers (jaise UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, online POS payments) ko **in 4 cash withdrawals mein count nahi kiya ja sakta!** Digital transactions completely unconstrained hone chahiye.
5. **Charges Beyond Free Quota:** 4 free cash withdrawals ke baad agar customer 5th cash withdrawal karta hai, toh banks nominal charges laga sakte hain (jaise SBI **₹15 + applicable GST** per transaction charge karta hai).

### 5.2 Account Classification Stages: Active se Unclaimed Tak
Bank accounts ki operational status ko time ke hisab se 3 stages mein classify kiya jata hai:

```
[ Customer-Induced Activity Stops ]
                |
                v (After 1 Year of inactivity)
     +-----------------------+
     |   INACTIVE ACCOUNT    |  --> Alerts sent via SMS/Email to customer
     +-----------------------+
                |
                v (After 2 Years of inactivity)
     +-----------------------+
     | INOPERATIVE / DORMANT |  --> Transactions frozen; requires fresh KYC to reactivate
     +-----------------------+
                |
                v (After 10 Years of inactivity)
     +-----------------------+
     |  UNCLAIMED DEPOSIT    |  --> Transferred to RBI's DEAF Fund under BR Act Sec 26A
     +-----------------------+
```

1. **Inactive Account:** Agar account mein lagatar **1 saal (12 months)** tak customer dwara koi transaction (deposit, withdrawal, transfer) na kiya jaye.
2. **Inoperative / Dormant Account:** Agar account mein lagatar **2 saal (24 months)** tak customer-induced activity na ho. Bank is account se debit freeze kar deta hai. Reactivate karne ke liye customer ko branch jaakar fresh KYC documents submit karne hote hain (reactivation par koi charge nahi lagta).
3. **Unclaimed Deposit:** Agar lagatar **10 saal (120 months)** tak account mein koi transaction na ho, ya Fixed Deposit (FD) mature hone ke 10 saal tak claim na kiya jaye, toh use "Unclaimed Deposit" declare kiya jata hai.

## Part 6: Indian Currency, Banknotes & CBDC

### 6.1 Legal Framework: RBI Act vs Coinage Act
Bharat mein currency circulation aur issue do alag-alag kanoonon ke tahat govern hota hai:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                 INDIAN CURRENCY LEGAL ARCHITECTURE                                |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Parameter               | Banknotes (Paper & Digital)       | Coins & One Rupee Note              |
+-------------------------+-----------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
| Governing Statute       | RBI Act, 1934                     | The Coinage Act, 2011               |
| Issuing Authority       | Reserve Bank of India (RBI)       | Government of India (Ministry of    |
|                         | (Section 22 sole monopoly)        | Finance)                            |
| Maximum Denomination    | Up to ₹10,000 (Section 24)        | Coins up to ₹1,000 denomination     |
| Promise Clause Text     | "I promise to pay the bearer the  | NO PROMISE CLAUSE                   |
|                         |  sum of ... Signed by Governor"   | (Intrinsic sovereign monetary unit) |
| Signatory Authority     | Governor, Reserve Bank of India   | Finance Secretary, GoI              |
| Production Locations    | 4 Currency Presses (BRBNMPL:      | 4 India Government Mints:           |
|                         | Salboni & Mysuru; SPMCIL:         | Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, NOIDA   |
|                         | Dewas & Nashik)                   |                                     |
+-------------------------+-----------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
```

#### Key Sections of RBI Act, 1934:
- **Section 22:** Sole right to issue currency notes in India vests exclusively with the RBI.
- **Section 24:** Power to issue denominations up to **₹10,000**.
- **Section 25:** Banknote design, character, and materials Central Board of RBI recommend karti hai aur Central Government approve karti hai.
- **Section 26:** Every banknote is legal tender throughout India guaranteed by the Central Government.
  - **Section 26(2):** Central Government on recommendation of the Central Board can declare any series of banknotes as ceases to be legal tender (yehi provision demonetization mein use hua tha).

### 6.2 Banknote Features, Star Series & Classification Rules
1. **Polymer Banknotes:** Substrate Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene (BOPP) hota hai. World mein pehli baar **1988 mein Australia** ne polymer notes launch kiye the.
2. **Mahatma Gandhi (MG) Series:** First introduced in **1996**, jisne Ashoka Pillar watermark ko Mahatma Gandhi portrait watermark se replace kiya. (Lekin ₹1 note par aaj bhi Ashoka Pillar aur ₹1 coin design rehta hai).
3. **Star (*) Series Banknotes:** 2006 mein introduce hua. Note printing ke dauran agar serial number wale packet mein koi defective note nikal jaye, toh uski jagah exact number sequence maintain karne ke liye **prefix aur serial number ke beech mein star (*)** laga kar replacement note print kiya jata hai.
4. **Banknote Categories (RBI Reserve Bank of India Note Refund Rules):**
   - **Soiled Note:** Normal use se ganda ya thoda sa fita hua note, ya do tukde jod kar banaya gaya note jisme poora note present ho. Har bank branch exchange karne ke liye legally bound hai.
   - **Mutilated Note:** Jiska ek hissa gayab ho ya jo 2 se zyada tukdon mein toota ho.
   - **Imperfect Note:** Jiska color completely wash ho chuka ho, shrink ho gaya ho, ya illegible ho chuka ho.
5. **Bank Exchange Incentives:**
   - Banks ko soiled notes exchange karne ke liye RBI **₹2 per packet (of 100 notes)** incentive deta hai.
   - Mutilated notes adjudicate/exchange karne par banks ko **₹2 per note** ka service fee milta hai.
6. **Pre-2005 Banknotes:** 2005 se pehle print huye notes ke reverse bottom center par printing ka year nahi hota tha. Inhe circulation se withdraw kiya ja chuka hai (RBI issue offices par exchange hote hain).

### 8.2 Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) Corridor
RBI ke monetary policy interest rates ek tight corridor ke andar operate karte hain:

```
[ CEILING RATE: MSF / Bank Rate ] = Repo Rate + 25 bps (0.25%)
                ^
                |   <--- Corridor Width = 50 bps (0.50%)
                v
[ FLOOR RATE: SDF (Standing Deposit) ] = Repo Rate - 25 bps (0.25%)
```

1. **Policy Repo Rate:** Woh benchmark rate jis par RBI scheduled banks ko overnight short-term liquidity provide karta hai government securities ke collateral par.
2. **SDF (Standing Deposit Facility):** The floor rate. Banks RBI ke paas excess cash deposit karte hain **bina kisi G-Sec collateral ke**. SDF = **Repo Rate - 0.25%**.
3. **MSF (Marginal Standing Facility):** The penal ceiling rate. Scheduled banks emergency overnight funding lete hain dipping into SLR quota. MSF = **Repo Rate + 0.25%**.
4. **Bank Rate:** The discount rate for long-term rediscounting of commercial bills without repo agreement. Hamesha MSF ke barabar aligned rehta hai (Repo + 25 bps).

### 10.2 Large Credits Reporting: CRILC & LEI Mandate
Bank defaults ko early identify karne aur corporate financial monitoring ke liye do critical tools hain:

1. **CRILC (Central Repository of Information on Large Credits):**
   - Setup by: Reserve Bank of India.
   - Mandatory Reporting: Sabhi scheduled commercial banks aur NBFCs ko har us borrower ki exposure detail report karni hoti hai jiska aggregate credit exposure **₹5 Crore aur usse upar** hai.
   - Frequency: **Monthly reporting** (SMA-2 accounts ki reporting weekly ki jaati hai).
2. **LEI (Legal Entity Identifier):**
   - Structure: **20-Digit Unique Alphanumeric Global Code** (ISO 17442 standard).
   - India Operator: Legal Entity Identifier India Limited (LEIL - CCIL subsidiary).
   - Threshold: Har non-individual borrower ya corporate entity jiska aggregate credit exposure ya RTGS/NEFT payment transaction **₹50 Crore aur usse upar** hai, uske liye 20-digit LEI quote karna mandatory hai.

### 10.3 SARFAESI Act, 2002 & The 4 Golden Exemptions
SARFAESI Act banks ko power deta hai ki bina kisi court ya kachehri ke chakkar kate, secured property ko seize aur auction karke loan recover kar sakein. Demand notice 60 din ka Section 13(2) ke tehat diya jata hai.

> [!WARNING]
> **SARFAESI Ki 4 Golden Exemptions (Jahan SARFAESI Bilkul Lagu Nahi Hota!):**  
> 1. **Agricultural Land:** Kisaan ki kheti ki zameen par SARFAESI ke tehat koi action ya seizure nahi ho sakta.  
> 2. **Unsecured Loans:** Personal loan, credit card debt ya bina security wale loans par SARFAESI nahi lagta.  
> 3. **Remaining Debt Under ₹1 Lakh:** Agar loan balance **₹1,00,000 se kam** bacha hai, toh SARFAESI trigger nahi ho sakta.  
> 4. **Remaining Balance Under 20%:** Agar borrower ne principal + interest ka **80% se zyada chuka diya hai** aur bacha hua debt total claim ka **20% se kam** hai, toh SARFAESI proceed nahi ho sakta.

### 12.4 Self-Regulatory Organisations (SRO) Framework
RBI ne financial sectors mein self-governance aur ethical conduct maintain karne ke liye omnibus SRO framework jari kiya hai:
- **Eligibility:** Companies Act, 2013 ke **Section 8** ke tahat incorporated non-profit company honi chahiye.
- **Minimum Net Worth:** Minimum **₹2 Crore**.
- **Representation:** Sector ke kam se kam **ek-tihai (33%) entities** ya **75% asset size / minimum 100 members** ka pratinidhitwa hona chahiye.
- **Recognized SROs:**
  - **FIMMDA:** Fixed Income Money Market and Derivatives Association (Bond and Money Markets).
  - **Sahamati:** Account Aggregator ecosystem ka official SRO.
  - **FIDC (Finance Industry Development Council):** NBFCs ka representative SRO.
  - **Sa-Dhan & MFIN (Microfinance Institutions Network):** NBFC-MFIs ke recognized SROs.
  - **FACE (Fintech Association for Consumer Empowerment):** Digital lending aur FinTech companies ka SRO.

### 14.2 DICGC (Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation)
