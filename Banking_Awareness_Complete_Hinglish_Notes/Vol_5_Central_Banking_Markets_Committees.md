## 🏧 Module 27: Bank ATMs in India & Deployment Dynamics

### 1. ATM Basics & How It Works — *[PDF Page 147]*
* **Full Form:** **Automated Teller Machine**.
* Ek computerized telecommunications device jo bank customers ko bina bank branch jaye cash withdrawal, balance enquiry, pin change aur fund transfer jaisi financial services 24x7 deta hai.
* **Key Components:**
  * **Input Devices:** Card Reader (EMV Chip reader), Keypad / Pinpad.
  * **Output Devices:** Display Screen, Receipt Printer, Cash Dispenser (Cassettes), Cash Deposit Slot (in Cash Recyclers - CRMs).

---

### 2. Types of ATMs by Ownership & Operation Model — *[PDF Pages 149–150]*

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        ATM TYPES BY OWNERSHIP                            |
+----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------+
| Bank-Owned ATM       | Brown Label ATM (BLA)     | White Label ATM (WLA) |
+----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------+
| Hardware, software,  | Hardware owned by service | Setup, owned and      |
| cash & network       | provider/vendor; Cash &   | operated by NON-BANKS |
| completely owned     | connectivity provided by  | under PSS Act 2007    |
| by the Bank itself   | the Sponsor Bank          | (e.g., Indicash, India1)|
+----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------+
```

1. **Bank-Owned ATM:** Machine, location, network aur cash sab kuch bank ka apna hota hai.
2. **Brown Label ATM (BLA) [PDF Page 150]:**
   * Ek hybrid model hota hai:
     * **Vendor / Service Provider:** Machine hardware khareedta hai, lease par space leta hai aur machine maintain karta hai.
     * **Sponsor Bank:** Cash supply karta hai aur apne banking network/core banking software ki connectivity provide karta hai. Machine par sponsor bank ka logo hota hai.
3. **White Label ATM (WLA) [PDF Page 149]:**
   * Aise ATMs jinhe **Non-Banking Companies** setup, own aur operate karti hain.
   * Authorized under the **Payment & Settlement Systems Act, 2007** by RBI.
   * In ATMs par kisi bank ka logo nahi hota, balki WLA operator company ka apna brand hota hai.
   * Cash handling: WLA operator kisi sponsor scheduled commercial bank ke sath tie-up karke cash manage karta hai.
   * *Example:* **Tata Communications Payment Solutions Ltd (Indicash)** - India's 1st WLA; **India1 Payments Ltd** (formerly BTI Payments), **Muthoot Finance**, **Vakrangee**.

---

### 3. Color-Coded ATMs (Specialized Purpose ATMs)

* 🟢 **Green Label ATM:** Exclusively for **Agricultural Transactions** (kisaano ke liye kheti se jude transactions).
* 🟡 **Yellow Label ATM:** Exclusively for **E-Commerce Transactions** (online shopping payments & cash delivery).
* 🟠 **Orange Label ATM:** Exclusively for **Share Transactions / Share Trading**.
* 🌸 **Pink Label ATM:** Exclusively for **Women Customers** (women safety aur convenience ke liye).
* 🟤 **Brown Label ATM:** Cost-sharing outsourced ATM model.
* ⚪ **White Label ATM:** Non-bank operated commercial ATM.

---

### 4. ATM Transaction Rules & Fee Ceilings — *[PDF Page 151]*
* **Free Transactions at Own Bank ATMs:** Minimum **5 Free Transactions** (financial + non-financial) per month.
* **Free Transactions at Other Bank ATMs:**
  * **6 Metro Centres** (Mumbai, New Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad): Minimum **3 Free Transactions** per month.
  * **Non-Metro Centres:** Minimum **5 Free Transactions** per month.
* **Customer Charges Beyond Free Limit:** Maximum **₹21 per financial transaction** (+ applicable taxes).
* **Interchange Fee (Bank-to-Bank reimbursement):** ₹17 per financial transaction and ₹6 per non-financial transaction.

## 💵 Module 29: Money Market Instruments

### 1. Money Market Kya Hai? — *[PDF Page 164]*
* Ek aisa financial market jahan **Short-Term Funds (up to 1 Year maturity)** ki borrowing aur lending hoti hai.
* Yeh highly liquid market hota hai jisme low default risk hota hai.
* Participants: RBI, Commercial Banks, Primary Dealers (PDs), Financial Institutions, Mutual Funds, Corporate Treasuries.

---

### 2. Treasury Bills (T-Bills) — *[PDF Page 165]*
* **Issuer:** Exclusively issued by the **Central Government (Government of India)** through RBI.
* **State Governments T-Bills issue NAHI kar sakte!** (State governments market loans issue karti hain jise State Development Loans - SDL kehte hain).
* **Historical Fact [PDF Page 165]:** Bharat me sabse pehle Treasury Bills **1917** me issue kiye gaye the.
* **Maturity Tenors:** Standard 3 tenors:
  1. **91-Day T-Bills** (Auctioned weekly)
  2. **182-Day T-Bills** (Auctioned fortnightly)
  3. **364-Day T-Bills** (Auctioned fortnightly)
* **Minimum Investment:** **₹25,000** (and in multiples of ₹25,000 thereof).
* **Zero Coupon Feature:** T-Bills par koi regular interest nahi milta. Yeh **Discounted Price par issue hote hain aur Par (Face Value) par redeem hote hain**.
  * *Example:* ₹100 face value ka T-Bill ₹97 me issue hua, maturity par pura ₹100 mila $\rightarrow$ ₹3 is the investor's return!

---

### 3. Cash Management Bills (CMBs) — *[PDF Page 166]*
* **Introduced In:** **2010** by Government of India in consultation with RBI.
* **Purpose:** Central Government ke cash flows me temporary mismatches ko meet karne ke liye.
* **Tenor:** **Less than 91 Days** (91 din se kam maturity ke bills ko CMBs kehte hain). Issued at a discount like T-Bills.

---

### 4. Commercial Paper (CP) — *[PDF Page 167]*
* **Introduced In:** **1990** on the recommendations of the **Vaghul Committee**.
* **Nature:** Ek **Unsecured Money Market Instrument** jo Promissory Note ke form me issue hota hai.
* **Eligible Issuers:** Highly rated Corporates, Primary Dealers (PDs), aur All India Financial Institutions (AIFIs).
* **Eligibility Conditions for Corporates:** Tangible net worth $\ge$ ₹4 Crore; sanctioned working capital limit; credit rating of minimum **A2** (CRISIL/ICRA).
* **Maturity Period:** Minimum **7 Days** to Maximum **1 Year**.
* **Denomination:** Minimum **₹5 Lakh** (and in multiples of ₹5 Lakh thereof).

---

### 5. Certificates of Deposit (CD)
* **Introduced In:** **1989** on the recommendations of the **Vaghul Committee**.
* **Nature:** A negotiable, dematerialized money market promissory note against deposits made at a bank.
* **Eligible Issuers:**
  1. **Scheduled Commercial Banks** (excluding RRBs and Local Area Banks).
  2. **All India Financial Institutions (AIFIs)**.
* **Maturity Period:**
  * If issued by Commercial Banks: **7 Days to 1 Year**.
  * If issued by AIFIs: **1 Year to 3 Years**.
* **Denomination:** Minimum **₹1 Lakh** (and in multiples of ₹1 Lakh).

---

### 6. Call, Notice & Term Money Markets — *[PDF Pages 168–169]*
Interbank uncollateralised lending market:
* **Call Money:** Loan period for **1 Day / Overnight** (aaj liya, kal wapas).
* **Notice Money:** Loan period from **2 Days up to 14 Days** (bina collateral ke).
* **Term Money:** Loan period from **15 Days up to 1 Year**.
* **Borrowing & Lending Limits [PDF Pages 168–169]:**
  * **Scheduled Commercial Banks (Borrowing Limit):** Maximum 100% of Capital Funds (Tier 1 + Tier 2 capital) on a daily average basis in a reporting fortnight; maximum 125% on any single day.
  * **SCBs (Lending Limit):** Maximum 50% of Capital Funds in a reporting fortnight.
  * **Co-operative Banks:** No prudential limits on call/notice money.
  * **Primary Dealers (PDs):** Borrowing limit up to 225% of Net Owned Funds (NOF) on average.

---

### 7. Ways and Means Advances (WMA) & Overdraft Facility — *[PDF Page 170]*
* **Legal Basis:** Section 17(5) of RBI Act, 1934.
* **Purpose:** Central aur State Governments ke receipts aur payments ke beech temporary cash mismatch ko bridge karne ke liye RBI dwara diya jane wala temporary credit facility.
* **Repayment Period:** WMA loan **3 Months (90 Days)** ke andar repay karna mandatory hota hai.
* **Interest Rate:** Charged at RBI Repo Rate.
* **Overdraft (OD) Limits for State Governments / UTs [PDF Page 170]:**
  * State Government ek baar me **consecutive 14 Days** se zyada Overdraft me nahi reh sakti!
  * Ek quarter (3 mahine) me maximum **36 Days** se zyada OD allow nahi hota. Agar 14 din ya 36 din cross hote hain, toh RBI state ke payments freeze kar deta hai!

---

### 8. Collateralised Instruments: Repos, TREPS, BA & Commercial Bills — *[PDF Pages 171–173]*
* **Repo (Repurchase Agreement):** G-Secs bech kar short-term funds borrow karna with an agreement to buy back at a future date at a pre-determined price.
* **Reverse Repo:** Cash park karke securities buy karna.
* **TREPS (Tri-Party Repo System):** Facilitated by CCIL (Clearing Corporation of India Ltd) jahan third-party agent collateral evaluation aur settlement guarantee deta hai.
* **Banker’s Acceptance (BA) [PDF Page 171]:** Ek aisa bill of exchange jise bank unconditionally accept aur guarantee kar deta hai payment ki date par. Maturity usually 30 days to 180 days hoti hai.
* **Letter of Credit (LC) [PDF Page 172]:** Importer ke bank dwara exporter ko diya gaya undertaking ki agar documents contract terms meet karein toh bank payment ki guarantee leta hai.

## 🏛️ Module 30: Everything about RBI (Reserve Bank of India)

### 1. Genesis, History & Nationalization — *[PDF Page 174]*
* **Royal Commission on Indian Currency and Finance (Hilton Young Commission - 1926)** ki report ke basis par establish hua.
* **Act:** **The Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934**.
* **Commenced Operations:** **1 April 1935** (original share capital of ₹5 Crore divided into shares of ₹100 each, fully paid up as a private shareholders' bank).
* **Head Office Movement:** Commenced in **Kolkata**, permanently shifted to **Mumbai in 1937**.
* **Nationalization:** **1 January 1949** ko **Reserve Bank (Transfer to Public Ownership) Act, 1948** ke tehat nationalize kiya gaya.
* **Central Bank to Other Nations:** RBI ne **Burma (Myanmar)** ke central bank ke roop me 1947 tak aur **Pakistan** ke central bank ke roop me June 1948 tak kaam kiya!

### 5. Banknotes & Coinage Rules — *[PDF Pages 181–182]*
* **Language Panel on Banknotes [PDF Page 182]:**
  * Currency note ke language panel me **15 Languages** hoti hain.
  * Plus center me prominent **Hindi** aur back par **English** hoti hai.
  * Total languages appearing on the banknote = **17 Languages**!
* **Coinage Act, 2011 [PDF Page 181]:**
  * Coins can be issued up to the denomination of **₹1,000**.
  * Currently in circulation: 50 paise, ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10, and ₹20.
  * Coins up to 50 paise are called **"Small Coins"** (legal tender up to ₹10). Coins of ₹1 and above are **"Rupee Coins"** (legal tender up to ₹1,000).
* **Section 22 of RBI Act, 1934:** RBI has the **sole right to issue banknotes in India** (from ₹2 to ₹10,000 notes).
* **₹1 Note Exception:** One Rupee note is issued by the **Ministry of Finance, Government of India** and bears the signature of the **Finance Secretary**, NOT the RBI Governor!
* **Currency Chest:** Bank branches authorized by RBI to store cash balances and banknotes on behalf of RBI.

## 🎯 Module 31: RBI Monetary Policy Framework

### 1. Flexible Inflation Targeting (FIT) Framework — *[PDF Page 183]*
* **Statutory Basis:** **May 2016** me RBI Act, 1934 ko amend kiya gaya to implement the flexible inflation targeting framework.
* **Inflation Target:** Headline **Consumer Price Index (CPI) Combined inflation of 4%**.
* **Tolerance Band:** **$\pm$ 2% (i.e., Range: 2.0% to 6.0%)**.
* **Failure to Maintain Target:** Agar average inflation consecutive **3 Quarters (9 Months)** tak upper ceiling (6%) se upar ya lower floor (2%) se niche rahe, toh RBI ko Central Government ko written report submit karni padti hai jisme reasons, remedial steps aur timeline batana hota hai.

---

### 2. Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) — *[PDF Pages 183–184]*
* **Constituted under:** **Section 45ZB of the RBI Act, 1934**.
* **Total Members:** **6 Members**:
  * **3 Members from RBI:**
    1. Governor of RBI — **Ex-officio Chairperson**
    2. Deputy Governor in charge of Monetary Policy — Member
    3. One officer of the RBI nominated by Central Board — Member
  * **3 External Members Appointed by Central Government:**
    * Appointed for a term of **4 Years**; NOT eligible for re-appointment.
* **Meetings & Quorum:** MPC meets at least **4 times in a year**. Quorum for meeting is **4 Members** (at least one must be Governor or DG).
* **Voting:** Each member has 1 vote. In case of a tie, the **Governor has a Casting Vote (Second Vote)**!

---

### 3. Quantitative Tools of Monetary Policy — *[PDF Pages 185–186]*

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     LIQUIDITY ADJUSTMENT FACILITY (LAF)                  |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| UPPER CEILING  -> Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) [Repo Rate + 0.25%]   |
| POLICY RATE    -> Repo Rate (Key Policy Signaling Rate)                 |
| LOWER FLOOR    -> Standing Deposit Facility (SDF)  [Repo Rate - 0.25%]   |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Repo Rate (Policy Rate):** Woh rate jispar RBI commercial banks ko short-term loans deti hai against approved government securities collateral.
2. **Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) [PDF Page 185]:**
   * **Introduced In:** **8 April 2022** under Section 17 of RBI Act.
   * **Purpose:** System se excess liquidity ko absorb karna **BINA KOI COLLATERAL / G-SECS DIYE (Uncollateralised)**!
   * Replaced Fixed Rate Reverse Repo as the operative lower floor of the LAF corridor.
3. **Marginal Standing Facility (MSF):**
   * Overnight emergency credit window for Scheduled Commercial Banks facing severe liquidity squeeze.
   * Banks can dip into their statutory SLR quota up to a specified percentage to borrow funds. Operates at a penal spread over Repo rate.
4. **Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR):**
   * **Section 42(1) of the RBI Act, 1934**.
   * Commercial banks ko apne Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) ka specified percentage **Cash ke roop me RBI ke paas rakhna hota hai**.
   * **RBI CRR balances par KOI INTEREST NAHI DETA**!
5. **Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR):**
   * **Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949**.
   * Banks ko apne paas liquid assets (Cash, Gold, Approved G-Secs) ke roop me maintain karna padta hai. Statutory maximum cap: **40%**.
6. **Open Market Operations (OMO):** Outright purchase/sale of Government securities by RBI in secondary market to inject/absorb durable liquidity.
7. **Market Stabilization Scheme (MSS):** 2004 me introduce hua for sterilizing massive foreign exchange capital inflows by issuing special MSS bonds.

---

### 4. Qualitative Tools of Monetary Policy — *[PDF Page 186]*
* **Margin Requirements (LTV - Loan to Value):** Borrowers ke margin money requirement ko badha ya ghata kar loan demand control karna.
* **Moral Suasion:** Periodic meetings me banks ke CEOs ko informal persuasion ya advisory dena.
* **Credit Rationing / Selective Credit Control:** Specific sectors (jaise speculative real estate ya shares) ke loans par quotas ya ceilings lagana.

## 📈 Module 32: Capital Market, Stock Exchanges & Trading Concepts

### 1. Capital Market Overview — *[PDF Page 187]*
* Aisa financial market jahan **Long-Term Funds (maturity greater than 1 Year)** ki raising aur trading hoti hai (shares, debentures, bonds).
* Regulated by: **SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)**, established on 12 April 1988, given statutory powers under SEBI Act, 1992.
* Divided into:
  1. **Primary Market (New Issues Market):** Jahan nayi securities pehli baar create aur issue hoti hain.
  2. **Secondary Market (Stock Market):** Jahan already issued securities investors ke beech khareedi aur bechi jaati hain.

---

### 2. Primary Market Operations: IPO, FPO & Issue Types — *[PDF Page 188]*
* **IPO (Initial Public Offering) [PDF Page 188]:** Jab ek unlisted private company pehli baar apne shares general public ko offer karke stock exchange par list hoti hai.
* **FPO (Follow-on Public Offering) [PDF Page 188]:** Ek already listed public company jab expansion ya debt repayment ke liye dubara public se fresh capital raise karti hai.
* **Rights Issue:** Existing shareholders ko unki shareholding ke proportion me discounted rate par naye shares offer karna.
* **Bonus Issue:** Free shares issued to existing shareholders out of accumulated retained reserves (capitalization of reserves).
* **Private Placement / QIP (Qualified Institutional Placement):** Selected institutional buyers (mutual funds, insurance companies) ko shares issue karna without public prospectus.
* **ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount):** IPO apply karte waqt applicant ka paisa bank account se kat'ta nahi hai, balki sirf block ho jata hai; shares allot hone par hi paisa debit hota hai, otherwise unblock ho jata hai.

---

### 3. Stock Exchanges & Market Indices — *[PDF Pages 187, 193]*

#### 🏢 1. Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE):
* **Asia's Oldest Stock Exchange**, established in **1875** as *"The Native Share & Stock Brokers' Association"*.
* Located at Dalal Street, Mumbai.
* **S&P BSE Sensex (Sensitive Index) [PDF Page 193]:**
  * Comprises **30 Well-established, financially sound companies** across key sectors.
  * **Base Year:** **1978–79** (Base index value = **100**).
  * **Launch Date:** **1 January 1986** (Sensex traded at 561 points on launch date).
  * Calculated using **Free-Float Market Capitalization** weighted methodology.

#### 🏢 2. National Stock Exchange of India (NSE):
* Incorporated in **1992**, operationalised in **1994** on the recommendations of the **Pherwani Committee**.
* Bharat ka largest electronic screen-based stock exchange.
* **Nifty 50 Index:** Comprises **50 Leading diversified companies**.
  * **Base Year:** **1995** (Base index value = **1,000**).

---

### 4. Depositories & Clearing Corporations — *[PDF Pages 189–190]*
* **Dematerialization (DEMAT):** Physical paper share certificates ko electronic/digital balance me convert karna.
* **Depositories (Digital Vault for Securities) [PDF Page 190]:** Bharat me sirf **2 Registered Depositories** hain:
  1. **NSDL (National Securities Depository Limited):** First depository, established **August 1996**, promoted by IDBI, UTI and NSE.
  2. **CDSL (Central Depository Services India Limited):** Established **February 1999**, promoted by BSE and State Bank of India.
* **Clearing Corporations [PDF Page 189]:** Trade settlement aur counterparty risk guarantee provide karte hain:
  * **NSE Clearing Limited** (formerly NSCCL) — subsidiary of NSE.
  * **Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL)** — subsidiary of BSE. Settlement cycle in India is **T+1 Settlement** (moving to T+0 / instant settlement).

---

### 5. Essential Trading & Market Concepts — *[PDF Pages 191–192]*
* **Bull Market vs Bear Market:**
  * **Bull:** Market jab lagatar upar ja raha ho (optimism).
  * **Bear:** Market jab 20% ya usse zyada gir chuka ho (pessimism).
* **Long Position vs Short Position [PDF Page 191]:**
  * **Going Long (Buy):** Jab investor ko lagta hai ki price badhega, toh pehle khareedta hai aur baad me unche daam par bechta hai.
  * **Going Short (Sell):** Jab trader ko lagta hai ki price girega, toh bina share own kiye pehle high price par sell karta hai aur baad me lower price par buyback karke delivery complete karta hai.
* **Buyback of Shares [PDF Page 192]:** Company apne hi shares market se premium price par repurchase karti hai taaki promoter holding badha sake aur EPS (Earnings Per Share) improve ho sake.
* **Market Capitalization Categories [PDF Page 82]:**
  * **Large Cap:** Top 100 companies by market capitalization (ranks 1 to 100).
  * **Mid Cap:** Next 150 companies (ranks 101 to 250).
  * **Small Cap:** Companies ranking **251st onwards**.

## 📊 Module 33: Mutual Funds Complete Guide

### 1. Mutual Fund Concept & 3-Tier Structure — *[PDF Pages 71–75]*
* **Definition:** Ek collective investment vehicle jo hazaro investors se paisa pool (ikatha) karta hai aur use professional fund managers ke zariye diversified equities, bonds aur money market securities me invest karta hai.
* Regulated strictly by **SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996**.
* **Structure:** SEBI guidelines ke mutabik India me Mutual Funds ka **3-Tier Structure** hota hai taaki investors ka paisa 100% surakshit rahe:

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     3-TIER MUTUAL FUND STRUCTURE                         |
+---------------------+---------------------+------------------------------+
| 1. THE SPONSOR      | 2. TRUST & TRUSTEES | 3. AMC (Asset Mgmt Co.)      |
| Promoter who sets   | Protectors of fund; | Employs Fund Managers who    |
| up the fund with    | Independent board;  | manage investments; collects |
| SEBI approval       | Oversees AMC        | management fee               |
+---------------------+---------------------+------------------------------+
| CUSTODIAN (Tier 4 Security):                                             |
| Holds all shares & bonds in safe custody, completely separate from AMC!  |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **The Fund Sponsor [PDF Page 72]:** Promoter jo SEBI ke paas approach karta hai. Sponsor Indian Trust Act, 1882 ke under Public Trust create karta hai.
2. **Trust & Trustees [PDF Page 73]:** Fund ke protectors hote hain. Kam se kam do-tihaai (2/3rd) trustees independent hone chahiye. Yeh ensure karte hain ki AMC SEBI regulations ke anusar kaam kare.
3. **Asset Management Company (AMC) [PDF Page 80]:** SEBI approved operating company jo actual investments manage karti hai (e.g., SBI Funds Management Ltd, HDFC AMC).
4. **Custodian [PDF Page 74]:** Ek independent entity jo fund ke securities/shares ko physically/digitally safe custody me rakhti hai taaki AMC koi fraud na kar sake! (e.g., Stock Holding Corporation of India, Citi).
5. **Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) [PDF Page 74]:** Investor servicing, application processing, KYC, aur dividend payouts handle karta hai (e.g., CAMS, KFin Technologies).

---

### 2. History of Mutual Funds in India (4 Phases) — *[PDF Pages 76–77]*
* **Phase 1 (1963–1987) — UTI Monopoly:**
  * Parliament ke Act ke zariye **Unit Trust of India (UTI)** 1963 me establish hua (by RBI). 1964 me India ka pehla open-ended mutual fund scheme **US-64 (Unit Scheme 1964)** launch hua.
* **Phase 2 (1987–1993) — Public Sector Entry:**
  * Non-UTI public sector banks aur institutions ne funds launch kiye. **SBI Mutual Fund (1987)** pehla bank-sponsored fund tha, followed by Canbank, PNB, LIC, GIC.
* **Phase 3 (1993–2003) — Private Sector Entry [PDF Page 76]:**
  * SEBI Act 1992 aane ke baad private sector ko permission mili. **Kothari Pioneer (1993)** pehla private fund tha (later merged with Franklin Templeton).
* **Phase 4 (2003 onwards) — Post-Bifurcation & Massive Expansion:**
  * UTI Act repealed in 2003; UTI was bifurcated into Specified Undertaking of UTI (SUUTI) and UTI Mutual Fund.
* **AUM Milestones [PDF Page 77]:**
  * Indian Mutual Fund Industry ka AUM August 2017 me **₹20 Lakh Crore** cross kiya.
  * November 2020 me **₹30 Lakh Crore** cross kiya.
  * July 2022 tak AUM **₹37.75 Lakh Crore** tak expand ho gaya!

---

### 3. Classification of Mutual Fund Schemes — *[PDF Pages 78–82]*

#### 🔸 By Structure:
* **Open-ended Schemes [PDF Page 79]:** Perpetual hoti hain. Year-round kisi bhi business day par current NAV par buy aur redeem ki ja sakti hain. No fixed maturity.
* **Close-ended Schemes [PDF Page 79]:** Fixed maturity date hoti hai. NFO (New Fund Offer) ke baad units stock exchange par trade hoti hain; redemption strictly maturity date par hota hai.
* **Interval Schemes:** Open-ended aur Close-ended ka combination jahan specific intervals par buy/sell allowed hota hai.

#### 🔹 By Investment Style / Asset Class:
* **Equity Funds:** Large Cap, Mid Cap, Small Cap, Multi Cap, Flexi Cap, Sectoral/Thematic, ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme - 3 years lock-in, Section 80C deduction).
* **Debt Funds:** Liquid Funds, Overnight Funds, Short Duration Funds, Corporate Bond Funds, Gilt Funds.
* **Hybrid Funds:** Balanced Advantage Funds, Aggressive Hybrid (equity + debt mix).

#### 🔄 Systematic Plans & Dividend (IDCW) Options — *[PDF Pages 78, 81]*:
* **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan):** Har mahine/quarter ek fixed amount invest karna (Rupee Cost Averaging).
* **STP (Systematic Transfer Plan):** Ek liquid fund se equity fund me regular fixed paisa shift karna.
* **SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan) [PDF Page 78]:** Monthly income pane ke liye fund se har mahine ek fixed amount nikalna.
* **Growth Option vs Dividend Option (IDCW) [PDF Page 81]:**
  * **Growth:** Profits re-invest hote hain, NAV badhta hai.
  * **Dividend (IDCW):** Interim cash payout milta hai; dividend pay hote hi scheme ka **NAV payout amount ke barabar gir jata hai**!

---

### 4. AMFI & Net Asset Value (NAV) — *[PDF Pages 80, 83]*
* **AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India):** Established in 1995. Non-profit apex body of all SEBI registered asset management companies. Slogan: *"Mutual Funds Sahi Hai"*.
* **Net Asset Value (NAV) Formula:**
$$\text{NAV} = \frac{\text{Total Market Value of Scheme Assets} - \text{Scheme Liabilities & Expenses}}{\text{Total Number of Outstanding Units}}$$
NAV calculated daily on every business day after market hours.
