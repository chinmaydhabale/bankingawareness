## 🏦 Module 06: Differentiated Banks (Payments Banks & SFBs)

### 1. Differentiated Banks (Niche Banks) Kya Hote Hain?
Traditional Universal Banks (jaise SBI, PNB, HDFC) sabhi tarah ke customers ko saari banking services dete hain.
Lekin **Differentiated Banks (Niche Banks)** specific target audience (jaise chote kisan, migrant workers, micro businesses) ke liye specific products provide karne ke purpose se banaye gaye hain.
* **Kiski Committee ki recommendation par bane?** **Dr. Nachiket Mor Committee (Committee on Comprehensive Financial Services for Small Businesses and Low Income Households - 2014)**.

---

### 2. Payments Banks vs Small Finance Banks: Full Detailed Comparison

| Feature | Payments Banks (PBs) | Small Finance Banks (SFBs) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Objective** | Small savings accounts & low-cost remittance (paisa transfer) services | Unserved aur underserved segments ko loans aur credit provide karna |
| **Minimum Paid-up Capital** | **₹100 Crore** | **₹200 Crore** (earlier ₹100 Cr; ₹100 Cr for UCBs converting to SFB) |
| **Promoter's Minimum Initial Stake** | Minimum **40% for the first 5 years** | Minimum **40% for the first 5 years** (gradually phased down to 26% in 12 yrs) |
| **Can they accept Deposits?** | **YES**, but ONLY **Demand Deposits** (Savings & Current A/c). **NO Fixed/Recurring Deposits (Term Deposits)!** | **YES, ALL Deposits** (Savings, Current, Fixed, and Recurring Deposits accepted) |
| **Maximum Deposit Limit** | **₹2 Lakh per individual customer** (earlier ₹1 Lakh, raised to ₹2 Lakh in April 2021) | **NO Maximum Limit** (Universal bank jaisa koi bhi amount jama kar sakte hain) |
| **Can they lend / give loans?** | **STRICTLY NO! Loans are completely banned.** | **YES! Lending is their core business.** |
| **Can they issue Credit Cards?** | **NO! (Cannot issue credit cards)** | **YES! They can issue both Debit and Credit Cards.** |
| **Can they issue Debit / ATM Cards?**| **YES! ATM/Debit cards can be issued.** | **YES! ATM/Debit cards can be issued.** |
| **Priority Sector Lending (PSL)** | **Not Applicable** (Kyunki loan dete hi nahi) | **75% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC)** mandatory! |
| **Loan Ticket Size Condition** | NA | Minimum **50% of loan portfolio must be loans up to ₹25 Lakh**! |
| **Investment Requirements** | Min **75% of demand deposits in Government Securities (SLR)**; Max 25% in current/term deposits with other SCBs | Normal CRR (Section 42) and SLR (Section 24) applicable |
| **Minimum Capital Adequacy (CRAR)**| **15%** of RWA | **15%** of RWA (Tier 1 min 7.5%) |
| **Foreign Shareholding (FDI)** | Up to **74%** (as per private sector bank FDI norms) | Up to **74%** (as per private sector bank FDI norms) |
| **Can accept NRI Deposits?** | **NO! NRI deposits strictly prohibited.** | YES (Subject to FEMA / RBI guidelines) |

## 🚜 Module 07: Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) & Local Area Banks (LABs)

### 1. Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) Ka Itihas & Genesis
* **Background:** 1972 ke Banking Commission ne rural credit ke liye special institution banane ka suggestion diya.
* **Recommendation:** **M. Narasimham Working Group (1975)** ki recommendation par banaya gaya.
* **Presidential Ordinance:** 26 September 1975 ko ordinance pass hua, aur **2 October 1975 (Gandhi Jayanti)** ke din desh ke pehle 5 RRBs set up kiye gaye!
* **Act:** **Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976**.

#### 🥇 Pehla RRB Kaun Sa Tha?
* **Prathama Bank:** Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh me establish hua.
* **Sponsor Bank:** **Syndicate Bank** (now merged with Canara Bank).
* **Authorised Capital:** Shuru me ₹5 Crore thi.
* **US Samay Ke Key Dignitaries [PDF Page 35]:**
  * RBI Governor: **K.R. Puri** (August 1975 – May 1977).
  * Prime Minister: **Indira Gandhi**.
  * Finance Minister: **Chidambaram Subramaniam**.

---

### 2. RRBs Ka Ownership & Capital Structure (Exam Must-Know 🌟)
RRB ki share capital me 3 partners ka fixed hissa hota hai:
* **Central Government:** **50%**
* **Sponsor Bank (Commercial Bank):** **35%**
* **State Government:** **15%**

$$\text{Ratio: } 50 : 35 : 15$$

* **RRBs Amendment Act, 2015:** Is amendment ne RRBs ko Central Government aur Sponsor Bank ke 51% combined holding ke baad bachi hui capital market se raise karne ki permission di.

---

### 3. Operational Norms & Current Status of RRBs — *[PDF Pages 36–38]*
* **Number of RRBs:** 1980s-90s me peak par **196 RRBs** the. Phased amalgamation ke baad:
  * As on 31 March 2021: **43 RRBs** operating across 26 states and 3 UTs (Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Puducherry).
  * **States with NO RRBs:** **Sikkim** aur **Goa** me koi bhi RRB nahi hai!
* **PSL Target for RRBs:** **75% of ANBC / CEOBE** (mandatory).
* **Supervision:** RRBs ko supervise aur inspect karne ka kaam **NABARD** karta hai (under Section 35(6) of Banking Regulation Act, 1949).
* **Dr. K.C. Chakrabarty Committee (2010):** RRBs ki financial condition review ki aur 82 me se 40 RRBs ke recapitalization ki recommendation di.
* **Table V: Borrowings of RRBs [PDF Page 38]:**
  * RRBs ki total borrowings me se **85% - 91% hissa akele NABARD** se refinance ke roop me aata hai, jabki sponsor banks se approx 9% - 14% funding aati hai.

### 4. Local Area Banks (LABs) — *[PDF Pages 39–40]*
* **Introduction:** **August 1996** me announce kiya gaya pursuant to Budget speech of Finance Minister **Dr. Manmohan Singh / P. Chidambaram**.
* **Objective:** Private sector participation ke zariye rural aur semi-urban areas me local savings mobilize karna aur viable credit gaps ko bridge karna.
* **Geographical Jurisdiction:** Local Area Banks ka operational area **Maximum 3 Contiguous (aapas me jude hue) Districts** tak hi seemit hota hai!
* **Capital & Prudential Norms:**
  * Minimum Paid-up Capital: **₹5 Crore** (Promoters contribution min ₹2 Crore).
  * Minimum Capital Adequacy (CRAR): **15% on an ongoing basis** (PDF Page 40 clarifies earlier 9%, upgraded to 15%).
  * Governed by: Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (licensed under Section 22).
* **Ramachandran Committee (2002) [PDF Page 40]:**
  * RBI review group (chaired by G. Ramachandran) ne LABs ki working review ki aur recommend kiya ki existing viable LABs ko continue karne diya jaye par governance norms strict kiye jayein.
* **Active LABs in India:** Coastal Local Area Bank Ltd (Vijayawada, AP), Krishna Bhima Samruddhi LAB Ltd (Mahbubnagar, Telangana), Subhadra LAB Ltd (Kolhapur, license later cancelled).

## 🛡️ Module 09: Government Social Security & Financial Inclusion Schemes

### 1. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) — *[PDF Page 50]*
* **Launched:** **28 August 2014** by PM Narendra Modi (Slogan: *"Mera Khata, Bhagya Vidhata"*).
* **Guinness World Record:** Sabse kam samay me sabse zyada bank accounts kholne ka record banaya (1.8 crore accounts opened in one week).

#### 🔑 Key Features of PMJDY:
* **Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA):** Zero balance facility (koi minimum balance charge nahi).
* **RuPay Debit Card:** Free card provided with built-in accidental insurance cover:
  * Accounts opened before 28.08.2018: **₹1 Lakh** accidental cover.
  * Accounts opened post 28.08.2018: **₹2 Lakh** accidental insurance cover (condition: card must be used at least once in 90 days).
* **Overdraft (OD) Facility:**
  * Maximum OD limit: **₹10,000** (pehle ₹5,000 thi).
  * Age limit for OD: **18 to 65 years** (pehle 18-60 years thi).
  * Up to **₹2,000 OD requires no conditions/questions asked**!
  * Only one person per household (preferably woman). Account must be satisfactorily operated for 6 months.

---

### 2. Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) — *[PDF Page 51]*
* **Launched:** **9 May 2015** (Kolkata).
* **Type:** Term Life Insurance (kisi bhi kaaran se death hone par).
* **Eligibility Age:** **18 to 50 Years** (Risk cover continues up to age 55, provided annual renewal premium is paid).
* **Life Cover Amount:** **₹2 Lakh** (nominee ko milta hai).
* **Annual Premium:** **₹436 per year** (pehle ₹330 tha, revised w.e.f. June 1, 2022). Auto-debited from bank account in May.
* **Lien Period:** Shuru ke **30 Days** me accidental death ke alawa normal death cover nahi hoti.

---

### 3. Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) — *[PDF Page 51]*
* **Launched:** **9 May 2015**.
* **Type:** Accidental Death and Disability Insurance.
* **Eligibility Age:** **18 to 70 Years** (having a savings bank a/c).
* **Insurance Cover:**
  * Accidental Death: **₹2 Lakh**.
  * Total & Irrecoverable loss of both eyes or both hands/feet: **₹2 Lakh**.
  * Partial disability (loss of one eye/hand/foot): **₹1 Lakh**.
* **Annual Premium:** **₹20 per year** (pehle ₹12 tha, revised w.e.f. June 1, 2022). Auto-debited in single instalment.

### 4. Atal Pension Yojana (APY) — *[PDF Page 51–52]*
* **Launched:** **9 May 2015**. Administered by **PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority)**.
* **Target:** Unorganised sector ke workers.
* **Eligibility Age:** **18 to 40 Years** (Kyunki minimum contribution period **20 Years** hona zaroori hai).
* **Pension Amount:** Guaranteed monthly pension of **₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000 per month** starting at age **60 Years** (contribution amount depends on joining age).
* **After Death Benefits:**
  * Subscriber ki death par spouse ko same pension milti hai.
  * Dono ki death par accumulated pension wealth nominee ko return kar di jaati hai.
* **Taxpayer Restriction (w.e.f. 1 Oct 2022):** Jo vyakti Income Tax payer hai, woh APY account nahi khol sakta.

### 5. Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) — *[PDF Page 53]*
* **Launched:** **8 April 2015**.
* **Full Form of MUDRA:** **Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency Ltd.**
* **Structure:** MUDRA Bank ko **SIDBI ki wholly owned subsidiary** ke roop me setup kiya gaya.
* **Purpose:** Non-Corporate, Non-Farm Small and Micro Enterprises ko ₹10 Lakh tak ka loan provide karna.
* **Key Features:** No processing fee, No collateral security required. Borrowers get a **MUDRA Card** (RuPay debit card with cash credit limit).

#### 🏷️ 3 Categories of MUDRA Loans:
1. **Shishu:** Loans sanctioned **up to ₹50,000** (Seed capital for new micro startups).
2. **Kishore:** Loans sanctioned from **₹50,001 up to ₹5 Lakh** (For established business looking for expansion).
3. **Tarun:** Loans sanctioned from **₹5,00,001 up to ₹10 Lakh** (For growth/scale-up).
*(Budget 2024 announcement proposed increasing Tarun limit up to ₹20 Lakh for entrepreneurs who have successfully repaid Tarun loans).*

## 💳 Module 10: Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPI) Guidelines

### 1. PPI Kya Hote Hain? — *[PDF Page 55]*
* **Legal Authority:** RBI issues master directions under **Section 18 read with Section 10(2) of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (PSS Act)**.
* **Definition:** PPIs aise payment instruments hote hain jinme pehle se paisa store (preloaded) hota hai, jiske badle goods & services khareedi ja sakti hain, financial transactions kiye ja sakte hain ya remittances bheje ja sakte hain.
* **Forms:** Smart cards, magnetic stripe cards, internet accounts, internet wallets, mobile wallets, paper vouchers.
* **Issuers:** 
  * **Banks:** RBI approval ke baad issue kar sakte hain.
  * **Non-Banks:** Companies Act ke under registered companies jo RBI se specific authorization leti hain.

---

### 2. PPIs Ke 3 Types (Closed, Semi-Closed, Open) — *[PDF Pages 56–57]*

1. **Closed System PPIs:**
   * Sirf usi entity ke outlets/website par use ho sakte hain jisne ise issue kiya hai.
   * Doosre kisi merchant par use nahi ho sakte; Cash withdrawal strictly prohibited hai.
   * Isko issue karne ke liye **RBI ki authorization ki zaroorat NAHI hoti** (kyunki yeh payment system nahi hai).
   * *Example:* Amazon Pay gift card (only on Amazon), Shoppers Stop gift voucher, metro rail transit cards for travel only.
2. **Semi-Closed System PPIs — [PDF Page 57]:**
   * Multiple clearly identified merchants ke paas accept hote hain jinka issuer ke sath contract/tie-up hota hai.
   * Cash withdrawal permitted nahi hota (except specific BC points under limits).
   * RBI authorization mandatory hai.
   * *Example:* PayU wallet, Mobikwik wallet (merchants tied up with them).
3. **Open System PPIs:**
   * Sirf aur sirf **Banking Institutions** dwara issue kiye ja sakte hain!
   * Kisi bhi merchant par swipe ho sakte hain (via Visa, MasterCard, RuPay network) aur **ATMs/PoS machines se Cash Withdrawal permitted** hota hai.
   * *Example:* Bank prepaid gift cards, travel cards issued by SBI, HDFC, ICICI.

---

### 3. Small PPIs vs Full-KYC PPIs (Limits & Norms) — *[PDF Page 56]*

#### 🔸 Small PPIs (Minimum Detail PPIs):
* Issued by banks and non-banks after obtaining minimum details of customer (mobile OTP + officially valid document number declaration).
* Only loaded from a bank account/debit card (no cash loading allowed for fresh PPIs).
* Maximum balance at any point: **₹10,000**.
* Total amount loaded in a financial year cannot exceed **₹1,20,000**.
* Must be converted into **Full-KYC PPI within 24 Months** from date of issue, otherwise further reload is stopped.

#### 🔹 Full-KYC PPIs:
* Full Video KYC / In-Person KYC verification complete hota hai.
* Maximum outstanding balance limit: **₹2,00,000** (raised from ₹1 Lakh).
* Funds transfer permitted (interoperable via UPI).

#### 🏧 Cash Withdrawal Limits on PPIs [PDF Page 56]:
* **Bank-issued PPIs:** Cash withdrawal permitted at ATMs, PoS devices, BCs.
  * PoS devices cash withdrawal: Limit of **₹2,000 per transaction** within an overall monthly limit of **₹10,000** across all locations.
* **Non-bank issued PPIs (Full-KYC):** Cash withdrawal permitted up to **₹2,000 per transaction** with an overall monthly cap of **₹10,000 per PPI** across all channels.
* **Important Rule:** PPI balances par **KOI INTEREST PAYABLE NAHI HOTA**!

## ⚖️ Module 11: Banking Ombudsman & Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS, 2021)

### 1. Scheme Ka Itihas & Evolution — *[PDF Page 69]*
* **1995:** Sabse pehla **Banking Ombudsman Scheme (BOS)** shuru hua under Section 35A of Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
* Five revisions hue (2002, 2006, etc.).
* Bad me non-bank entities ke liye alag ombudsman schemes aayi:
  * **OSNBFC (2018):** Ombudsman Scheme for Non-Banking Financial Companies.
  * **OSDT (2019):** Ombudsman Scheme for Digital Transactions.
* **Problem:** Har entity ke liye alag ombudsman hone se customer confuse ho jate the.

---

### 2. Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021 (RB-IOS)
* **Launched On:** **12 November 2021** by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
* **Philosophy:** **"One Nation, One Ombudsman"** (Ek Desh, Ek Lokpal).
* Teeno purani schemes (BOS 1995, OSNBFC 2018, OSDT 2019) ko merge karke single integrated framework bana diya gaya.
* **Covered Entities:** Sabhi Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, Scheduled Primary Urban Co-operative Banks, Non-scheduled UCBs with deposits $\ge$ ₹50 Crore, all NBFCs with asset size $\ge$ ₹100 Crore and public customer interface, and all System Participants (payment system operators).

---

### 3. Centralised Complaint Filing & CRPC
* Customer kisi bhi bank/NBFC ke khilaf online single window portal par complaint file kar sakta hai: `cms.rbi.org.in`.
* **CRPC (Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre):** Setup at **RBI, Chandigarh** for physical and email-based receipt of complaints from across the country.

---

### 4. Complaint Kab Aur Kaise Karein? — *[PDF Page 70]*
1. Customer ko pehle apne bank/financial institution ko written complaint karni hoti hai.
2. Ombudsman ke paas tabhi ja sakte hain jab:
   * Bank ne **30 Dino ke andar** koi reply na diya ho, YA
   * Bank ne complaint **reject** kar di ho, YA
   * Customer bank ke decision se **satisfy na ho**.
3. **Time Limit for Filing:** Bank se reply aane ke **1 Year ke andar**; agar bank ne koi reply nahi diya toh original complaint date se **1 Year aur 30 Days ke andar** Ombudsman ke paas jana hota hai!

---

### 5. Compensation & Award Limits (High-Yield Exam Data 🌟) — *[PDF Page 70]*
* **Dispute Amount Limit:** Dispute amount par **KOI UPPER LIMIT NAHI HAI** (Ombudsman can hear cases of any amount!).
* **Compensation for Direct / Consequential Loss:** Ombudsman maximum **₹20 Lakh tak ka compensation** award kar sakta hai!
* **Additional Compensation for Mental Harassment & Loss of Time:** In addition to the ₹20 Lakh, Ombudsman mental agony, harassment aur time loss ke liye up to **₹1 Lakh** ka extra compensation de sakta hai!

#### 🏛️ Appellate Authority:
* Ombudsman ke award/decision ke khilaf appeal kahan hoti hai?
* **Executive Director in-charge of Consumer Education and Protection Department (CEPD), RBI**.
* Appeal order milne ke **30 Dino ke andar** file karni hoti hai.

## 🪙 Module 12: Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) & Gold Monetization Scheme (GMS)

### 1. Gold Monetization Scheme (GMS, 2015) — *[PDF Page 84]*
* **Launched:** November 2015 (replaced Gold Deposit Scheme 1999).
* **Objective:** Bharat ke gharelu parivaron aur mandiro/trusts me rakha lagbhag **23,000 se 24,000 Tonnes idle gold** mobilize karke productive economy me lana, taaki sona import karne ka kharcha aur Current Account Deficit (CAD) kam ho sake.
* **Eligible Banks:** All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs).

#### 🏷️ 2 Main Deposit Types under GMS:
1. **Short Term Bank Deposit (STBD):**
   * Tenure: **1 to 3 Years**.
   * Treated as bank's liability; bank pays interest directly (around 0.50% - 0.75%).
2. **Medium and Long Term Government Deposit (MLTGD):**
   * **Medium Term:** **5 to 7 Years** (Interest approx 2.25% p.a.).
   * **Long Term:** **12 to 15 Years** (Interest approx 2.50% p.a.).
   * Deposit central government ke behalf par hold hota hai.
* **Minimum Quantity:** Minimum deposit **10 Grams of raw gold** (bars, coins, jewellery without stones). Earlier it was 30 grams.
* **No Maximum Limit** on gold deposit. Tax exemption on capital gains and interest income!

---

### 2. Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Scheme — *[PDF Page 85]*
* **Issuing Authority:** Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the **Government of India**.
* **Denomination:** Multiples of **1 Gram of Gold**.
* **Tenor of Bond:** **8 Years**.
* **Exit / Premature Redemption:** Allowed from the **5th Year** onwards (exercisable on coupon payment dates in 5th, 6th, and 7th year).
* **Minimum Investment:** **1 Gram**.
* **Maximum Subscription Limits per Fiscal Year (April–March):**
  * **Individuals:** **4 KG**
  * **Hindu Undivided Families (HUF):** **4 KG**
  * **Trusts and similar entities:** **20 KG**
* **Fixed Rate of Interest:** **2.50% per annum** (paid semi-annually on nominal issue price).
* **Payment Mode / Online Discount:** SGB online khareedne aur digital payment karne par **₹50 per gram ka discount** milta hai.
* **Taxation Benefits:**
  * Annual 2.5% interest income is taxable as per income tax slab.
  * **Redemption after 8 years is 100% EXEMPT from Capital Gains Tax**!
* **Collateral:** SGB bonds ko bank loans ke liye collateral/pledge ke roop me use kiya ja sakta hai.

---

### 🧠 Quick Self-Check Quiz (Module 12)
1. **What is the tenor of Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB)?**
   * *Answer:* 8 Years (exit option available in 5th, 6th, and 7th year).
2. **What is the fixed annual interest rate paid to investors in SGB?**
   * *Answer:* 2.50% per annum (paid semi-annually).
3. **What is the maximum permissible investment limit in SGB for an individual in one financial year?**
   * *Answer:* 4 KG (for Trusts it is 20 KG).
4. **Is capital gains tax applicable on redemption of SGB at maturity?**
   * *Answer:* No, capital gains tax is completely exempt on redemption for individuals.
