## 🏛️ Module 14: All India Financial Institutions (AIFIs) Overview & NABARD

### 1. AIFIs Kya Hote Hain? — *[PDF Pages 89, 100–101]*
Bharat ki economy ke development aur crucial sectors (krishi, chhote udyog, videshi vyapar, housing aur infrastructure) ko long-term finance provide karne ke liye apex specialized development finance institutions banaye gaye hain jinhe **All India Financial Institutions (AIFIs)** kehte hain.
* **RBI dwara Regulated aur Supervised 5 Apex AIFIs:**
  1. **NABARD** (Agriculture & Rural Development)
  2. **EXIM Bank** (Foreign Trade & Exports)
  3. **SIDBI** (MSME Sector)
  4. **NHB** (Housing Finance)
  5. **NaBFID** (Infrastructure Financing - newest entrant setup in 2021)

---

### 2. National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) — *[PDF Pages 89–100]*

#### 📜 Genesis & Establishment:
* **Recommendation:** **B. Sivaraman Committee** (Committee to Review Arrangements For Institutional Credit for Agriculture and Rural Development - **CRAFICARD**).
* **Act:** **National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development Act, 1981**.
* **Established On:** **12 July 1982** (RBI ke Agricultural Credit Department - ACD aur Rural Planning and Credit Cell - RPCC aur ARDC ko merge karke banaya gaya).
* **Headquarters:** **Mumbai, Maharashtra**.
* **Ownership:** Pehle RBI aur Government of India ka joint share tha. **Ab 100% Government of India owned hai** (RBI sold its stake to GoI).
* **Initial Paid-up Capital:** ₹100 Crore (Authorized capital expanded to ₹30,000 Crore under NABARD Amendment Act 2018).

#### 🔍 Supervisory Powers (Section 35(6) of Banking Regulation Act, 1949) — [PDF Page 91]:
Section 35(6) of BR Act, 1949 NABARD ko power deta hai ki woh followings ka statutory inspection aur supervision kare:
1. **State Co-operative Banks (StCBs)**
2. **District Central Co-operative Banks (DCCBs)**
3. **Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)**

---

### 3. NABARD Ke Key Funds & Development Programmes — *[PDF Pages 92–97]*

#### 🏗️ 1. Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF) — [PDF Page 92]:
* **Created In:** **1995-96** by Government of India with an initial corpus of **₹2,000 Crore**.
* **Source of Funding:** Domestic commercial banks jo apna **Priority Sector Lending (PSL) target achieve karne me fail** hote hain, unka shortfall amount RIDF me deposit karwaya jata hai!
* **Allocation Growth:** RIDF XXVII (2021-22) me annual allocation **₹40,000 Crore** kiya gaya, jisse cumulative allocation ₹4 Lakh Crore se upar pahunch gaya.
* **Eligible Institutions:** State Governments, Union Territories, State Owned Corporations / Undertakings, Panchayati Raj Institutions, SHGs, NGOs.
* **Mode of Finance [PDF Page 94]:** Reimbursement basis par release hota hai, except initial mobilisation advance of **30% for North Eastern & Hilly States** and **20% for other States**.

#### 💧 2. Watershed Development Fund (WDF) — [PDF Page 97]:
* 1999-2000 me setup hua. Initial contribution: **₹100 Crore each** by Ministry of Agriculture (GoI) and NABARD (Total ₹200 Cr corpus).
* Purpose: Dryland areas me soil moisture conservation aur watershed development.

#### 🌳 3. Tribal Development Programme (TDF - "Wadi" Model) — [PDF Page 97]:
* Adivasi (tribal) families ko sustainable livelihood dene ke liye **"Wadi" (Orchard farming)** model par based fruit orchards aur agri-horticulture development program.

#### 👥 4. Producers Organisation Development Fund (PODF) — [PDF Page 96]:
* NABARD ne 2011 me create kiya taaki **Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)** aur primary producers ko end-to-end financial, technical aur marketing support diya ja sake.

---

### 4. NABARD Ki Subsidiaries & Associated Entities — *[PDF Pages 98–99]*
1. **NABFINS (NABARD Financial Services Ltd):** Microfinance aur financial inclusion provide karne wali subsidiary.
2. **NABKISAN Finance Ltd:** Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) aur agriculture startups ko credit facilitate karti hai.
3. **NABCONS (NABARD Consultancy Services):** Agriculture aur rural development domain me consultancy aur advisory provide karti hai.
4. **NABVENTURES Ltd:** Agri-tech, food processing aur rural fintech startups me invest karne wala Venture Capital Fund.
5. **NABFOUNDATION:** Section 8 Not-for-Profit company promoted and fully owned by NABARD to execute pan-India development projects.

## 🚢 Module 15: Export-Import Bank of India (EXIM Bank)

### 1. Genesis, Logo & Ownership — *[PDF Pages 101–104]*
* **Act:** **The Export-Import Bank of India Act, 1981**.
* **Commenced Operations:** **1 January 1982**.
* **Headquarters:** **Mumbai, Maharashtra**.
* **Ownership:** **100% Government of India owned** (fully owned by GoI).
* **Logo Significance [PDF Page 102]:** Logo two-way flow represent karta hai: **Import arrow patla (thinner) hai aur Export arrow mota (thicker) hai**, jo yeh darshata hai ki Bharat ko exports promote karke trade surplus banana hai!

---

### 2. Leadership & Management Structure — *[PDF Pages 103–104]*
* **First Managing Director:** **R. C. Shah**.
* **First Woman Managing Director:** **Tarjani Vakil** (1993 me appoint hui - Bharat me kisi bhi All India Financial Institution ki pehli mahila MD!).
* **Tenure:** Chairman and Managing Director (CMD) / Whole-time Director ka tenure Central Government dwara specify kiya jata hai, **not exceeding 5 Years**, aur re-appointment ke liye eligible hote hain.

---

### 3. Key Functions, Services & Programmes — *[PDF Pages 105–106]*
* **Lines of Credit (LOCs):** Foreign governments aur financial institutions ko concessional credit lines di jaati hain taaki woh Bharat se engineering goods, machinery aur services import karein (supported by GoI).
* **Buyer's Credit & Supplier's Credit:** Indian exporters aur foreign buyers ko trade credit guarantee aur financing.
* **Ubharte Sitaare Programme (USP) [PDF Page 105]:** EXIM Bank aur SIDBI dwara jointly launch kiya gaya program jo aisi Indian companies ko identify karta hai jinme global market me export champions banne ki high potential hai.
* **Clusters of Excellence Programme [PDF Page 106]:** EXIM Bank ne **1994-95** me shuru kiya taaki Indian export manufacturing clusters ko **ISO 9000 quality certification** lene me technical aur financial help di ja sake.

---

### 🧠 Quick Self-Check Quiz (Module 15)

   * *Answer:* Tarjani Vakil.

   * *Answer:* EXIM Bank and SIDBI.

## 🏭 Module 16: Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) & PSLCs

### 1. Genesis & Institutional Profile — *[PDF Page 107]*
* **Act:** **Small Industries Development Bank of India Act, 1989**.
* **Setup On:** **2 April 1990** (originally IDBI ki wholly owned subsidiary tha; 2000 me delink hokar independent AIFI bana).
* **Headquarters:** **Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh** (Exam favourite: Almost all AIFIs have HQ in Mumbai or Delhi, but SIDBI HQ is in **Lucknow**!).
* **Principal Mandate:** Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) sector ke promotion, financing aur development ke liye principal financial institution ke roop me kaam karna.

---

### 2. Specialized Direct Loan Schemes of SIDBI — *[PDF Pages 108, 111–112]*
* **STAR Scheme (SIDBI Term-Loan Assistance for Rooftop Solar PV Plants) [PDF Page 112]:**
  * MSMEs ko apne power bills kam karne ke liye 25 KW se 500 KW capacity ke rooftop solar plants lagane ke liye cheap rate par term loan provide karta hai.
* **SPEED & SPEED PLUS:** Loan for purchase of machinery and expansion with fast-track processing within 48-72 hours.
* **SMILE (SIDBI Make in India Soft Loan Fund for MSMEs):** Soft loans/quasi-equity for emerging enterprises in the manufacturing and services sectors.
* **ARISE & STHAPAN:** Greenfield and brownfield expansion assistance for MSMEs.
* **SHWAS & AROG [PDF Page 111]:** COVID-19 pandemic ke dauran medical aur healthcare MSMEs ko oxygen cylinders, masks, PPE kits aur medical equipment manufacturing ke liye emergency collateral-free funding.
* **Swavalamban Challenge Fund [PDF Page 111]:** Non-profits, educational institutions, aur social startups ko financial assistance provide karta hai jo sustainable livelihood, financial inclusion aur entrepreneurship promote karte hain.

---

### 3. Market Infrastructure: RXIL & MSME Pulse — *[PDF Pages 110, 113]*
* **MSME Pulse Report [PDF Page 110]:** SIDBI aur credit rating agency **TransUnion CIBIL** dwara jointly publish ki jaane wali quarterly report jo MSME sector ki credit health, NPA trends aur loan growth ka analysis deti hai.
* **RXIL (Receivables Exchange of India Ltd) [PDF Page 113]:**
  * Incorporated on **25 February 2016** as a joint venture between **SIDBI and National Stock Exchange of India (NSE)**.
  * Bharat ka pehla RBI-authorized **TReDS (Trade Receivables Discounting System)** platform hai jo MSME suppliers ke pending bills aur invoices ko competitive online auction ke zariye instantly discount/cash me convert karta hai.

---

### 4. Priority Sector Lending Certificates (PSLCs) — *[PDF Page 114]*
* **Concept:** Agar kisi bank ka kisi priority sector category me loan target short pad raha hai, toh use doosre bank se physically loans transfer karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Woh RBI ke **e-Kuber Portal** par PSLC khareed sakta hai.
* **Traded Categories (4 Types):**
  1. **PSLC Agriculture**
  2. **PSLC SF/MF (Small and Marginal Farmers)**
  3. **PSLC Micro Enterprises**
  4. **PSLC General**
* **Validity:** All PSLCs expire on **31st March** of the financial year (irrespective of the date of purchase).

---

## 🏗️ Module 17: National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development (NaBFID)

### 1. Genesis & Need for DFI — *[PDF Pages 115–116]*
* **Background:** Commercial banks short-term deposits (1-3 saal) lekar 20-30 saal ke highways, ports aur power plants ko loan dete the, jisse unki balance sheet me **Asset-Liability Mismatch (ALM)** ho jata tha aur huge NPAs create hote the.
* Is samasya ko door karne ke liye ek dedicated statutory **Development Financial Institution (DFI)** setup kiya gaya.
* **Act:** **National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development Act, 2021** (passed in Parliament in March 2021).
* **Headquarters:** **Mumbai, Maharashtra**.

---

### 2. Capital Structure & Tax Benefits — *[PDF Page 116]*
* **Authorised Share Capital:** **₹1,00,000 Crore (₹1 Lakh Crore)**!
* **Initial Paid-up Equity:** **₹20,000 Crore** provided by the Central Government.
* **Government Grant:** Central Government ne initial operations support karne ke liye **₹5,000 Crore ka non-repayable grant** provide kiya.
* **Minimum Government Shareholding:** Central Government ka stake kisi bhi samay **26% se kam nahi hoga**.
* **Tax Concession:** NaBFID ko shuru ke **10 Years ke liye Income Tax se 100% Tax Holiday** diya gaya hai!

---

### 3. Key Leadership — *[PDF Page 116]*
* **First Chairman:** **K. V. Kamath** (veteran banker, former chief of ICICI Bank and New Development Bank).
* **First Managing Director (MD):** **Rajkiran Rai G** (former MD & CEO of Union Bank of India).

---

   * *Answer:* Chairman: K. V. Kamath; MD: Rajkiran Rai G.

## 🏠 Module 18: National Housing Bank (NHB) & Housing Regulations

### 1. Genesis & Institutional History — *[PDF Pages 117–120]*
* **Recommendation:** **7th Five Year Plan (1985–90) Sub-Group on Housing Finance** chaired by **Dr. C. Rangarajan** ne housing sector ke liye apex institution banane ki recommendation di.
* **Act:** **National Housing Bank Act, 1987**.
* **Established On:** **9 July 1988**.
* **Headquarters:** **New Delhi**.
* **Ownership Transition:** 
  * Originally NHB ki 100% paid-up capital **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** ke paas thi.
  * **Narasimham Committee II** ki recommendation par (ki regulator ko regulated entity own nahi karni chahiye), Government of India ne **2019 me RBI ka 100% stake acquire kar liya**. Ab NHB 100% Government of India owned hai!

---

### 2. Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio Ceilings for Housing Loans — *[PDF Page 118]*
RBI aur NHB dwara housing finance companies (HFCs) aur banks ke liye individual housing loans par mandatory LTV limits:

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                      HOUSING LOAN LTV RATIO CEILINGS                     |
+------------------------------------+-------------------+-----------------+
| Individual Housing Loan Amount     | Maximum LTV Ratio | Minimum Margin  |
+------------------------------------+-------------------+-----------------+
| Up to ₹30 Lakh                     | **90%**           | 10%             |
| Above ₹30 Lakh and up to ₹75 Lakh  | **80%**           | 20%             |
| Above ₹75 Lakh                     | **75%**           | 25%             |
+------------------------------------+-------------------+-----------------+
```
> **Explanation:** Agar ghar ki value ₹30 Lakh hai, toh bank maximum ₹27 Lakh (90%) loan de sakta hai; bacha hua ₹3 Lakh (10%) borrower ko apne paas se (margin money) lagana padega.

---

### 3. Credit Risk Guarantee Fund Scheme for Low Income Housing (CRGFS) — *[PDF Page 119]*
* Formulated by: Ministry of Housing and Urban Poverty Alleviation (MoHUPA) with Department of Financial Services (DFS).
* Managed by: **National Housing Bank**.
* Purpose: EWS (Economically Weaker Sections) aur LIG (Low Income Groups) ko bina kisi third-party guarantee ya collateral ke housing loans up to ₹5 Lakh (later revised under PMAY) ke liye lending institutions ko credit risk guarantee cover dena.

---

### 4. NHB SUVRIDDHI Term Deposit Scheme & RESIDEX — *[PDF Page 120]*
* **NHB SUVRIDDHI Scheme:**
  * Individuals aur HUFs ke liye Section 80C tax saving deposit scheme.
  * Minimum deposit: **₹10,000** (and multiples of ₹10,000). Maximum deposit: **₹1,00,000 / ₹1,50,000**.
  * Fixed Maturity: **60 Months (5 Years)**.
* **NHB RESIDEX:** Bharat ka pehla official Housing Price Index jo Bharat ke alag-alag shahro me residential property prices ke movement ko track karta hai (launched July 2007).

---

   * *Answer:* 90%.

## ⚖️ Module 19: Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC, 2016) & IBBI

### 1. IBC Ka Background & Need — *[PDF Pages 121–124]*
* Bharat me pehle companies default hone par case Sick Industrial Companies Act (SICA), BIFR, Recovery of Debts Due to Banks (RDDBFI Act), aur SARFAESI me uljhe rehte the, jisse recovery me average 4.3 saal lagte the.
* **Enactment:** **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016** passed in May 2016 to provide a single consolidated time-bound framework.
* **Basic Distinction:**
  * **Insolvency:** State of inability to pay debts as they become due. (Financial status).
  * **Bankruptcy:** Legal declaration of insolvency by a competent court.
  * **Liquidation:** The process of winding up a company and selling its assets to distribute proceeds among creditors.

---

### 2. IBC Ke 4 Institutional Pillars (Foundation)

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     4 INSTITUTIONAL PILLARS OF IBC                       |
+---------------------+---------------------+------------------------------+
| 1. IBBI             | 2. IPs & IPAs       | 3. Information Utilities     |
| Apex Regulator      | Insolvency          | Electronic repository of     |
| (Setup 1 Oct 2016)  | Professionals who   | financial debts & records    |
| First Chief:        | manage CIRP         | (NeSL is India's 1st IU)     |
| M. S. Sahoo         |                     |                              |
+---------------------+---------------------+------------------------------+
| 4. ADJUDICATING AUTHORITIES:                                             |
| - NCLT (National Company Law Tribunal): For Corporates & LLPs            |
| - DRT (Debts Recovery Tribunal): For Individuals & Partnership Firms     |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **IBBI (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India):** Apex statutory regulator, established on **1 October 2016**. First Chairperson: **M. S. Sahoo**.
2. **Insolvency Professionals (IPs) & Agencies (IPAs):** Licensed professionals jo debtor company ka control aur operations apne haath me lete hain CIRP ke dauran.
3. **Information Utilities (IUs):** Central electronic database jahan debts aur defaults ka record authenticate hota hai. **NeSL (National E-Governance Services Ltd)** is India's first registered IU.
4. **Adjudicating Authorities:**
   * **NCLT (National Company Law Tribunal):** Companies aur Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) ke cases sunta hai. Appeal goes to **NCLAT** $\rightarrow$ **Supreme Court**.
   * **DRT (Debts Recovery Tribunal):** Individuals aur Unlimited Partnership firms ke cases sunta hai. Appeal goes to **DRAT** $\rightarrow$ **Supreme Court**.

---

### 3. Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP) Timelines & Thresholds — *[PDF Page 123]*

#### 💰 Minimum Default Threshold:
* Originally CIRP trigger karne ke liye minimum default amount **₹1 Lakh** tha.
* COVID-19 ke dauran MSMEs ko safeguard karne ke liye Central Government ne Section 4 ke tehat is threshold ko badha kar **₹1 Crore** kar diya!

#### ⏱️ CIRP Mandatory Time Limits:
* **Normal Resolution Timeline:** **180 Days** from date of admission.
* **Extension:** NCLT one-time extension de sakta hai up to **90 Days**.
* **Overall Statutory Outer Limit:** **330 Days** (including all legal challenges and litigation). Agar 330 din me koi resolution plan approve nahi hota, toh company automatically **Liquidation (Nilaami)** me chali jaati hai.

#### 🗳️ Committee of Creditors (CoC):
* Financial creditors ki committee banti hai.
* Kisi bhi resolution plan ko approve karne ke liye CoC me kam se kam **66% Voting Share** ki approval zaroori hoti hai (pehle 75% thi).

---

### 4. Section 53: Waterfall Mechanism (Liquidation Hierarchy)
Jab company liquidate hoti hai, toh assets bechkar aane wala paisa strictly is sequence me banta jata hai:
1. **Insolvency Resolution Process Costs & Liquidator Costs** (Sabse pehle).
2. **Workmen's Dues (for 24 months preceding liquidation)** & Secured Creditors who relinquished security (Pari-passu).
3. **Wages of other employees (for 12 months)**.
4. **Unsecured Financial Creditors**.
5. **Government Dues (State & Central Govt for 2 years)** & Remaining balance to secured creditors.
6. **Any remaining debt and dues** (Operational creditors).
7. **Preference Shareholders**.
8. **Equity Shareholders** (Sabse aakhir me, agar kuch bache toh).

## 🏛️ Module 20: Financial Services Institutions Bureau (FSIB) & Banks Board Bureau (BBB)

### 1. Banks Board Bureau (BBB) Ka Safar — *[PDF Pages 125, 127]*
* **Genesis:** **P.J. Nayak Committee (2014)** ne Public Sector Banks ke governance ko improve karne aur political interference khatam karne ke liye ek independent bureau banane ki recommendation di.
* **Established:** February 2016 me Government of India ke **"Indradhanush Mission"** ke under BBB setup hua.
* **First Chairman:** **Vinod Rai** (Former Comptroller and Auditor General of India).
* **Legal Challenge & Crisis [PDF Page 127]:**
  * Delhi High Court ne 2021 me ek ruling me kaha ki BBB ke mandate me Public Sector General Insurance companies ke General Managers aur Directors select karne ka legal provision nahi tha, jisse kai appointments stall ho gaye.
  * Is legal defect ko theek karne ke liye Government ne BBB ko officially dissolve karke naya broader bureau banaya.

---

### 2. Financial Services Institutions Bureau (FSIB) — *[PDF Pages 125–126]*
* **Effective Date:** **1 July 2022** ko Central Government ne gazette notification ke zariye FSIB constitute kiya.
* **First Chairman of FSIB:** **Bhanu Pratap Sharma** (former Secretary of DoPT).

#### 🎯 Mandate & Functions of FSIB:
1. Public Sector Banks (PSBs), Public Sector Insurance Companies (PSIs), aur Financial Institutions (FIs jaise NABARD, SIDBI, EXIM, NHB, NaBFID) ke **Whole-Time Directors (WTDs) aur Non-Executive Chairpersons** ke selection aur appointment ki recommendation dena.
2. Board members ke performance appraisal aur talent development ka framework tayyar karna.
3. PSBs aur FIs ko growth, capital raising aur business strategy formulate karne me advise karna.

---

### 3. FSIB Board Composition — *[PDF Pages 125–126]*
The Bureau comprises:
* **Chairperson:** Central Government dwara nominated eminent person.
* **Ex-Officio Members:**
  1. Secretary, Department of Financial Services (DFS)
  2. Secretary, Department of Public Enterprises (DoPE)
  3. Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
  4. Chairperson, Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)
* **Domain Experts (Part-Time Members):**
  * **3 Experts relating to PSBs and FIs** (Kam se kam 2 ex-bankers).
  * **3 Experts relating to Public Sector Insurance Companies (PSIs)** (Kam se kam 2 senior insurance professionals).
* Bureau ka permanent secretariat New Delhi me operate karta hai.

---
