## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Indigenous Banking System:** Prachin kal me Sharoffs, Seths, Sahukars, Mahajans, Chettiars dwara chalaya jata tha. Hundis (Darshani aur Muddati) ka upyog hota tha. Public deposits na milne ke karan yeh modern banking me develop nahi ho paya.
* **Banking Vikas ke 3 Mukhya Charn (Three Phases of Evolution):**
  1. **Phase 1 (Early Phase: 1786 se 1947 tak):** General Bank of India (1786), Bank of Hindustan (1770), Presidency Banks (1806, 1840, 1843), Imperial Bank (1921), Swadeshi Movement banks (BOI, CBI, Canara, PNB).
  2. **Phase 2 (Post-Independence to Pre-Reforms: 1947 se 1991 tak):** Banking Regulation Act 1949, SBI formation 1955, SBI Associates 1959, 14 Banks Nationalisation 1969, RRB setup 1975, 6 Banks Nationalisation 1980, NABARD/EXIM setup 1982.
  3. **Phase 3 (Post-1991 Reforms Era):** LPG reforms, **Narasimham Committee I (1991)** & **II (1998)**, naye private banks ko entry (1993 me HDFC, ICICI, Axis), prudential norms (CAR, NPA classification), digital banking aur mega mergers.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Sahukar vs Modern Bank: Kyun zaroorat padi Vikas ki?
* **Puraana Zamana (Indigenous Bankers):** Gaon me Lala ji ya Sahukar hote the. Agar kisan ko beej khareedne ke liye paise chahiye, toh Lala ji 36% se 50% compound interest par loan dete the aur kisan ki zameen girwi rakh lete the. Agar fasal kharab hui, toh zameen hadap li jati thi!
* **Naya Daur (Modern Banking Era):** Sarkar ne dekha ki jab tak aam kisan aur garib aadmi ko sasta sansthadhyak (institutional) loan nahi milega, desh aage nahi badhega. Isliye SBI banaya gaya, 1969 me banks ka rashtriyakaran kiya gaya aur gaon-gaon me branches kholi gayi.

### 📌 2. 1991 ke Reforms aur Narasimham Committee
* **Scenario:** 1990 tak sarkari banks me bohot zyada bheed, purani file system, kharab customer service aur bohot saare doobe huye loans (NPAs) the.
* **Narasimham Committee ka Chamatkar:** M. Narasimham (purve RBI Governor) ki committee ne kaha:
  * Banks ko azaadi do interest rate tay karne ki.
  * CRR aur SLR ko ghatao taaki banks ke paas business karne ke liye paisa bache.
  * Private sector ko banks kholne do taaki competition badhe (jisse ICICI, HDFC aaye aur ATM, Internet Banking, Debit Cards shuru huye!).

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Phase 1: Pre-Independence Period (1786 to 1947)
1. **Pehle Banks:**
   * **The General Bank of India (1786):** Shuru hua par 1791 me fail ho gaya.
   * **Bank of Hindustan (1770):** Alexander and Company dwara Calcutta me banaya gaya, 1829-32 me band hua.
2. **First Limited Liability Indian Bank:**
   * **Oudh Commercial Bank (1881):** Faizabad me establish hua. Yeh **pehla bank tha limited liability ke sath jiska board of directors purely Indians the**. Yeh 1958 me fail hua.
3. **First Purely Indian Commercial Bank:**
   * **Central Bank of India (1911):** Sir Sorabji Pochkhanawala dwara sthapit. Isse Bharat ka pehla aam commercial bank mana jata hai jo puri tarah bhartiya management aur bhartiya capital se chala (*Pheroze Shah Mehta iske pehle Chairman the*).
4. **Punjab National Bank (PNB):** 1894 me register hua aur 1895 me Lahore me shuru hua.
5. **Swadeshi Movement (1906):** Bank of India (1906), Canara Bank (1906), Indian Bank (1907), Bank of Baroda (1908) sthapit huye.

---

### B. Phase 2: Post-Independence to 1991
| Year | Major Milestone Event | Exam Importance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1949** | **Banking Regulation Act, 1949** | Commercial banks ko RBI ke regulation aur supervision ke andar laya gaya. Pehle iska naam *Banking Companies Act, 1949* tha (1966 me badla). |
| **1955** | **State Bank of India (SBI) Formation** | Imperial Bank of India ko Gorwala Committee ki sifarish par 1 July 1955 ko nationalise karke SBI banaya gaya. |
| **1959** | **SBI (Subsidiary Banks) Act, 1959** | 8 princely state banks ko SBI ke associate banks banaya gaya (State Bank of Bikaner, Jaipur, Hyderabad, Indore, Mysore, Patiala, Saurashtra, Travancore). |
| **1969** | **14 Major Banks ka Nationalisation** | 19 July 1969 ko Indira Gandhi sarkar ne ₹50 Crore se zyada deposit wale 14 banks ko nationalise kiya. |
| **1975** | **Regional Rural Banks (RRB)** | M. Narasimham Committee ki sifarish par 2 October 1975 ko pehla RRB **Prathama Bank** (Moradabad, UP) sthapit hua (Sponsored by Syndicate Bank). |
| **1980** | **6 More Banks ka Nationalisation** | 15 April 1980 ko ₹200 Crore se zyada deposit wale 6 banks ko nationalise kiya gaya. |
| **1982** | **NABARD & EXIM Bank Setup** | Agriculture ke liye NABARD (12 July 1982) aur foreign trade ke liye EXIM Bank (1 Jan 1982) banaye gaye. |

### C. Phase 3: Post-1991 Banking Sector Reforms (Narasimham Committees)

#### 1. Narasimham Committee - I (1991): Committee on Financial System (CFS)
* **Recommendations:**
  * **Reduction in CRR and SLR:** SLR ko 38.5% se ghatakar 25% aur CRR ko ghatana.
  * **Prudential Norms:** International standard ke aadhar par **Income Recognition, Asset Classification (IRAC) aur Provisioning norms** lagu karna.
  * **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** Banks ke liye minimum **8% Capital to Risk-weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR)** anivarya karna.
  * **Entry of Private Sector Banks:** Naye tech-savvy private banks ko license dena (1993 guidelines). Iske tahet 10 private banks aaye: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, UTI Bank (baad me Axis Bank), IndusInd Bank, Global Trust Bank, waghera.
  * **Debt Recovery Tribunals (DRT):** Bad loans ki tez recovery ke liye special tribunals banana.

#### 2. Narasimham Committee - II (1998): Committee on Banking Sector Reforms
* **Recommendations:**
  * Strong banks ka merger karna aur weak banks ko **Narrow Banking** me convert karna.
  * Capital Adequacy Ratio ko 8% se badhakar **9%** karna.
  * **Asset Reconstruction Companies (ARCs)** sthapit karna bad loans ko buy-out karne ke liye.
  * PSB boards ko administrative autonomy dena.
  * Computerisation aur electronic fund transfer ko mandatory karna.

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

### 💡 Mnemonic: Three Historic Indian Banks
* **Oudh Commercial Bank (1881):** Pehla limited liability bank with Indian board (*Oudh = Oldest Board*).
* **PNB (1894/95):** Pehla purely Indian capital bank (*PNB = Paisa National Bharat ka*).
* **Central Bank of India (1911):** Pehla purely Indian commercial bank (*Central = Completely Indian Management*).

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Which was the first bank established with limited liability managed by an Indian board?
(a) Oudh Commercial Bank  
(b) Punjab National Bank  
(c) Central Bank of India  
(d) Allahabad Bank  
(e) Imperial Bank of India  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Oudh Commercial Bank (1881)** pehla bank tha jisme limited liability thi aur jiska board purely Indians dwara manage hota tha.

#### Q2. Central Bank of India was established in which year?
(a) 1906  
(b) 1911  
(c) 1913  
(d) 1921  
(e) 1895  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Central Bank of India ki sthapna **1911** me Sir Sorabji Pochkhanawala dwara ki gayi thi. Yeh pehla pure Indian commercial bank tha.

#### Q3. In which year was the Banking Regulation Act passed?
(a) 1934  
(b) 1947  
(c) 1949  
(d) 1955  
(e) 1969  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **1949** me Banking Companies Act pass hua tha, jiska naam 1966 me badalkar *Banking Regulation Act, 1949* kiya gaya.

#### Q4. Who headed the Committee on the Financial System (1991) that recommended banking reforms?
(a) C. Rangarajan  
(b) M. Narasimham  
(c) Bimal Jalan  
(d) Y.V. Reddy  
(e) Raghuram Rajan  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **M. Narasimham** committee ne 1991 aur 1998 me banking sector ke historic reforms recommend kiye the. Unhe 'Father of Indian Banking Reforms' bhi kaha jata hai.

#### Q5. The first Regional Rural Bank (RRB) in India was established on:
(a) 15 August 1975  
(b) 2 October 1975  
(c) 1 January 1976  
(d) 19 July 1969  
(e) 1 April 1974  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Bharat ka pehla RRB **Prathama Bank (Moradabad, UP)** **2 October 1975** (Gandhi Jayanti) ko sthapit hua tha.

#### Q6. Narasimham Committee-I recommended fixing the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) at:
(a) 4%  
(b) 6%  
(c) 8%  
(d) 10%  
(e) 12%  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Narasimham Committee-I (1991) ne international Basel standards ke tehat CAR ko **8%** fix karne ki sifarish ki thi (jo baad me 1998 me 9% ki gayi).

#### Q7. The Swadeshi Movement of 1906 gave birth to which of the following banks?
(a) Canara Bank  
(b) Bank of India  
(c) Bank of Baroda  
(d) Indian Bank  
(e) All of the above  
**Answer: (e)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Swadeshi Movement (1906) ke dauran Bank of India (1906), Canara Bank (1906), Indian Bank (1907), aur Bank of Baroda (1908) sabhi sthapit huye the.

#### Q8. What was the original name of Axis Bank when it was licensed as a new private bank in 1993?
(a) Global Trust Bank  
(b) UTI Bank  
(c) Centurion Bank  
(d) Times Bank  
(e) Bank of Punjab  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Axis Bank ka purana naam **UTI Bank** tha, jisse 2007 me rename karke Axis Bank kiya gaya.
