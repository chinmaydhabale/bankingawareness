## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **NPA (Non-Performing Asset):** Aisa koi bhi loan ya advance jisme principal (mool-dhan) ya interest (byaj) ka payment **lagatar 90 din tak** overdue rahe aur bank ko usse aamdani hona band ho jaye.
* **Agriculture Loans me NPA Rule:**
  * **Short duration crops** (Dhan, gehun): Payment **2 crop seasons (fasal chakra)** tak overdue rahe.
  * **Long duration crops** (Ganna, nariyal): Payment **1 crop season** tak overdue rahe.
* **Asset Classification Slabs:**
  1. **Standard Asset:** Performing loan (Normal).
  2. **Sub-Standard Asset:** Jo NPA banne ke baad **12 mahine ya usse kam** samay tak NPA rahe. (Provision: 15% secured, 25% unsecured).
  3. **Doubtful Asset:** Jo **12 mahine se zyada** samay tak sub-standard category me rahe (D1: up to 1 yr, D2: 1-3 yrs, D3: > 3 yrs).
  4. **Loss Asset:** Bank, internal/external auditor ya RBI inspection dwara uncollectible ghoshit kiya gaya asset (Provision: **100%**).
* **Early Warning SMA (Special Mention Accounts):**
  * **SMA-0:** Overdue **1 se 30 din**.
  * **SMA-1:** Overdue **31 se 60 din**.
  * **SMA-2:** Overdue **61 se 90 din** (90 din paar karte hi ban jata hai NPA!).
* **SARFAESI Act, 2002:** *Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act*.
  * Banks ko adhikar deta hai ki wo court gaye bina girwi rakhi property ko kabze me lekar neelam (auction) kar sakein!
  * **Section 13(2):** Borrower ko **60 din ka demand notice** dena.
  * **Section 13(4):** Agar borrower 60 din me pay na kare, toh bank asset ka possession le sakta hai.
  * **Section 31 Exemptions (Jahan SARFAESI lagu NAHI hota):**
    1. Agricultural Land (Kheti ki zameen).
    2. Unsecured loans.
    3. Outstanding balance $< ₹1 Lakh$.
    4. Outstanding debt $< 20\%$ of principal and interest.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Loan se NPA banne ki Journey (90 Din ka Countdown)
* **Day 1 to 30 (SMA-0):** Ramesh ki Home loan ki EMI 5 tareekh ko katni thi, par khate me balance nahi tha. Bank usse SMS bhejta hai: *"Aapki EMI overdue hai, kripya jama karein"*.
* **Day 31 to 60 (SMA-1):** Ek mahina beet gaya, doosri EMI bhi toot gayi. Bank manager Ramesh ko phone lagata hai.
* **Day 61 to 90 (SMA-2 - Red Alert!):** Teesra mahina shuru ho gaya. Bank ke system me red flag aa gaya: *"Agar agle 30 din me paisa nahi aaya, toh yeh loan NPA ghoshit ho jayega!"*
* **Day 91 (NPA - Asset Doob Gaya!):** 90 din poore ho gaye aur ₹1 bhi nahi aaya. Ab yeh loan bank ke balance sheet me **NPA** ban gaya. Bank ko apne profit me se paisa nikal kar iske liye **Provisioning** karni padegi!

### 📌 2. SARFAESI ka Jaadu: Court ke Chakkaron se Chhutkara
* **SARFAESI se pehle (Before 2002):** Agar defaulter paisa na de, toh bank civil court me case daalta tha. Tareekh par tareekh padti thi, 15-20 saal tak case chalta tha aur bank ka paisa fasa rehta tha.
* **SARFAESI ke baad (Power of Bank):** Ab bank court nahi jata. Bank borrower ko seedhe Section 13(2) ka **60-day notice** chipkata hai: *"60 din me pura paisa chukao, warna hum tumhari dukan/factory par tala laga denge!"* Agar 60 din me paisa nahi aaya, toh bank Police aur Magistrate ki madad se property par kabza karke paper me auction ka ad nikal deta hai!

### A. Asset Classification & Provisioning Norms Matrix
| Category | Definition | Provisioning Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Asset** | Regular paying loan | • Agri/SME: **0.25%**<br>• Commercial Real Estate: **1.00%**<br>• Housing: **0.40%** |
| **Sub-standard Asset** | NPA for $\le$ 12 months | • **15%** (Secured portion)<br>• **25%** (Unsecured portion) |
| **Doubtful 1 (D1)** | Up to 1 year in doubtful | • **25%** (Secured) + **100%** (Unsecured) |
| **Doubtful 2 (D2)** | 1 year to 3 years in doubtful | • **40%** (Secured) + **100%** (Unsecured) |
| **Doubtful 3 (D3)** | More than 3 years in doubtful | • **100%** (Secured) + **100%** (Unsecured) |
| **Loss Asset** | Uncollectible, write-off eligible | • **100%** Provisioning |

### B. Legal Recovery Channels for Bad Loans
1. **Lok Adalat:**
   * Compromise settlement for small loans up to **₹20 Lakh**. Decisions are binding, no appeal in court.
2. **Debt Recovery Tribunals (DRT):**
   * Set up under RDDBFI Act, 1993.
   * Recovery cases of **₹20 Lakh and above** (earlier ₹10 Lakh).
   * Appeal goes to **DRAT (Debt Recovery Appellate Tribunal)**.
3. **SARFAESI Act, 2002:**
   * Secured creditors can enforce security interest without court intervention.
4. **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016:**
   * Time-bound corporate resolution (180 to 330 days) through **NCLT (National Company Law Tribunal)**.
5. **National Asset Reconstruction Company Ltd (NARCL - Bad Bank):**
   * Stressed assets ko buy-out karne ke liye setup. Government 15% cash aur 85% Security Receipts (SRs) guarantee karti hai.

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. A loan is classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) if the interest or installment remains overdue for more than:
(a) 30 days  
(b) 60 days  
(c) 90 days  
(d) 180 days  
(e) 365 days  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Commercial loans me **90 din se zyada** overdue rehne par asset NPA ban jata hai.

#### Q2. For agricultural loans for short-duration crops, a loan is treated as NPA if the installment remains overdue for:
(a) 1 crop season  
(b) 2 crop seasons  
(c) 3 crop seasons  
(d) 90 days  
(e) 180 days  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Short-duration crops ke mamle me **2 crop seasons (fasal chakra)** tak overdue hone par loan NPA banta hai.

#### Q3. An asset which remains an NPA for a period less than or equal to 12 months is classified as:
(a) Standard Asset  
(b) Sub-standard Asset  
(c) Doubtful Asset  
(d) Loss Asset  
(e) SMA-2  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Jo asset 12 mahine ya usse kam samay tak NPA rahe, use **Sub-standard Asset** kehte hain.

#### Q4. An account where the principal or interest is overdue between 61 to 90 days is classified as:
(a) SMA-0  
(b) SMA-1  
(c) SMA-2  
(d) Sub-standard  
(e) Doubtful  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **61 se 90 din** tak overdue account ko **SMA-2** categorize kiya jata hai.

#### Q5. Under Section 13(2) of the SARFAESI Act, how many days notice must be given to the borrower to discharge liabilities?
(a) 15 days  
(b) 30 days  
(c) 45 days  
(d) 60 days  
(e) 90 days  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** SARFAESI Act ke Section 13(2) ke tahet borrower ko **60 din ka demand notice** diya jata hai.

#### Q6. The provisions of SARFAESI Act are NOT applicable to which of the following?
(a) Commercial properties  
(b) Residential apartments  
(c) Agricultural land  
(d) Industrial machinery  
(e) Warehouse stock  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** SARFAESI Act ke Section 31 ke mutabiq **Agricultural Land (kheti ki zameen)** par yeh kanoon lagu nahi hota.

#### Q7. Cases involving debt recovery of what minimum amount can be filed before the Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT)?
(a) ₹5 Lakh  
(b) ₹10 Lakh  
(c) ₹20 Lakh  
(d) ₹50 Lakh  
(e) ₹1 Crore  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** DRT me case file karne ke liye minimum claim amount **₹20 Lakh** hona chahiye.
