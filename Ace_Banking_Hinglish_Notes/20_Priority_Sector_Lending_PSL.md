## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Priority Sector Lending (PSL):** Desh ke un arthik kshetron (economic sectors) ko banks dwara loan ka ek nishchit hissa dena anivarya karna jo desh ke vikas aur rozgar ke liye zaroori hain par jinko commercial banks aam taur par loan dene me katrate hain.
* **Origin:** 1968 me National Credit Council ki baithak me pehli baar yeh mudda utha. Formalized hua **1972 me Dr. K.S. Krishnaswamy Committee** ki report par.
* **8 Eligible PSL Categories:**
  1. Agriculture
  2. Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)
  3. Export Credit
  4. Education
  5. Housing
  6. Social Infrastructure
  7. Renewable Energy
  8. Others.
* **Overall Targets (% of ANBC / CEOBE):**
  * Domestic Commercial Banks & Foreign Banks with $\ge 20$ branches: **40%**.
  * Regional Rural Banks (RRBs): **75%**.
  * Small Finance Banks (SFBs): **75%**.
  * Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks (UCBs): **75%** (March 2026 tak phased target).
  * Foreign Banks with $< 20$ branches: **40%** (jisme se max 32% export credit ho sakta hai).
* **Agriculture Sub-target:** **18% of ANBC** (jisme se **10% Small and Marginal Farmers - SMF** ko jana compulsory hai).
* **Micro Enterprises Sub-target:** **7.5% of ANBC**.
* **Weaker Sections Sub-target:** **12% of ANBC** (RRBs ke liye 15%).
* **Non-Achievement Penalty:** Agar koi bank PSL target miss karta hai, toh us shortfall ke barabar paisa **RIDF (NABARD)**, SIDBI, NHB, ya MUDRA me kam byaj par deposit karna padta hai.
* **PSLCs (Priority Sector Lending Certificates):** 4 types (PSLC-Agriculture, PSLC-SFM, PSLC-Micro, PSLC-General) jo RBI ke **e-Kuber** portal par trade hote hain.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. PSL Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Banker ka Commercial Mindset vs Desh ka Vikas)
* **Banker ka Soch:** Agar bank ke paas ₹100 Crore hain, toh bank chahega ki wo pura ₹100 Crore kisi badi company (jaise Tata, Reliance ya Adani) ko de de. Ek hi file me kaam khatam, security pakki, aur time bachega. Bank gaon ke 10,000 chhote kisano ko ₹50,000-₹50,000 ka loan dene me jhanjhat samjhega.
* **RBI ka Danda (PSL Mandate):** RBI ne kanoon banaya: *"Sun SBI aur HDFC! Tum apne total loan ka 40% hissa Priority Sector (Kheti, Chhoti dukan, Education, Saste makan) ko hi doge! Agar nahi diya, toh tumhe penalty bharni padegi!"*
* **Real-life Benefit:** Isi mandate ki wajah se garib bache ko IIT/IIM padhne ke liye Education Loan milta hai, kisan ko fasal ke liye KCC milta hai aur gaon ke karobari ko MSME loan milta hai.

### 📌 2. PSLCs: Carbon Credit jaisa Banking Market
* **Scenario:** Socho Bandhan Bank ya kisi SFB ne gaon me kheti ke bohot loan baante aur uska Agriculture PSL **25%** ho gaya (Target 18% tha, yani 7% surplus!).
* Doosri taraf, ek videshi bank (jaise Citi ya HSBC) ki gaon me branch hi nahi hai aur uska Agriculture PSL sirf **11%** reh gaya (7% ka shortfall!).
* Ab HSBC ko NABARD me penalty bharni padegi. Isse bachne ke liye HSBC RBI ke **e-Kuber** platform par Bandhan Bank se **PSLC-Agriculture Certificate** khareed leta hai.
* Bandhan Bank ko premium (fee) kamane ko milta hai, aur HSBC ka compliance poora ho jata hai (bina koi loan transfer kiye!).

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. PSL Targets & Sub-Targets Master Matrix
| Category | Domestic Commercial Banks & Foreign Banks ($\ge 20$ branches) | Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) | Small Finance Banks (SFBs) | Foreign Banks ($< 20$ branches) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total PSL Target** | **40% of ANBC** | **75% of ANBC** | **75% of ANBC** | **40% of ANBC** |
| **Total Agriculture** | **18% of ANBC** | **18% of ANBC** | **18% of ANBC** | Not applicable |
| **Small & Marginal Farmers (SMF)** | **10% of ANBC** | **10% of ANBC** | **10% of ANBC** | Not applicable |
| **Micro Enterprises** | **7.5% of ANBC** | **7.5% of ANBC** | **7.5% of ANBC** | Not applicable |
| **Weaker Sections** | **12% of ANBC** | **15% of ANBC** | **12% of ANBC** | Not applicable |

---

### B. Definitions of Farmer Categories
* **Marginal Farmer (सीमांत किसान):** Landholding up to **1 hectare** (2.5 acres).
* **Small Farmer (लघु किसान):** Landholding **1 hectare se zyada aur 2 hectare tak** (2.5 se 5 acres).
* **Landless Agricultural Labourers, Tenant Farmers, Oral Lessees, aur Sharecroppers** ko bhi SMF sub-target ke andar shamil kiya jata hai.

---

### C. PSL Loan Limits for Key Non-Farm Sectors
1. **Education Loans:**
   * Bharat me ya videsh me padhai ke liye **₹20 Lakh tak ke loans** PSL me count hote hain.
2. **Housing Loans:**
   * **Metro Centres** (population $\ge 10$ lakh): Makan ki cost up to ₹45 Lakh, jisme loan amount **₹35 Lakh tak** PSL mana jata hai.
   * **Non-Metro Centres:** Makan ki cost up to ₹30 Lakh, jisme loan amount **₹25 Lakh tak** PSL mana jata hai.
   * Makan ki marammat (repair) ke liye: Metro me up to ₹10 Lakh aur non-metro me up to ₹6 Lakh.
3. **Renewable Energy:**
   * Solar generators, biomass plants, wind mills waghera ke liye commercial borrowers ko **₹30 Crore tak** aur individual households ko **₹10 Lakh tak** ka loan PSL me aata hai.
4. **Social Infrastructure:**
   * Schools, drinking water facilities, sanitation facilities, aur Ayushman Bharat health infrastructure ke liye Tier-2 se Tier-6 centres me **₹5 Crore per borrower** tak ka loan PSL me eligible hota hai.

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

### 💡 Mnemonic: PSL Target Slabs
* **40%** = Domestic Commercial Banks
* **75%** = RRBs & SFBs (Gaon aur Chhote banks ko 75% dena padta hai)
* **18 - 10 - 7.5 - 12**
  * **18%** Agri
  * **10%** Small/Marginal Farmer
  * **7.5%** Micro Enterprise
  * **12%** Weaker Section

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. What is the overall Priority Sector Lending (PSL) target for domestic commercial banks in India?
(a) 30% of ANBC  
(b) 40% of ANBC  
(c) 50% of ANBC  
(d) 75% of ANBC  
(e) 60% of ANBC  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Domestic commercial banks ke liye overall PSL target **40% of ANBC** (ya CEOBE, whichever is higher) hota hai.

#### Q2. What is the total PSL target for Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) and Small Finance Banks (SFBs)?
(a) 40%  
(b) 50%  
(c) 60%  
(d) 75%  
(e) 90%  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** RRBs aur SFBs dono ke liye overall PSL target **75% of ANBC** anivarya hai.

#### Q3. What is the sub-target for Agriculture lending under PSL for domestic commercial banks?
(a) 10% of ANBC  
(b) 12% of ANBC  
(c) 15% of ANBC  
(d) 18% of ANBC  
(e) 20% of ANBC  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Commercial banks ke liye agriculture ka sub-target **18% of ANBC** fix hai.

#### Q4. Out of the 18% agriculture target, what percentage is earmarked for Small and Marginal Farmers (SMF)?
(a) 8%  
(b) 9%  
(c) 10%  
(d) 12%  
(e) 5%  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Small and Marginal Farmers (SMF) ke liye sub-target **10% of ANBC** hai.

#### Q5. What is the maximum landholding for a farmer to be classified as a 'Marginal Farmer'?
(a) Up to 1 hectare  
(b) Up to 2 hectares  
(c) Up to 5 hectares  
(d) Up to 0.5 hectare  
(e) Up to 2.5 hectares  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **1 hectare tak** zameen wale kisan ko **Marginal Farmer (सीमांत किसान)** kaha jata hai. (1 se 2 hectare wale ko Small Farmer).

#### Q6. What is the PSL sub-target for Micro Enterprises?
(a) 5% of ANBC  
(b) 7.5% of ANBC  
(c) 10% of ANBC  
(d) 12% of ANBC  
(e) 15% of ANBC  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Micro enterprises ke liye mandatory sub-target **7.5% of ANBC** hai.

#### Q7. Education loans up to what limit are eligible under Priority Sector Lending?
(a) ₹5 Lakh  
(b) ₹10 Lakh  
(c) ₹15 Lakh  
(d) ₹20 Lakh  
(e) ₹30 Lakh  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Education loans **₹20 Lakh tak** PSL category me eligible hote hain.

#### Q8. When banks face a shortfall in achieving their PSL targets, where is the shortfall amount deposited?
(a) Consolidated Fund of India  
(b) Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF) with NABARD  
(c) Prime Minister Relief Fund  
(d) Deposit Insurance Fund  
(e) Contingency Fund of RBI  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** PSL shortfall ka paisa **RIDF (NABARD)**, SIDBI ya doosre specified funds me deposit karwaya jata hai.
