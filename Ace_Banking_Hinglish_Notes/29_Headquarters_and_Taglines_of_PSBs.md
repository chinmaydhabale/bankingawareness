* **Bank of India** $ightarrow$ Mumbai
* Central **Bank of India** $ightarrow$ Mumbai

👉 *Sabhi "Bank of India" Mumbai me baithe hain!*

👉 *Dono "Indian" Chennai me hain!*

### 💡 Rule 3: 'Punjab' Wale $ightarrow$ NEW DELHI!

👉 *Dono "Punjab" New Delhi me hain!*

* Bank of **Baroda** $ightarrow$ **Vadodara** (Baroda)

* **Canara** Bank $ightarrow$ **Bengaluru** (Karnataka)

(c) Chennai
