## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **DICGC Full Form:** **Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation**.
* **Sthapna:** **15 July 1978** ko *Deposit Insurance Corporation (1962)* aur *Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (1971)* ko merge karke hui.
* **Act:** **DICGC Act, 1961**.
* **Ownership:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI) ki **100% wholly owned subsidiary**. HQ: **Mumbai**.
* **Insurance Cover Limit:** **₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakh)** per depositor, per bank (Principal + Interest milaakar). [Budget 2020 se pehle yeh limit ₹1 Lakh thi].
* **Covered Banks:** Sabhi Commercial Banks (PSBs, Private, Foreign, RRBs, LABs, SFBs, Payments Banks) aur sabhi eligible Urban & State/District Co-operative Banks.
* **Covered Deposits:** Savings Account, Current Account, Fixed Deposit (FD), Recurring Deposit (RD).
* **Excluded Deposits (Jinhe cover NAHI milta):**
  1. Foreign Government ke deposits.
  2. Central aur State Governments ke deposits.
  3. Inter-bank deposits (Ek bank ka doosre bank me jama paisa).
  4. State Land Development Banks ke deposits.
  5. Bharat ke bahar se received koi bhi deposit amount.
* **Premium Payment:** Premium **sirf aur sirf Bank bharta hai** (12 paise per ₹100 assessable deposit). Depositor ke account se ₹1 bhi nahi kata jata.
* **90-Day Payout Rule (2021 Amendment):** Agar RBI kisi bank par moratorium (rok) lagata hai, toh DICGC ko **90 din ke andar** depositors ko ₹5 Lakh tak ka paisa lautaana padta hai!

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. "₹5 Lakh Per Depositor, Per Bank" ka Asli Ganit (Math)
* **Example 1 (Ek hi Bank ki Do Branches):**  
  * Ramesh ke paas SBI ki Main Branch me ₹4 Lakh ki FD hai aur SBI ki Station Branch me ₹3 Lakh ki Savings deposit hai. Total SBI me Ramesh ka paisa = **₹7 Lakh**.  
  * Agar bhagwan na kare SBI doob jaye, toh DICGC Ramesh ko kitna dega?  
  * 👉 **Sirf ₹5 Lakh!** Kyunki dono accounts ek hi bank (SBI) me ek hi capacity (individual name) me hain. Baki ₹2 Lakh doob jayenge.
* **Example 2 (Do Alag-Alag Banks):**  
  * Ramesh ne samajhdari dikhayi: Usne ₹4 Lakh SBI me jama kiye aur ₹4 Lakh PNB me jama kiye.  
  * Agar dono bank doob jayein, toh DICGC SBI se bhi ₹4 Lakh dilaega aur PNB se bhi ₹4 Lakh dilaega! Poora **₹8 Lakh surakshit** hai!
* **Example 3 (Single vs Joint Account):**  
  * Ramesh ka apna individual khata hai (₹5 Lakh).  
  * Ramesh aur uski patni Sunita ka Joint Account hai (₹5 Lakh).  
  * Yeh dono alag capacity hain, isliye dono accounts par alag-alag ₹5 Lakh ka insurance cover milega!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Insured vs Non-Insured Deposits Master Matrix
| Deposit Category | DICGC Insurance Milta Hai? | Rationale / Rule |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Savings Bank Account** | ✅ **HAAN** | Covered up to ₹5 Lakh |
| **Fixed Deposit (FD)** | ✅ **HAAN** | Principal + Interest dono judkar max ₹5L tak |
| **Recurring Deposit (RD)** | ✅ **HAAN** | Covered up to ₹5 Lakh |
| **Current Account** | ✅ **HAAN** | Business current account bhi ₹5L tak covered hai |
| **NBFC Deposits (Bajaj Finance, etc.)** | ❌ **NAHI** | NBFCs DICGC ke adheen nahi aati |
| **Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)** | ❌ **NAHI** | PACS cooperative societies hain, banks nahi |
| **Central / State Govt Deposits** | ❌ **NAHI** | Government khud sovereign hai |
| **Inter-Bank Deposits** | ❌ **NAHI** | Banks ka aapas ka paisa covered nahi hota |
| **Foreign Government Deposits** | ❌ **NAHI** | Videshi sarkaron ka paisa excluded hai |

### B. DICGC Amendment Act, 2021 ke Mukhya Pravdhan
* Pehle jab PMC Bank ya Yes Bank par sankat aaya, toh depositors ko saalo intezar karna padta tha jab tak bank poori tarah liquidate na ho jaye ya resolution plan na bane.
* 2021 ke sanshodhan ke mutabiq:
  * Pehle **45 din** me stressed bank ko sabhi depositors ke records DICGC ko bhejne hote hain.
  * Agle **45 din** me DICGC unka verification karke depositors ke khate me seedhe ₹5 Lakh tak transfer kar deta hai.
  * Yani total **90 din (3 mahine)** ke andar customer ko uska beema ka paisa mil jata hai!

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

### 💡 Mnemonic: DICGC Golden Digits '1961 - 5 Lakh - 90 Din'
* **1961:** Act pass hua
* **5 Lakh:** Insurance ki limit
* **90 Din:** Moratorium ke baad payout deadline
* **12 Paise:** Bank dwara diya jane wala premium per ₹100.

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. DICGC was established under which Act?
(a) Banking Regulation Act, 1949  
(b) Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934  
(c) DICGC Act, 1961  
(d) Companies Act, 1956  
(e) Insurance Act, 1938  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** DICGC ki sthapna **DICGC Act, 1961** ke tahet hui thi.

#### Q2. What is the current maximum deposit insurance coverage provided by DICGC per depositor per bank?
(a) ₹1 Lakh  
(b) ₹2 Lakh  
(c) ₹5 Lakh  
(d) ₹10 Lakh  
(e) Unlimited  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Union Budget 2020 ke baad se DICGC insurance cover badhakar **₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakh)** kar diya gaya hai.

#### Q3. DICGC is a wholly owned subsidiary of:
(a) Government of India  
(b) Reserve Bank of India  
(c) State Bank of India  
(d) Life Insurance Corporation of India  
(e) IRDAI  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** DICGC **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** ki 100% wholly owned subsidiary hai.

#### Q4. Which of the following deposits is NOT insured by DICGC?
(a) Savings deposits of individuals  
(b) Fixed deposits of senior citizens  
(c) Recurring deposits of students  
(d) Inter-bank deposits  
(e) Current accounts of small traders  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Inter-bank deposits** (ek bank ka doosre bank me paisa) DICGC ke tahet insured nahi hote.

#### Q5. Who pays the insurance premium to DICGC for deposit insurance?
(a) Depositor  
(b) Concerned Bank  
(c) Central Government  
(d) State Government  
(e) Shared equally by Bank and Depositor  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Beema ka poora premium **concerned bank** khud bharta hai, depositor se koi paisa nahi liya jata.

#### Q6. Under the DICGC Amendment Act 2021, within how many days must depositors get access to their insured deposits up to ₹5 Lakh during a moratorium?
(a) 30 days  
(b) 60 days  
(c) 90 days  
(d) 120 days  
(e) 180 days  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** DICGC Amendment Act 2021 ke mutabiq moratorium lagne ke **90 din** ke andar depositors ko unka paisa milna anivarya hai.
