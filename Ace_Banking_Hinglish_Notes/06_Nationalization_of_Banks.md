## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Background:** 1949 se pehle sabhi commercial banks private industrialists ke hath me the. Wo aam janta ka paisa lekar apne hi business aur factories me lagate the, jabki Kheti (Agriculture), Chhote udyog (MSMEs) aur Gaon (Rural areas) ko bilkul loan nahi milta tha.
* **Pehla Rashtriyakaran:** **19 July 1969** ko Indira Gandhi sarkar ne **14 bade commercial banks** ka nationalisation kiya jinke deposits **₹50 Crore ya usse zyada** the.
* **Doosra Rashtriyakaran:** **15 April 1980** ko **6 anya commercial banks** ka nationalisation kiya gaya jinke deposits **₹200 Crore ya usse zyada** the.
* **Total Nationalised Banks:** $14 + 6 = 20$ banks. (1993 me *New Bank of India* ko losses ke karan *Punjab National Bank* me merge kar diya gaya, jisse count 19 ho gaya).
* **Objectives:** "Class Banking" ko "Mass Banking" me badalna, credit ka santulit vitaran karna, aur banking ko gaon-gaon tak pahunchana.

---

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 "Class Banking" se "Mass Banking" ka Safar
* **Class Banking (Ameeron ka Bank):** 1969 se pehle socho Birla, Tata ya Dalmia ka apna private bank tha. Bank ki branch sirf Mumbai ya Delhi ke posh ilaqe me hoti thi. Gaon ke kisan ya shahar ke rickshaw wale ko bank ke gate ke andar ghusne tak nahi diya jata tha. Sara credit sirf bade karobariyon ko milta tha.
* **Mass Banking (Sabka Bank):** 19 July 1969 ko sarkar ne ek jhatke me ordinance pass kiya: *"Ab yeh bank kisi seth ji ke nahi hain, yeh Bharat Sarkar ke hain!"*
* **Real-life Impact:** Nationalisation ke baad kanoon bana ki har bank ko apni branch gaon me kholni padegi aur kam se kam 40% loan kheti aur chhote vyapariyon ko dena padega (**Priority Sector Lending**). Isse kisan ko tractor ke liye loan milna shuru hua aur gaon me branch aane se logon ki bachat bank me aayi.

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Phase 1 of Nationalisation: 19 July 1969
* **Ordinance:** *Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Ordinance, 1969* (baad me Act bana).
* **Prime Minister:** Smt. Indira Gandhi.
* **Criteria:** Aise sabhi scheduled commercial banks jinka deposit base **₹50 Crore ya usse adhik** tha.
* **Total Banks:** **14 Banks**.
* **List of 14 Banks:**
  1. Central Bank of India
  2. Bank of India
  3. Punjab National Bank (PNB)
  4. Bank of Baroda (BOB)
  5. United Commercial Bank (Ab UCO Bank)
  6. Canara Bank
  7. United Bank of India
  8. Dena Bank
  9. Syndicate Bank
  10. Union Bank of India
  11. Allahabad Bank
  12. Indian Bank
  13. Bank of Maharashtra
  14. Indian Overseas Bank (IOB).

---

### B. Phase 2 of Nationalisation: 15 April 1980
* **Criteria:** Aise scheduled commercial banks jinka deposit base **₹200 Crore ya usse adhik** tha.
* **Total Banks:** **6 Banks**.
* **List of 6 Banks:**
  1. Andhra Bank
  2. Corporation Bank
  3. New Bank of India
  4. Oriental Bank of Commerce (OBC)
  5. Punjab & Sind Bank
  6. Vijaya Bank.

---

### C. Merger Milestone (1993)
* 1980 me nationalise huye banks me se **New Bank of India** heavy losses aur non-performing assets me chala gaya.
* September **1993** me Bharat Sarkar ne New Bank of India ko **Punjab National Bank (PNB)** ke sath merge kar diya.
* Yeh swatantra Bharat me do nationalised banks ka **pehla merger** tha! Iske baad nationalised banks ki sankhya 20 se घटकर 19 ho gayi.

---

### D. Objectives & Impacts (Uddeshya aur Parinam)
| Objectives (Maksad) | Impacts & Achievements (Uplabdhiyan) | Drawbacks & Challenges (Chunautiyan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Social Welfare:** Garibon aur kamzor vargo tak pahunchna. | **Branch Expansion:** Gaon aur semi-urban areas me hazaron nayi branches khuli. | **Overstaffing & Bureaucracy:** Sarkari babu culture aa gaya, customer service slow ho gayi. |
| **Agriculture & MSME Credit:** Kheti aur chhote udyogo ko sasta loan dena. | **Green Revolution Support:** Kisan credit aur tubewells ke liye loan mila, jisse Green Revolution safal hua. | **Political Interference:** 'Loan Melas' lagaye gaye bina credit appraisal ke loan baante gaye. |
| **Private Monopoly Khatam Karna:** Chuninda corporate houses ke control ko todna. | **Mobilisation of Savings:** Aam public ka paisa formal banking me aane laga. | **NPA Crisis:** Recovery par dhyan na hone se bad loans (NPAs) bohot badh gaye. |

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

### 💡 Mnemonic 1: Criteria Formula '69 me 50, 80 me 200'
* **1969:** **14** Banks $\rightarrow$ Deposit $\ge$ **₹50 Crore**
* **1980:** **6** Banks $\rightarrow$ Deposit $\ge$ **₹200 Crore**  
👉 *Trick:* '69 me 14 log 50 rupaye laye, 80 me 6 log 200 rupaye laye!'

### 💡 Mnemonic 2: 1993 First Merger
* **New Bank of India** + **PNB** = PNB (*Naya bank purane PNB me sama gaya*).

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. When was the first phase of bank nationalisation conducted in India?
(a) 15 August 1947  
(b) 1 January 1949  
(c) 19 July 1969  
(d) 15 April 1980  
(e) 2 October 1975  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Pehla phase **19 July 1969** ko hua tha jisme 14 banks nationalise kiye gaye the.

#### Q2. How many commercial banks were nationalised in the first phase on 19 July 1969?
(a) 6  
(b) 8  
(c) 14  
(d) 20  
(e) 12  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 19 July 1969 ko **14 commercial banks** ka nationalisation hua tha.

#### Q3. What was the minimum deposit criteria for banks nationalised in 1969?
(a) ₹25 Crore  
(b) ₹50 Crore  
(c) ₹100 Crore  
(d) ₹200 Crore  
(e) ₹500 Crore  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 1969 me nationalisation ke liye criteria tha ki bank ke paas **₹50 Crore ya usse zyada** ke deposits hone chahiye.

#### Q4. In the second phase on 15 April 1980, how many banks were nationalised?
(a) 4  
(b) 6  
(c) 8  
(d) 10  
(e) 14  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 15 April 1980 ko **6 commercial banks** ka nationalisation kiya gaya tha.

#### Q5. What was the deposit threshold for banks nationalised in 1980?
(a) ₹50 Crore  
(b) ₹100 Crore  
(c) ₹150 Crore  
(d) ₹200 Crore  
(e) ₹500 Crore  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 1980 ke phase me deposit criteria **₹200 Crore ya usse zyada** rakha gaya tha.

#### Q6. In 1993, which nationalised bank was merged into Punjab National Bank (PNB)?
(a) New Bank of India  
(b) Oriental Bank of Commerce  
(c) United Bank of India  
(d) Dena Bank  
(e) Vijaya Bank  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 1993 me financial losses ke chalte **New Bank of India** ko **Punjab National Bank (PNB)** me merge kiya gaya tha.

#### Q7. Which of the following banks was NOT among the 14 banks nationalised in 1969?
(a) Allahabad Bank  
(b) Bank of Baroda  
(c) Canara Bank  
(d) Punjab & Sind Bank  
(e) Central Bank of India  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Punjab & Sind Bank** 1969 me nahi, balki doosre phase me **1980** me nationalise hua tha.
