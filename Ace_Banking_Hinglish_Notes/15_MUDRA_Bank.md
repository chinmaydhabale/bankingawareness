## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Full Form of MUDRA:** **Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency Ltd.**
* **Launch Date:** **8 April 2015** ko Prime Minister Narendra Modi dwara launch kiya gaya.
* **Parent Institution:** Shuruat me yeh **SIDBI ki subsidiary** ke roop me set up kiya gaya.
* **Target Audience:** Non-Corporate, Non-Farm Small/Micro Enterprises (Chhote dukandar, fal-sabji wale, salon wale, dhabe wale, tailor waghera).
* **Funding the Unfunded:** MUDRA bank seedhe aam nagrik ko loan nahi deta; yeh commercial banks, RRBs, SFBs aur MFIs ko **Refinance** karta hai.
* **3 Loan Slabs (Loan Categories):**
  1. **Shishu (शिशु):** Loans up to **₹50,000**.
  2. **Kishor (किशोर):** Loans above **₹50,000 up to ₹5 Lakh**.
  3. **Tarun (तरुण):** Loans above **₹5 Lakh up to ₹10 Lakh** (Union Budget me ab naya slab Tarun Plus ₹20 Lakh tak kiya gaya hai).
* **Collateral-Free:** MUDRA ke tehat **koyi collateral (security ya property girwi)** nahi maangi jati. Credit guarantee CGFMU (Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units) dwara milti hai.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Chhotu ki Chai ki Dukan se Supermarket tak ka Safar (Shishu, Kishor, Tarun)
* **Stage 1: Shishu (₹50,000 tak):**  
  * *Example:* Chhotu ne railway station ke bahar nayi chai ki tapri shuru karni hai. Usse gas stove, bartan aur thoda saman lene ke liye ₹30,000 chahiye. Bank usse **Shishu** category ke tehat bina kisi security ke loan de deta hai.
* **Stage 2: Kishor (₹50,000 se ₹5 Lakh tak):**  
  * *Example:* Ab Chhotu ki chai bohot famous ho gayi! Ab wo apni tapri ko ek pakke cafe/restaurant me badalna chahta hai jisme fridge, furniture aur deep freezer lagana hai. Iske liye usse ₹3 Lakh chahiye. Bank usse **Kishor** loan deta hai.
* **Stage 3: Tarun (₹5 Lakh se ₹10 Lakh tak):**  
  * *Example:* Chhotu ka cafe superhit ho gaya. Ab wo shahar me 3 naye outlets kholna chahta hai aur delivery van khareedna chahta hai jisme ₹8 Lakh lagenge. Ab wo **Tarun** category me loan leta hai!

### 📌 2. MUDRA Card kya hota hai?
* Jab kisi vyapari ko MUDRA loan milta hai, toh working capital kharch karne ke liye usse ek **RuPay Debit Card** diya jata hai jise **MUDRA Card** kehte hain.
* Dukandar jab wholesale mandi jata hai, toh cash nikalne ya card swipe karke maal khareedne ke liye is card ka upyog karta hai. Byaj sirf utne hi paise par lagta hai jitna usne card se withdraw kiya ho!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. MUDRA Loan Categories & Parameter Matrix
| Category | Loan Amount Range | Target Business Stage | Typical Beneficiaries |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Shishu (शिशु)** | **Up to ₹50,000** | Shuruati daur (Startups / Entry level) | Raddi wale, sabji/fal thele wale, choti dukan, gaddi repair |
| **2. Kishor (किशोर)** | **Above ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000** | Business expand karne wale (Mid-stage) | Kirana stores, beauty parlour, tailor shops, boutique, bakery |
| **3. Tarun (तरुण)** | **Above ₹5,00,000 to ₹10,00,000** | Mature small units (Scaling up) | Small manufacturing units, fleet operators, wholesale traders |

---

### B. MUDRA Scheme ke Key Operational Norms
1. **Collateral Free Nature:** RBI guidelines ke mutabiq Micro enterprises ko ₹10 Lakh tak ke loans par koi collateral security nahi li ja sakti.
2. **Processing Fee:** **Shishu loans par ZERO processing fee** hoti hai.
3. **Lending Institutions:**
   * Public Sector Banks
   * Private Sector Banks
   * Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)
   * Small Finance Banks (SFBs)
   * Micro Finance Institutions (MFIs) aur NBFCs.
4. **Credit Guarantee:** **CGFMU (Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units)** banaya gaya hai jisse NCGTC (National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company) manage karti hai. Agar borrower default kare, toh bank ko guarantee fund se bharpai milti hai.

#### Q1. What does 'MUDRA' stand for?
(a) Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency  
(b) Medium Units Development & Reinvestment Authority  
(c) Monetary Union for Development of Rural Areas  
(d) Micro Urban Development and Regulatory Agency  
(e) None of these  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** MUDRA ka full form **Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency** hai.

(a) 15 August 2014  
(b) 8 April 2015  
(c) 2 October 2015  
(d) 1 January 2016  
(e) 26 January 2015  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** PMMY ko **8 April 2015** ko launch kiya gaya tha.
