## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Scheduled Banks vs Non-Scheduled Banks:**
  * **Scheduled Bank:** Wo bank jo **Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 ke Second Schedule** me shamil ho. Do shartein: 1) Paid-up capital + reserves kam se kam **₹5 Lakh** hone chahiye, 2) Bank ka kaam depositors ke hit ke khilaf na ho. RBI se saste rate par loan lene aur clearing house membership ke yogya hote hain.
  * **Non-Scheduled Bank:** Jo RBI Act ke Second Schedule me listed na ho.
* **Commercial Banks ke 4 Bhaag:**
  1. **Public Sector Banks (PSBs):** Sarkar ki hissedari $\ge 51\%$ (e.g. SBI, PNB, BOB).
  2. **Private Sector Banks:** Private investors ki majority equity (Old Private vs New Private jaise HDFC, ICICI).
  3. **Foreign Banks:** Headquarter videsh me hai, branch Bharat me operate karti hai (e.g. Standard Chartered, Citi, HSBC).
  4. **Regional Rural Banks (RRBs):** Gramin vikas ke liye (Sthapna 1975).
* **Co-operative Banks:** "One member, one vote" ke cooperative principle par chalne wale banks.
  * **Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs)**
  * **Rural Co-operatives (3-tier structure):** State Co-op Bank (StCB) $\rightarrow$ District Central Co-op Bank (DCCB) $\rightarrow$ Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS).

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Commercial Bank vs Co-operative Bank me antar kya hai?
* **Commercial Bank (SBI, HDFC):** Mukhya uddeshya hota hai **Profit kamana** aur share-holders ko dividend dena. Ek shareholder ke paas jitne zyada shares honge, uske paas utni voting power hogi.
* **Co-operative Bank (Saraswat Bank, Shamrao Vithal Bank):** Mukhya uddeshya hota hai **Apne members ki aapsi madad (Mutual Help) karna**. Yahan democratic model hota hai — chahe aapke paas 1 share ho ya 100 share, vote sirf **1 hi milega (One Person, One Vote)**!

### 📌 2. Scheduled Bank hone ka kya rutba (prestige) hota hai?
Socho ek cricket player jo Ranji Trophy khel raha hai vs ek jo Indian National Team (BCCI Central Contract) me chuna gaya hai.
* **Scheduled Bank** wahi national team ka player hai:
  * Usse RBI se Repo Rate par emergency loan mil sakta hai.
  * Wo aam public se deposits accept karne me zyada vishwasniya mana jata hai.
  * Sarkari schemes aur clearing house me seedhe entry milti hai.

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Structure of Indian Banking System
```
Indian Banking System
├── Reserve Bank of India (Central Bank & Regulator)
│
├── Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs)
│   ├── Public Sector Banks (12 PSBs - SBI + 11 Nationalised Banks)
│   ├── Private Sector Banks (Old Private Banks + New Private Banks)
│   ├── Foreign Banks in India
│   └── Regional Rural Banks (RRBs - 43 RRBs)
│
├── Scheduled Co-operative Banks
│   ├── Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs)
│   └── State Co-operative Banks (StCBs)
│
├── Non-Scheduled Banks (Local Area Banks & small non-scheduled co-ops)
│
└── Differentiated Banks (Niche Banks)
    ├── Small Finance Banks (SFBs)
    └── Payments Banks (PBs)
```

---

### B. Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs) Criteria
* **Section 42(6)(a) of RBI Act, 1934:**
  1. Minimum paid-up capital aur aggregate value of reserves **₹5 Lakh** se kam nahi honi chahiye.
  2. Bank ka conduct aur management depositors ke interests ke against nahi hona chahiye.
* **Fayde:**
  * RBI se Bank Rate / Repo rate par financial assistance lene ka kanooni adhikar.
  * Automated Clearing House membership.
  * First-class commercial bills rediscount karwane ki suvidha.

---

### C. Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) Ownership Pattern
RRB Act, 1976 ke tahet RRBs me 3 hitdharakon (stakeholders) ki hissedari fix hoti hai:
| Stakeholder | Equity Shareholding Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Central Government (Bharat Sarkar)** | **50%** |
| **Sponsor Bank (Sarkari Commercial Bank)** | **35%** |
| **State Government (Rajya Sarkar)** | **15%** |

---

### D. Cooperative Credit Structure (ग्रामीण सहकारी ढांचा)
Rural cooperatives 3-tier structure me kaam karte hain:
1. **Apex Level (Rajya star par):** **State Co-operative Bank (StCB)** — Har rajya me ek apex body.
2. **Intermediate Level (Zila star par):** **District Central Co-operative Bank (DCCB)**.
3. **Grassroots Level (Gaon star par):** **Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS)** — Seedhe kisano ko short-term crop loans deti hai (PACS bank nahi hoti, cooperative society hoti hai).

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

### 💡 Mnemonic: RRB Shareholding 50 : 35 : 15
* **50%** = Centre (Bada Bhai)
* **35%** = Sponsor Bank (Bank chalane wala)
* **15%** = State (Zameen dene wali state)  
👉 *Trick:* **50 - 35 - 15** (Pachaas, Paintis, Pandrah).

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. To be included in the Second Schedule of the RBI Act, 1934, a bank must have paid-up capital and reserves of at least:
(a) ₹1 Lakh  
(b) ₹5 Lakh  
(c) ₹50 Lakh  
(d) ₹1 Crore  
(e) ₹5 Crore  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** RBI Act ke Second Schedule me aane ke liye statutory requirement kam se kam **₹5 Lakh** ki paid-up capital aur reserves ki hoti hai.

#### Q2. What is the shareholding pattern of the Central Government in Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)?
(a) 35%  
(b) 15%  
(c) 50%  
(d) 51%  
(e) 100%  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** RRBs me Central Government ka share **50%**, Sponsor Bank ka **35%**, aur State Government ka **15%** hota hai.

#### Q3. What is the grassroot level institution in the short-term rural cooperative credit structure?
(a) State Cooperative Bank  
(b) District Central Cooperative Bank  
(c) Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS)  
(d) Land Development Bank  
(e) Regional Rural Bank  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Gaon ke level par sabse basic unit **PACS (Primary Agricultural Credit Society)** hoti hai jo kisano ko seedhe kheti ke liye loan deti hai.
