## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi):**
  * Sthapna: **24 February 2019** (Effective from 1 Dec 2018).
  * Direct Income Support: **₹6,000 prati varsh** 3 barabar kishton me (**₹2,000 har 4 mahine me**) seedhe bank account me (DBT).
* **PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana):**
  * Launch: **18 February 2016** (Kharif 2016 se lagu).
  * Premium Slabs: **Kharif crops: 2%**, **Rabi crops: 1.5%**, **Commercial/Horticultural crops: 5%**. Baki subsidy Kendra aur Rajya sarkar 50:50 baant-ti hain.
* **KCC (Kisan Credit Card Scheme):**
  * Sthapna: **August 1998** (*R.V. Gupta Committee* sifarish par, model scheme by NABARD).
  * Feature: Single window credit for crop cultivation, post-harvest, domestic needs. Net interest **4%** (7% normal par 3% prompt repayment subvention milne ke baad up to ₹3 Lakh).
* **PMAY (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana):**
  * Launch: **25 June 2015**. "Housing for All" (PMAY-Urban & PMAY-Gramin).
  * Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) ke tehat interest subsidy provide ki jati hai.
* **PMEGP (Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme):**
  * Nodal Agency: **KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission)**.
  * Maximum Project Cost: Manufacturing me **₹50 Lakh** (revised), Services me **₹20 Lakh**. Margin money subsidy: **15% se 35%**.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. PMFBY: Barsat me Fasal Kharab Hui Toh Khet Ka Beema
* **Scenario:** Farmer Ramu ne 5 bigha zameen par dhan (paddy - Kharif crop) lagaya jiski fasal ki insured value ₹1,00,000 thi.
* **Premium:** Ramu ko sirf **2% (₹2,000)** premium bharna pada. Baaki ka bhari premium (jaise ₹10,000) Sarkar ne subsidy me diya.
* **Benefit:** Achanak badh (flood) aa gayi aur fasal barbad ho gayi. Insurance company ne satellite imagery aur drone survey se Ramu ke khate me poora ₹1,00,000 transfer kar diya!

### 📌 2. KCC (Kisan Credit Card): Kisan ka ATM aur Credit Card
* Kisan ko har baar fasal bone ke samay bank manager ke aage hath jodkar nayi file nahi lagani padti. Uska ek **Revolving Cash Credit limit** ban jata hai jo 5 saal ke liye valid hota hai.
* Kisan seedhe KCC RuPay card lekar beej aur khad ki dukan par swipe karta hai. Agar wo fasal katne ke baad time par loan chuka deta hai, toh usse sirf **4% saalana** ka byaj lagta hai (jo personal loan ke 12-14% ke mukable bohot sasta hai)!

---

### B. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Norms
* **Validity:** **5 saal** (har saal annual review hota hai).
* **Subvention Scheme:**
  * Base lending rate = **9%**
  * Government interest subvention = **2%** $ightarrow$ Effective rate = **7%**
  * Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) = **3%** $ightarrow$ **Net Rate = 4%** (up to ₹3 Lakh limit).
* **Expansion:** 2018-19 ke budget me KCC ko **Animal Husbandry (Pashupalan) aur Fisheries (Matsyapalan)** ke liye bhi extend kar diya gaya (limit ₹2 Lakh alag se).

---

### C. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
* **Outlay:** 100% Central Sector Scheme (Bharat Sarkar dwara fully funded).
* **Installments:** Har 4 mahine me **₹2,000** ki 3 kistein:
  1. Period 1: April se July
  2. Period 2: August se November
  3. Period 3: December se March.
* **Exclusion Criteria:** Institutional landholders, constitutional post holders, serving/retired government employees, income taxpayers, professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers).

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

* **5%** = **C**ommercial / Horticulture (Bade fasal par 5).

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Under PM-KISAN, what is the annual financial benefit provided to eligible farmer families?
(a) ₹4,000  
(b) ₹5,000  
(c) ₹6,000  
(d) ₹8,000  
(e) ₹10,000  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** PM-KISAN ke tahet kisan parivaron ko saalana **₹6,000** teen barabar kishton (₹2,000 har kist) me seedhe DBT ke zariye milte hain.

#### Q2. What is the premium rate payable by farmers for Kharif food crops under PMFBY?
(a) 1.5%  
(b) 2.0%  
(c) 3.0%  
(d) 5.0%  
(e) 1.0%  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** PMFBY ke tahet Kharif food aur oilseed crops par farmer ka premium **2.0%** hota hai.

#### Q3. What is the premium rate for commercial and horticultural crops under PMFBY?
(a) 2.0%  
(b) 3.5%  
(c) 5.0%  
(d) 7.0%  
(e) 1.5%  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Commercial aur horticultural crops ke liye farmer dwara diya jane wala premium **5.0%** hota hai.

#### Q4. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme was introduced in which year?
(a) 1991  
(b) 1995  
(c) 1998  
(d) 2000  
(e) 2004  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** KCC scheme ko **August 1998** me NABARD dwara model scheme ke roop me shuru kiya gaya tha.

#### Q5. With prompt repayment incentive, what is the effective net interest rate for short-term crop loans up to ₹3 Lakh under KCC?
(a) 3%  
(b) 4%  
(c) 5%  
(d) 7%  
(e) 2%  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 7% base rate me se 3% ka prompt repayment subvention milne ke baad kisan ko effectively sirf **4%** byaj dena padta hai.
