## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Basel Norms Kya Hain:** Duniya bhar ke banks ko doobne se bachane ke liye international capital adequacy aur risk management ke rules.
* **Formulating Body:** **BCBS (Basel Committee on Banking Supervision)**, jo **BIS (Bank for International Settlements)** ke headquarters **Basel, Switzerland** me sthit hai (Setup in 1974 by G-10 countries).
* **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR / CRAR):** Bank ke Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA) ke mukable bank ke paas kitni capital honi chahiye:
  $$\text{CRAR} = \frac{\text{Tier 1 Capital} + \text{Tier 2 Capital}}{\text{Risk Weighted Assets (RWA)}} \times 100$$
* **Basel Accords ka Vikas:**
  * **Basel I (1988):** Sirf **Credit Risk** par focus tha. Minimum CAR = **8%**. Bharat ne 1999 me adopt kiya.
  * **Basel II (2004):** Three Pillars model (Pillar 1: Minimum Capital for Credit, Market & Operational Risk; Pillar 2: Supervisory Review; Pillar 3: Market Discipline).
  * **Basel III (2010):** 2008 ke global financial crisis ke baad aaya. Higher capital, CCB (Capital Conservation Buffer 2.5%), Leverage Ratio, LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio), aur NSFR (Net Stable Funding Ratio).
* **RBI Mandate for Indian Banks:** Bharat me commercial banks ke liye minimum CAR **9%** hai (Basel minimum 8% se 1% zyada!), aur CCB jodkar **11.5%** hai.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Bank ke Paas "Airbag" (Capital) Kyun Zaroori Hai?
* **Car ka Example:** Jab aap car chalate ho, toh car me seatbelt aur airbag hote hain taaki accident hone par jaan bach sake.
* **Bank ka Accident (Loan Default):** Bank aam logon se deposit lekar loan baant-ta hai. Agar bohot saare log (jaise Vijay Mallya ya Nirav Modi) loan wapas na karein, toh bank ke paas apni khud ki aisi savings honi chahiye jisse wo aam depositors ka paisa lauta sake. Yeh jo bank ki khud ki permanent safety capital hoti hai, isse hi **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR)** kehte hain!

### 📌 2. Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA) kya hota hai?
Bank har asset (loan) ko ek saman nahi maanta. Jisme jitna risk, uspe utna weight:
* **Government Bond:** Risk = **0%** (Sarkar kabhi default nahi karti).
* **Home Loan:** Risk = **35% se 50%** (Ghar girwi hota hai, log apna ghar nahi chhodte).
* **Credit Card / Personal Loan (Unsecured):** Risk = **100% se 150%** (Koyi guarantee nahi hai, paisa doobne ka darr zyada hai).
* Agar bank ne ₹100 ka unsecured personal loan diya hai, toh usse support karne ke liye kam se kam ₹9 ki khud ki capital rakhni padegi!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Basel II ke 3 Pillars (Three Pillars of Basel II)
Basel II ne pure risk framework ko 3 pillars me divide kiya:
1. **Pillar 1: Minimum Capital Requirements (न्यूनतम पूँजी आवश्यकताएं)**
   * Bank ko teen tarah ke risks ke liye capital maintain karni padti hai:
     * **Credit Risk:** Borrower loan wapas na kare.
     * **Market Risk:** Share market, interest rate ya foreign exchange me utar-chhadhav se nuksan ho.
     * **Operational Risk:** Fraud, system failure, cyber attack ya manviya galati se nuksan ho.
2. **Pillar 2: Supervisory Review Process (पर्यवेक्षी समीक्षा प्रक्रिया)**
   * Central Bank (RBI) dekhta hai ki kya bank apne internal risks ko sahi se judge kar raha hai (**ICAAP** - Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process). Agar bank risky lagta hai, toh RBI usse standard limit se zyada capital rakhne ko keh sakta hai.
3. **Pillar 3: Market Discipline (बाज़ार अनुशासन)**
   * Transparency aur Disclosures: Banks ko har quarter publically disclose karna padta hai ki unka CAR kitna hai, NPA kitna hai, aur risk profile kaisa hai taaki investors aur depositors jagruk rahein.

### B. Tier 1 vs Tier 2 Capital
| Parameter | Tier 1 Capital (Core Capital) | Tier 2 Capital (Supplementary Capital) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quality** | Highest quality, permanent loss-absorbing capacity. | Secondary quality, limited loss absorption. |
| **Components** | • Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1): Paid-up equity capital, statutory reserves, disclosed free reserves.<br>• Additional Tier 1 (AT1): Perpetual Non-Cumulative Preference Shares (PNCPS), Perpetual Debt Instruments. | • Undisclosed reserves.<br>• Revaluation reserves (55% discount ke sath).<br>• General provisions & loss reserves (max 1.25% of RWA).<br>• Subordinated term debt (min 5-year maturity). |
| **Going Concern / Gone Concern** | **Going-concern capital** (bank chalte-chalte hi losses absorb kar leti hai). | **Gone-concern capital** (jab bank liquidate ya band hone ki naubat par aata hai tab depositors ko pay karne ke kaam aati hai). |

### C. Basel III Norms & Indian Implementation
2008 ke Lehman Brothers crash ke baad Basel III laya gaya taaki banks kisi bhi global jhatke ko jhel sakein:
1. **Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB):** Normal acche samay me banks ko **2.5% ka extra CET1 capital buffer** banana padta hai, taaki sankat ke samay use use kiya ja sake.
2. **Countercyclical Capital Buffer (CCCB):** Jab economy bohot tezi se grow kar rahi ho (boom period), tab credit growth ko control karne ke liye **0% se 2.5%** tak ka extra capital maintain karna.
3. **Leverage Ratio:** Total unweighted exposures ke mukable Tier 1 capital kam se kam **3.5%** honi chahiye.
4. **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR):** Bank ke paas aisi high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) honi chahiye jo **30 din tak ke extreme cash outflow** ko sambhal sakein (Min: **100%**).
5. **Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR):** 1 saal tak ke horizon ke liye bank ke paas stable funding honi chahiye (Min: **100%**).

## 📊 Capital Requirements Matrix (Basel vs RBI Norms)
| Capital Component | Basel III International Minimum | RBI Norms for Scheduled Commercial Banks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1)** | 4.5% | **5.5%** |
| **Tier 1 Capital (CET1 + AT1)** | 6.0% | **7.0%** |
| **Tier 2 Capital (Max)** | 2.0% | **2.0%** |
| **Minimum Total Capital (CRAR)** | **8.0%** | **9.0%** (PSBs ke liye 12% target) |
| **Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB)** | 2.5% | **2.5%** |
| **Total Capital + CCB** | **10.5%** | **11.5%** |

#### Q1. Where is the headquarters of the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) and Basel Committee located?
(a) Geneva, Switzerland  
(b) Basel, Switzerland  
(c) Zurich, Switzerland  
(d) London, UK  
(e) Washington D.C., USA  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** BCBS aur BIS ka headquarters **Basel, Switzerland** me sthit hai.

#### Q2. Basel-I accord was introduced in which year?
(a) 1974  
(b) 1988  
(c) 1992  
(d) 1999  
(e) 2004  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Basel-I accord ko **1988** me introduce kiya gaya tha jisme sirf credit risk par focus tha.

#### Q3. How many pillars are there in the Basel-II framework?
(a) 2  
(b) 3  
(c) 4  
(d) 5  
(e) 7  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Basel-II me **3 Pillars** hain: 1) Minimum Capital, 2) Supervisory Review, 3) Market Discipline.

#### Q4. What is the minimum Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) prescribed by RBI for commercial banks in India (excluding CCB)?
(a) 8%  
(b) 9%  
(c) 10%  
(d) 11.5%  
(e) 12%  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** RBI ne Indian commercial banks ke liye minimum CRAR **9%** fix kiya hai (jo international Basel 8% se 1% zyada hai).

#### Q5. What is the value of Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB) under Basel III?
(a) 1.5%  
(b) 2.0%  
(c) 2.5%  
(d) 3.0%  
(e) 3.5%  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** CCB ki value **2.5% of Risk-Weighted Assets** hoti hai, jo Common Equity Tier 1 ke form me maintain karni hoti hai.

#### Q6. Which capital is known as the 'Core Capital' of a bank?
(a) Tier 1 Capital  
(b) Tier 2 Capital  
(c) Tier 3 Capital  
(d) Subordinated Debt  
(e) Revaluation Reserves  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Tier 1 Capital** ko bank ki 'Core Capital' mana jata hai kyunki isme highest quality equity aur disclosed reserves hote hain.
