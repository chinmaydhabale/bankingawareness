#### Q1. Which authority is the nation's sole banknote issuing authority in India?
(a) Ministry of Finance  
(b) Reserve Bank of India  
(c) State Bank of India  
(d) Securities and Exchange Board of India  
(e) National Payments Corporation of India  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* **Kanooni Pravdhan:** *RBI Act, 1934 ka Section 22* RBI ko Bharat me banknotes issue karne ka exclusive/sole authority banata hai.  
* **Exceptions to remember:** ₹1 ka note aur sabhi coins Ministry of Finance (Bharat Sarkar) dwara issue hote hain, jisme ₹1 ke note par Finance Secretary ke sign hote hain. Baki sabhi notes RBI Governor ke signature se aate hain.

#### Q2. What does 'C' stand for in the abbreviation 'CRAR' under Basel banking norms?
(a) Credit  
(b) Capital  
(c) Currency  
(d) Cash  
(e) Central  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* **Full Form:** **Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR)** jisse aam taur par **CAR (Capital Adequacy Ratio)** bhi kehte hain.  
* **Formula:** $\text{CRAR} = \frac{\text{Tier 1} + \text{Tier 2 Capital}}{\text{Risk-Weighted Assets}} \times 100$.  
* **Significance:** Yeh batata hai ki bank ke paas risky loans ke mukable kitna surakshit capital cushion maujood hai. RBI ke mutabiq commercial banks ke liye minimum CRAR **9%** (aur CCB milaakar **11.5%**) hona compulsory hai.

#### Q3. In which year was the 'Bharatiya Reserve Bank Note Mudran Private Limited' (BRBNMPL) established by RBI?
(a) 1985  
(b) 1990  
(c) 1995  
(d) 1999  
(e) 2002  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* BRBNMPL ki sthapna **3 February 1995** ko RBI ki wholly owned subsidiary ke roop me hui thi.  
* Iske do modern note printing presses **Mysuru (Karnataka)** aur **Salboni (West Bengal)** me hain. (Baki do presses Nashik aur Dewas me hain jo central government ki SPMCIL ke adheen hain).

#### Q4. Under the SARFAESI Act, 2002, secured creditors can enforce their security interest without court intervention if the outstanding default amount is:
(a) More than ₹50,000  
(b) More than ₹1,00,000  
(c) More than ₹10,00,000  
(d) More than ₹20,00,000  
(e) Any amount  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* **Section 31 Exemption:** SARFAESI Act ke provisions un accounts par lagu nahi hote jahan total outstanding debt **₹1 Lakh se kam** ho ya jahan remaining balance principal aur interest ka **20% se kam** bacha ho. Isliye claim amount kam se kam ₹1 Lakh hona anivarya hai.  
* Iske alawa Agricultural land par bhi SARFAESI lagu nahi hota.

#### Q5. A financial entity which has characteristics of both commercial banks and NBFCs, and whose 75% lending is mandatorily directed towards Priority Sector is known as:
(a) Payments Bank  
(b) Small Finance Bank  
(c) Regional Rural Bank  
(d) Local Area Bank  
(e) Co-operative Bank  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* **Small Finance Banks (SFBs):** Usha Thorat committee ki sifarish par shuru huye niche banks hain jo normal commercial banks ki tarah deposits bhi accept karte hain aur loans bhi dete hain.  
* Inke liye golden condition yeh hai ki inka **75% of ANBC priority sector lending (PSL)** me jana chahiye aur **25% branches unbanked rural centres** me honi chahiye.

#### Q6. What is the maximum limit on end-of-the-day deposit balance in a Payments Bank account per individual customer?
(a) ₹50,000  
(b) ₹1,00,000  
(c) ₹2,00,000  
(d) ₹5,00,000  
(e) No limit  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* Shuruat me Nachiket Mor committee guidelines ke tahet limit ₹1 Lakh thi.  
* **April 2021** me RBI ne financial inclusion aur digital payments ko badhava dene ke liye Payments Banks me per customer maximum balance limit badhakar **₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh)** kar di.

#### Q7. The Reserve Bank of India transfers its surplus profits (dividend) to the Government of India under which Section of the RBI Act, 1934?
(a) Section 42  
(b) Section 45  
(c) Section 47  
(d) Section 48  
(e) Section 49  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* **Section 47 of RBI Act, 1934:** Yeh section pravdhan karta hai ki bad debts, depreciation aur contingency reserves me zaroori allocation karne ke baad bacha hua sara **surplus profit Reserve Bank Central Government ko transfer** karega.  
* Is surplus transfer ke framework ko finalize karne ke liye **Dr. Bimal Jalan Committee (2019)** ka gathan kiya gaya tha.

#### Q8. What does 'L' stand for in the acronym 'CAMELS' supervisory rating model?
(a) Leverage  
(b) Liquidity  
(c) Loan  
(d) Liability  
(e) Loss  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* **CAMELS Rating Components:**
  * **C** — Capital Adequacy
  * **A** — Asset Quality
  * **M** — Management Quality
  * **E** — Earnings Quality
  * **L** — **Liquidity**
  * **S** — Sensitivity to Market Risk.

#### Q9. Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) is a statutory body established under the provisions of which Act?
(a) Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934  
(b) Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992  
(c) Companies Act, 2013  
(d) Banking Regulation Act, 1949  
(e) Insurance Act, 1938  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* **SAT (Securities Appellate Tribunal):** SEBI Act, 1992 ke **Section 15K** ke tahet sthapit ek statutory body hai.  
* SEBI, IRDAI aur PFRDA ke orders ke khilaf appeal SAT me ki jati hai. SAT ke faisle ke khilaf aakhri appeal seedhe Supreme Court of India me hoti hai.

#### Q10. What is the maximum insurance compensation provided by DICGC to a depositor in case of a bank failure?
(a) ₹1,00,000  
(b) ₹2,00,000  
(c) ₹5,00,000  
(d) ₹10,00,000  
(e) ₹25,00,000  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* Union Budget 2020 me DICGC insurance coverage limit ko ₹1 Lakh se badhakar **₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakh)** kar diya gaya.  
* Yeh cover per depositor, per bank (Principal + Interest milaakar) lagu hota hai. 2021 ke sanshodhan ke mutabiq moratorium lagne ke **90 din** ke andar depositors ko yeh amount milna anivarya hai.

#### Q12. Under Priority Sector Lending guidelines, what percentage of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) is earmarked as sub-target for Micro Enterprises?
(a) 5%  
(b) 7.5%  
(c) 8%  
(d) 10%  
(e) 12%  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* Micro enterprises ke liye mandatory sub-target **7.5% of ANBC** fix hai.  
* Wahi Small and Marginal Farmers (SMF) ke liye **10%**, Total Agriculture ke liye **18%**, aur Weaker Sections ke liye **12%** target hota hai.

#### Q13. The Cheque Truncation System (CTS) operates on which grid-based clearing model in India?
(a) State-level grids  
(b) 3 National Regional Grids (Western, Northern, Southern)  
(c) 5 Zonal grids  
(d) Single Centralized Delhi grid  
(e) District-level grids  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* NPCI dwara managed CTS system Bharat me **3 Regional Grids** me chalta hai:  
  1. **Western Grid:** Mumbai  
  2. **Northern Grid:** New Delhi  
  3. **Southern Grid:** Chennai.  
* Isme physical cheque ko doosre shahar bhejne ki jagah high-resolution digital image aur magnetic MICR data electronically transfer kiya jata hai.

#### Q14. In Banking Ombudsman Scheme, what is the time limit given to a bank to respond to a customer's complaint before the customer can approach the Ombudsman?
(a) 15 days  
(b) 30 days  
(c) 45 days  
(d) 60 days  
(e) 90 days  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Detailed Explanation (Hinglish):**  
* Customer ko pehle concerned bank ke paas complaint lodge karni hoti hai. Agar bank **30 din tak** koi jawab nahi deta ya complaint reject kar deta hai, tabhi customer Banking Ombudsman ke CMS portal par appeal kar sakta hai.
