## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Share (शेयर / अंश):** Kisi company ki total capital ko chhote-chhote hisson me baantne par jo ek unit banti hai, use Share kehte hain. Shareholder company ka **part-owner (malik)** hota hai.
* **Debenture (ऋणपत्र):** Company dwara public se lambi avadhi (long-term) ke liye liya gaya kanooni udhaar dastavej (Debt instrument). Debenture holder company ka **Creditor (lender)** hota hai, malik nahi.
* **Shares ke Mukhya Bhaag:**
  * **Equity Shares:** Real owners, voting rights, fluctuating dividend (profit par aadharit).
  * **Preference Shares:** Fixed dividend, company liquidation par pehle capital wapsi ka adhikar. Aam taur par no voting rights.
  * **Sweat Equity Shares:** Employees ya directors ko intellectual property ya hard work ke badle discount ya non-cash consideration par diye gaye shares.
* **Debentures ke Mukhya Bhaag:**
  * **Secured vs Unsecured:** Asset mortgage hai ya nahi.
  * **Convertible vs Non-Convertible (NCDs):** Future me equity share me convert ho sakte hain ya nahi.
* **Key Corporate Actions:**
  * **Bonus Shares:** Free shares given to existing shareholders from accumulated reserves.
  * **Rights Issue:** Existing shareholders ko discounted price par naye shares khareedne ka pehla adhikar dena.
  * **Stock Split:** Badi face value wale share ko chhote shares me todna (e.g. ₹10 face value ko ₹2-₹2 ke 5 shares banana).

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Shareholder vs Debenture Holder: Malik vs Saahukaar
* **Shareholder (Company ka Malik - High Risk, High Reward):**  
  * Ramesh ne Tata Motors ke 100 shares khareede. Ramesh ab Tata Motors ka hissedar (malik) hai. Agar Tata Motors ko bumper profit hua, toh Ramesh ko mota **Dividend** milega aur share price badhegi. Lekin agar company ko loss hua, toh Ramesh ko ₹0 dividend milega aur uska share price gir jayega!
* **Debenture Holder (Saahukaar - Fixed Byaj):**  
  * Suresh ne Tata Motors ke ₹10,000 ke Debentures khareede jisme likha hai *'9% Interest per annum'*. Suresh ko company ke loss ya profit se koi lena-dena nahi hai. Chahe Tata Motors doob rahi ho ya record munafa kamaye, company ko Suresh ko har saal **9% byaj (₹900)** dena hi padega!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Shares vs Debentures Master Comparison Matrix
| Parameter | Shares (शेयर) | Debentures (डिबेंचर) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Status of Holder** | Company ka **Owner (मालिक)** | Company ka **Creditor / Lender (ऋणदाता)** |
| **Return / Income** | **Dividend (लाभांश)** — profit hone par hi milta hai | **Interest (ब्याज)** — mandatory charge on profit |
| **Voting Rights** | **Haan**, Annual General Meeting (AGM) me vote de sakte hain | **Nahi**, management me koi voting power nahi hoti |
| **Risk Level** | High Risk (Capital loss ho sakti hai) | Low / Moderate Risk (Secured hote hain) |
| **Liquidation Priority** | Sabse aakhri me bacha hua paisa milta hai | Shareholders se pehle repayment ka kanooni adhikar |
| **Tax Deductibility** | Dividend company ke net profit ke baad banta hai | Interest expense tax-deductible hota hai |

---

### B. Types of Preference Shares
1. **Cumulative vs Non-Cumulative:** Agar kisi saal company ko profit na ho, toh Cumulative preference shares ka dividend carry forward hokar agle saal judkar milta hai.
2. **Participating vs Non-Participating:** Fixed dividend lene ke baad agar company ke paas extra surplus bache, toh Participating holders us surplus me se bhi hissa paate hain.
3. **Redeemable vs Irredeemable:** Company law ke mutabiq Bharat me sabhi preference shares maximum **20 saal** ke andar redeem (wapas khareedna) karne hote hain (Infrastructure companies ke liye 30 saal).

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. A person who holds shares of a company is known as:
(a) Creditor  
(b) Owner / Shareholder  
(c) Debtor  
(d) Underwriter  
(e) Custodian  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Shareholder company ka **part-owner (hissedar)** mana jata hai.

#### Q2. What return does a debenture holder receive from the company?
(a) Dividend  
(b) Fixed Interest  
(c) Royalty  
(d) Commission  
(e) Bonus  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Debenture holders ko company dwara pre-determined **Fixed Interest (biyaj)** diya jata hai chahe company profit me ho ya loss me.

#### Q3. Shares issued by a company to its employees or directors at a discount or for consideration other than cash for technical know-how are called:
(a) Preference Shares  
(b) Sweat Equity Shares  
(c) Bonus Shares  
(d) Rights Shares  
(e) Growth Shares  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Employees aur directors ko unki mehnat aur technical expertise ke liye diye jane wale shares ko **Sweat Equity Shares** kehte hain.

#### Q4. Free additional shares given to existing shareholders based on their current holdings are called:
(a) Rights Shares  
(b) Bonus Shares  
(c) Preferred Shares  
(d) Convertible Shares  
(e) Split Shares  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Existing shareholders ko accumulated profits se free me diye gaye shares ko **Bonus Shares** kaha jata hai.
