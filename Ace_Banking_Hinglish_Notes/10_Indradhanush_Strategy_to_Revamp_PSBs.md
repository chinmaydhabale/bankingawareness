## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Launch Date:** **August 2015** me Ministry of Finance (GoI) dwara launch kiya gaya.
* **Objective:** Public Sector Banks (PSBs) ki functioning ko sudharna, unke bad loans (NPAs) ki samasya ko khatam karna, professional management lana aur capital provide karna.
* **Indradhanush ke 7 Rang (7 Pillars: A se G tak):**
  * **A — Appointments:** Chairman aur MD & CEO ke posts ko alag karna; private sector se talent lena.
  * **B — Bank Board Bureau (BBB):** PSB chiefs ki transparent selection aur capital planning ke liye autonomous body banana (*P.J. Nayak Committee*).
  * **C — Capitalization:** Sarkari banks me **₹70,000 Crore** ki equity capital daalna (4 saal me).
  * **D — De-stressing:** NPAs ko resolve karne ke liye 5:25 scheme, SDR (Strategic Debt Restructuring), Asset Reconstruction Companies, aur DRT ko mazboot karna.
  * **E — Empowering:** Bank boards ko business decisions lene ki azaadi dena, sarkari dakhil-andazi (interference) band karna.
  * **F — Framework of Accountability:** Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) set karna; performance ke aadhar par bonus aur incentives dena.
  * **G — Governance Reforms:** 'Gyan Sangam' conclave aayojit karna jahan banker aur sarkar milkar strategy banayein.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 Kyun zaroorat padi Indradhanush Scheme ki?
* **Problem:** 2014-15 ke aas-paas sarkari banks (PSBs) bohot buri halat me the. Unke karodo rupaye ke loans (infrastructure, steel, power sector) doob rahe the (NPA crisis). Sarkari babu aur neta log phone karke loan dilwa dete the. Bank ke paas naya loan dene ke liye capital hi nahi bachi thi.
* **Doctor ka Ilaaj (Indradhanush - 7 Medicines):** Sarkar ne ek comprehensive 7-point formula banaya jise **Mission Indradhanush** kaha gaya:
  1. Bank ke Top boss (MD) ko achhi salary aur professional azaadi do (**Appointments**).
  2. Neta ji ki sifarish par nahi, impartial committee ke interview se boss chuno (**BBB**).
  3. Bank ki tijori me naye paise (capital) daalo taaki wo mar na jaye (**Capitalization**).
  4. Fasi hui property ko bechkar paisa nikalo (**De-stressing**).
  5. Mantri ji phone karna band karein (**Empowering**).
  6. Jo manager achha kaam kare usse bonus do, jo laparwahi kare usse jawab maango (**Accountability**).
  7. Saare bankers ek jagah baithkar aage ka road map banayein (**Governance - Gyan Sangam**).

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### The 7 Pillars of Mission Indradhanush (A to G Matrix)

| Letter | Pillar Name | Detailed Actions & Exam Points |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | **Appointments** | • Pehle ek hi vyakti CMD (Chairman-cum-Managing Director) hota tha. Isse split karke **Non-Executive Chairman** aur **MD & CEO** banaya gaya.<br>• Private sector ke top bankers ko PSBs lead karne ka mauka diya gaya (jaise Bank of Baroda me P.S. Jayakumar aur Canara Bank me Rakesh Sharma). |
| **B** | **Bank Board Bureau (BBB)** | • Sthapna: **February 2016** me hui (*P.J. Nayak Committee* recommendation par).<br>• Pehle Chairman: **Vinod Rai** (Former CAG).<br>• Kaam: PSBs ke top level executives (MDs, EDs) ki selection recommendation dena aur capital raising strategy banana. |
| **C** | **Capitalization** | • Total Infusion: **₹70,000 Crore** over 4 years (FY 2015-16 se FY 2018-19 tak).<br>• Breakup: FY16 me ₹25,000 Cr, FY17 me ₹25,000 Cr, FY18 me ₹10,000 Cr, FY19 me ₹10,000 Cr.<br>• Banks ko market se baki capital raise karne ke liye encourage kiya gaya. |
| **D** | **De-stressing** | • Stressed assets ke resolution ke liye special instruments: **5:25 Flexible Structuring** (infrastructure loans ko 25 saal tak extend karna, har 5 saal me refinance), **SDR (Strategic Debt Restructuring)** jisme bank defaulter company ka 51% control le leta tha, aur **Asset Quality Review (AQR)**. |
| **E** | **Empowering** | • Banks ke Board of Directors ko commercial decisions lene ki poori azaadi di gayi.<br>• CVC/CBI ke darr ko kam karne ke liye legitimate commercial failure aur corrupt practices me bhedbhav kiya gaya. |
| **F** | **Framework of Accountability** | • Traditional inspection ki jagah **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** banaye gaye.<br>• 4 Quarters me banks ki performance measure hoti hai: NPA management, Return on Assets (ROA), Financial Inclusion, aur CASA growth. |
| **G** | **Governance Reforms** | • **Gyan Sangam:** Prime Minister, Finance Minister aur RBI Governor ke sath PSB chiefs ki do-divasiya retreat/meeting (Pune 2015, Gurgaon 2016). |

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. When was the 'Indradhanush' strategy launched by the Government of India for revamping Public Sector Banks?
(a) August 2014  
(b) August 2015  
(c) January 2016  
(d) April 2017  
(e) July 2018  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Mission Indradhanush **August 2015** me launch kiya gaya tha PSBs ke punarutthan ke liye.

#### Q2. How many key areas (pillars) are there in the Indradhanush framework?
(a) 5  
(b) 6  
(c) 7  
(d) 9  
(e) 12  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Indradhanush (Rainbow) me 7 rang hote hain, isliye iske **7 pillars (A to G)** hain.

#### Q3. In the Indradhanush framework, what does 'B' stand for?
(a) Banking Ombudsman  
(b) Bank Board Bureau  
(c) Basel Norms  
(d) Broad Money  
(e) BSBDA  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 'B' ka matlab **Bank Board Bureau (BBB)** hai jo PSB top management ke selection ke liye banaya gaya tha.

**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Purve CAG **Vinod Rai** Bank Board Bureau ke pehle Chairman the.
