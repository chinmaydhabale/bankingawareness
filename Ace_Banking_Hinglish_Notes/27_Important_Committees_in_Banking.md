## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Committees ka Mahatva:** Bharat ke pure banking aur financial structure me jab bhi koi bada badlav (reforms, naya institution ya kanoon) aaya hai, uske peeche kisi visheshagya (expert committee) ki report rahi hai.
* **Top 10 Most Frequently Asked Committees (Exam Favorites):**
  1. **Hilton-Young Commission (1926):** RBI ki sthapna.
  2. **All India Rural Credit Survey / Gorwala Committee (1954):** SBI ka gathan.
  3. **B. Sivaraman Committee / CRAFICARD (1979/1981):** NABARD ki sthapna.
  4. **M. Narasimham Committee I (1991) & II (1998):** Financial system aur banking sector reforms.
  5. **M.J. Pherwani Committee (1991):** National Stock Exchange (NSE) ki sthapna.
  6. **S.S. Tarapore Committee (1997 & 2006):** Capital Account Convertibility (CAC) & Weak banks ko Narrow Banks banana.
  7. **R.H. Khan Committee (1998):** Universal Banking.
  8. **Dr. C. Rangarajan Committee (2008):** Financial Inclusion.
  9. **Dr. Urjit Patel Committee (2014):** Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) aur Inflation Targeting.
  10. **Dr. Nachiket Mor Committee (2014):** Payments Banks aur small business credit.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 Committees kyun banayi jaati hain?
* **Real-life Analogy:** Socho ghar me shadi hai ya naya makan banana hai. Ghar ke buzurg akele faisla nahi lete; wo rishtedaron aur architecture ke experts ko bulakar ek 'salahakar committee' banate hain jo budget, design aur contractors ki list banakar deti hai.
* **Banking me:** Sarkar ya RBI jab bhi koi kanoon ya naya policy rate instrument lati hai, toh pehle desh ke top economists (jaise Urjit Patel, Narasimham, Bimal Jalan) ko lead dekar committee banayi jati hai. Unki recommendations par hi Parliament me bills aate hain!

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Which committee recommended the establishment of State Bank of India (SBI)?
(a) Hilton Young Commission  
(b) All India Rural Credit Survey Committee (A.D. Gorwala)  
(c) B. Sivaraman Committee  
(d) Narasimham Committee  
(e) K.S. Krishnaswamy Committee  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **A.D. Gorwala Committee (1954)** ki sifarish par Imperial Bank ko nationalise karke SBI banaya gaya tha.

#### Q2. The concept of Universal Banking in India was recommended by:
(a) R.H. Khan Committee  
(b) Narasimham Committee  
(c) Tarapore Committee  
(d) Sodhani Committee  
(e) Verma Committee  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Universal Banking ki recommendation **R.H. Khan Committee (1998)** dwara di gayi thi.

#### Q3. Which committee recommended the setting up of the National Stock Exchange (NSE)?
(a) M.J. Pherwani Committee  
(b) L.C. Gupta Committee  
(c) Malegam Committee  
(d) J.R. Varma Committee  
(e) Dave Committee  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** NSE ki sthapna **M.J. Pherwani Committee (1991)** ki report par hui thi.

#### Q4. Customer service improvements in banks were examined by which committee?
(a) Goiporia Committee  
(b) Damodaran Committee  
(c) Both (a) and (b)  
(d) Sodhani Committee  
(e) Tarapore Committee  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Customer service par do mukhya committees aayi hain: **Goiporia Committee (1990)** aur **Damodaran Committee (2011)**.

#### Q5. The framework for Flexible Inflation Targeting (FIT) and the 6-member MPC was recommended by:
(a) Raghuram Rajan Committee  
(b) Dr. Urjit Patel Committee  
(c) Bimal Jalan Committee  
(d) Y.V. Reddy Committee  
(e) C. Rangarajan Committee  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Monetary policy framework ko revamp karke MPC aur inflation target lana **Dr. Urjit Patel Committee (2014)** ki sifarish thi.
