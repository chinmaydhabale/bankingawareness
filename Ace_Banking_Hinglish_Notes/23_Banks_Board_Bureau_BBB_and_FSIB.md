## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Banks Board Bureau (BBB):** Ek autonomous recommendatory body jo Public Sector Banks (PSBs), Financial Institutions aur Public Sector Insurance Companies ke top management (MDs, CEOs, Executive Directors aur Non-Executive Chairmen) ke selection ke liye banayi gayi thi.
* **Committee:** **Dr. P.J. Nayak Committee (2014)** on Governance of Boards of Banks in India.
* **Formation:** **February 2016** me Mission Indradhanush ke Pillar 'B' ke tahet sthapit.
* **First Chairman:** **Vinod Rai** (Former Comptroller and Auditor General - CAG). HQ: **Mumbai** (RBI Central Office compound).
* **Composition:** Chairman + 3 ex-officio members (Secretary DFS, Secretary DoPT, Deputy Governor of RBI) + 3-5 private sector banking experts.
* **Recent Historic Evolution (Crucial Exam Point!):** Delhi High Court ke ek faisle ke baad, July 2022 me Central Government ne BBB ko replace karke **FSIB (Financial Services Institutions Bureau)** bana diya! FSIB ke pehle Chairman **Bhanu Pratap Sharma** banaye gaye.

---

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 BBB se pehle vs BBB ke baad kya badla?
* **Puraana System (Before 2016):** Pehle sarkari bank ke Chairman ya MD banne ke liye mantriyo aur sarkari afsaron ki sifarish chalti thi. Kai baar corruption aur political connection se log top post par baith jaate the, jisse banks me NPAs badh gaye.
* **BBB Model (After 2016):** Sarkar ne kaha: *"Ab political sifarish band! Ek independent bureau (BBB) banega jo private aur sarkari dono jagah se best bankers ka interview lega, unka merit check karega aur sarkar ko recommend karega ki SBI ya PNB ka boss kaun banega!"*
* **Real Impact:** Isse transparency aayi aur pehli baar private sector ke logon ko bhi sarkari banks ka MD & CEO banne ka mauka mila.

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Functions of Banks Board Bureau (BBB / FSIB)
1. **Selection & Appointments:** Public Sector Banks (PSBs), Public Sector Insurance Companies (LIC, GIC, National Insurance) aur AIFIs (NABARD, SIDBI, EXIM, NHB, NaBFID) ke whole-time directors aur non-executive chairpersons ki selection ke liye candidates recommend karna.
2. **Strategy & Capital Raising:** PSBs ko advise karna ki wo market se capital kaise raise karein aur corporate governance kaise sudharein.
3. **Training & Leadership Development:** Middle aur senior management ke liye leadership development programmes chalana.
4. **Code of Conduct:** PSB boards ke functioning ke liye standards aur ethics guide karna.

---

### B. Transition from BBB to FSIB (2022 Transformation)
* **Background:** 2021 me Delhi High Court ne ek ruling me kaha ki BBB ke mandate me General Insurance Companies ke General Managers/Directors ko select karne ka legal power nahi tha.
* **Cabinet Decision (1 July 2022):** Appointments Committee of the Cabinet (ACC) ne BBB ke structure ko revamp kiya aur **FSIB (Financial Services Institutions Bureau)** notify kiya.
* **FSIB Mandate:** Ab FSIB ke paas Banks, Financial Institutions ke sath-sath **Public Sector Insurers** ke chiefs ko bhi select karne ka poora kanooni adhikar hai.

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Banks Board Bureau (BBB) was set up based on the recommendations of which committee?
(a) Narasimham Committee  
(b) P.J. Nayak Committee  
(c) Bimal Jalan Committee  
(d) Urjit Patel Committee  
(e) Raghuram Rajan Committee  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** BBB ki sthapna **Dr. P.J. Nayak Committee (2014)** ki sifarisho ke aadhar par hui thi.

#### Q2. In which year was the Banks Board Bureau (BBB) constituted?
(a) 2014  
(b) 2015  
(c) 2016  
(d) 2017  
(e) 2018  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** BBB ko **February 2016** me notify aur constituted kiya gaya tha.

#### Q3. Who was appointed as the first Chairman of the Banks Board Bureau?
(a) Raghuram Rajan  
(b) Vinod Rai  
(c) Subhash Chandra Khuntia  
(d) H.N. Sinor  
(e) K.V. Kamath  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Purve CAG **Vinod Rai** BBB ke pehle Chairman the.

#### Q4. In 2022, the Central Government replaced the Banks Board Bureau (BBB) with which new entity?
(a) Banking Personnel Selection Bureau (BPSB)  
(b) Financial Services Institutions Bureau (FSIB)  
(c) Public Sector Bank Selection Authority (PSBSA)  
(d) National Bank Governance Authority (NBGA)  
(e) Indian Banking Regulatory Board (IBRB)  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** July 2022 me Central Government ne BBB ko replace karke **FSIB (Financial Services Institutions Bureau)** bana diya.
