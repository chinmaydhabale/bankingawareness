## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Banking ki Core Definition:** Public aur entities se paise deposit (jama) ke roop me lena aur zarooratmand logon ko loan (udhaar) dena taaki profit kamaya ja sake.
* **Modern Banking:** Sirf loan aur deposit tak seemit nahi hai; isme Debit/Credit cards, Safe custody (Lockers), ATM services, aur digital fund transfer (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI) bhi shamil hain.
* **Public Sector Banks (PSBs) ka Dabdaba:** Bharat ke total banking system assets ka **70% se zyada hissa** Public Sector Banks ke paas hai.
* **Global Milestone:** Bharat ka **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)** 'Faster Payments Innovation Index' (FPII) me 54 deshon ke beech akela aisa system tha jo **Level 5** par achieve hua.
* **Pehla Bank:** 
  * Western Joint Stock variety: **Bank of Bombay (1720)**.
  * European Management wala pehla aam bank: **Bank of Hindustan (1770)** Calcutta me.
* **Presidency Banks:** Bank of Bengal (1806), Bank of Bombay (1840), Bank of Madras (1843) — teeno ko 1921 me merge karke bana **Imperial Bank of India**, jo 1 July 1955 ko bana **State Bank of India (SBI)**.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 Banking ka Basic Model kya hai?
Socho ek gaon ya shahar me do log hain:
1. **Ramesh (Depositor / Saver):** Ramesh ki salary aati hai aur uske paas ₹1,00,000 extra bache hain. Agar wo ghar me rakhega toh chori ka darr hai aur paisa badhega bhi nahi.
2. **Suresh (Borrower / Businessman):** Suresh ko ek dukan kholni hai jiske liye usse ₹1,00,000 ki zaroorat hai, par uske paas paise nahi hain.

Yahan aata hai **Bank (Madhyastha / Intermediary)**:
* Ramesh apna ₹1,00,000 bank me deposit karta hai. Bank usse kehta hai: *"Hum aapke paise safe rakhenge aur saal ka 3.5% interest (byaj) bhi denge."*
* Bank wahi ₹1,00,000 Suresh ko loan me de deta hai aur Suresh se kehta hai: *"Aapko saal ka 9% interest dena hoga."*
* **Bank ka Profit (NIM - Net Interest Margin):**  
  $$\text{Spread} = 9\% - 3.5\% = 5.5\%$$  
  Yeh jo beech ka 5.5% byaj bacha, yahi bank ki kamai hoti hai jisse bank apne staff ki salary, branch ka rent nikalta hai aur profit kamata hai.

### 📌 Modern Banking: Sirf Deposit-Loan nahi, balki Full Financial Mall!
Aaj ka bank sirf paise lene-dene ka kaam nahi karta:
* **Lockers / Safe Custody:** Agar aapke paas sone ke gehne ya property ke kagaz hain, toh bank locker me safe rakh sakte ho.
* **Cards & Digital Services:** ATM se 24x7 cash nikalna, Debit card se shopping karna, NetBanking/UPI se turant paise bhejna.
* **Third Party Products:** Insurance bechna, Mutual Fund bechna (Bancassurance).

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Important Legal Definitions
1. **Banking:** Aise karobar ko kehte hain jisme public se aise paise deposit ke roop me accept kiye jayein jo maangne par wapas lautane hon (*repayable on demand or otherwise*) aur cheque, draft ya order ke zariye nikale ja sakein.
2. **Bank:** Ek aisi financial institution jo banking activities conduct karti hai.
3. **Banking Company:** Aisi koi bhi company jo banking ka business chalati hai (*Banking Regulation Act, 1949 ke Section 5(c) ke tahet*).

### B. Indian Banking Sector ka Current Market Size
* Bharat ka banking sector RBI ke according **sufficiently capitalised** aur **well-regulated** hai.
* Bharat ke pure banking system assets me **70% se zyada share Public Sector Banks (PSBs)** ka hai, jabki Private Banks ka share chhota hai.
* RBI ne innovative banking models launch kiye hain jaise:
  * **Payments Banks** (Airtel Payments Bank, India Post Payments Bank)
  * **Small Finance Banks (SFBs)** (AU Small Finance Bank, Equitas SFB).
* **FPII (Faster Payments Innovation Index):** 54 deshon ki study me Bharat ka **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)** akela system tha jisko highest rating **Level 5** mili.
* Mobile wallets aur UPI ke aane se fund transfers seedhe wallet to wallet aur bank to bank bina kisi restriction ke hone lage hain.

## 🏛️ 4. History of Indian Banking Sector (इतिहास का पूरा कालक्रम)

### Step 1: Early European Banks (18th Century)
| Bank Name | Sthapna Saal (Year) | Sthan (Location) | Mahatvapurna Facts (Key Exam Points) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bank of Bombay** | **1720** | Bombay | Joint stock variety ka Bharat me **sabse pehla bank** tha. English Agency houses dwara laya gaya. |
| **Bank of Hindustan** | **1770** | Calcutta | Alexander and Company (Agency House) ne banaya. European management ke tahet Bharat ka pehla aam bank. Yeh **1829–1832** me band (liquidate) ho gaya. |
| **General Bank of Bengal and Bihar** | **1773** | Bengal & Bihar | Warren Hastings (Governor, baad me Governor General) ke proposal par bana, par bohot kam samay chala (*short-lived*). |
| **General Bank of India** | **1786** | Calcutta | 1786 me shuru hua aur **1791** me fail (band) ho gaya. |

---

### Step 2: The Three Presidency Banks (ब्रिटिश काल के तीन बड़े बैंक)
East India Company ke 3 administrative units (Presidencies) the: Bengal, Bombay, aur Madras. In teeno me ek-ek bank banaya gaya jinhe **Presidency Banks** kaha gaya. Yeh Royal Charters dwara govern hote the.

| Presidency Bank | Sthapna Ki Tareekh | Capital (Poonji) | Special Power / Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Bank of Bengal** (Pehle *Bank of Calcutta*) | **2 June 1806** (1809 me naam badla) | **₹50 Lakh** | Bharat ka **Pehla Presidency Bank**. Isse **1823** me Currency Notes issue karne ka adhikar mila. |
| **2. Bank of Bombay** | **1840** | **₹52 Lakh** | **Doosra Presidency Bank**. Yeh bank collapse ho gaya, fir January 1868 me *New Bank of Bombay* establish hua. |
| **3. Bank of Madras** | **July 1843** | **₹30 Lakh** | **Teesra Presidency Bank**. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Presidency Banks Act, 1876:** Is act ne teeno Presidency Banks ko ek common kanoon ke andar laya aur unke business par kuch naye restrictions lagaye.  
> **Paper Currency Act, 1861:** 1861 se pehle Presidency banks apne currency note khud chhapte the. 1861 me Paper Currency Act aane ke baad Presidency banks se note chhapne ka adhikar chheen kar **British Government** ko de diya gaya.

---

### Step 3: The Birth of Imperial Bank of India & SBI
* **1921 (Imperial Bank of India):** Teeno Presidency Banks (Bengal, Bombay, Madras) ko aapas me merge (amalgamate) karke **Imperial Bank of India** banaya gaya.
  * Yeh bank akele 3 kaam karta tha:
    1. Commercial Banking (Aam janta se karobar)
    2. Central Banking (Baki banks ko dekhna)
    3. Banker to the Government (Sarkar ka khata sambhalna).
* **1929 (Indian Central Banking Enquiry Committee):** Is committee ne recommend kiya ki Bharat me ek dedicated Central Bank hona chahiye jo sirf currency aur credit ko sambhale.
* **1934 & 1935 (RBI ka Aagman):** RBI Act, 1934 pass hua aur **1 April 1935** ko Reserve Bank of India shuru hua. Banking sector RBI ke control me aa gaya.
* **1 July 1955 (State Bank of India - SBI):** All India Rural Credit Survey Committee (Gorwala Committee) ki sifarish par, Government ne Imperial Bank of India ko nationalise kiya aur **State Bank of India Act, 1955** ke tahet iska naam badalkar **State Bank of India (SBI)** rakh diya! Iska main maksad tha rural aur semi-urban areas me banking suvidhayein pahunchana.

---

### Step 4: First Indian-Owned Banks (भारतीयों के अपने बैंक)
| Bank Name | Year & City | Ownership & Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Allahabad Bank** | **1865**, Allahabad | **First Indian-owned bank** (Private ownership me shuru hua). |
| **Punjab National Bank (PNB)** | **1894/1895**, Lahore | **Second Indian-owned bank**. Lala Lajpat Rai ji aur anya rashtrawadi netaon ke sahyog se bana. Pehla bank jo pure Indian capital se manage hua. HQ: New Delhi. |
| **Bank of India (BOI)** | **7 September 1906**, Mumbai | **Third Indian-owned bank**. Mumbai ke prasiddh vyapariyon dwara sthapit. |

---

### Step 5: Swadeshi Movement (1906) ka Asar
1906 ke Swadeshi Movement ne bhartiya banks ki sthapna ko bohot tez raftar di. 1906 se 1913 ke beech kai bade bhartiya commercial banks bane:
* **Canara Bank** (1906)
* **Indian Bank** (1907)
* **Bank of Baroda** (1908)
* **Central Bank of India** (1911)
* **Bank of Mysore** (1913)

### Step 6: Urban Co-operative Credit Movement
* **Late Shri Vithal L Kavthekar:** Inhone **1889** me Baroda ki riyasat me Urban Co-operative Credit Movement ki shuruat ki thi (*Anyonya Sahakari Mandali*).
* **Pehla Registered Urban Co-operative Credit Society:** **Conjeevaram Urban Co-operative Bank**, jo October **1904** me Madras Presidency ke Conjeevaram (Kanchipuram) me register hua.
* Iske baad Bombay me **Pioneer Urban** (11 November 1905) sthapit hua.
