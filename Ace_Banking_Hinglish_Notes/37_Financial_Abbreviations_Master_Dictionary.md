## 📑 2. Complete A to Z Financial Abbreviations Dictionary

### 🔤 Section A
* **ALM (Asset Liability Management):** Bank ke assets (loans) aur liabilities (deposits) ke beech maturity mismatch aur risk ko manage karna.
* **AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India):** Bharat ki mutual fund industry ka self-regulatory manch.
* **ANBC (Adjusted Net Bank Credit):** PSL targets calculate karne ke liye use hone wala bank ka actual net loan base.
* **ARC (Asset Reconstruction Company):** Banks se bad loans (NPAs) discount par khareedkar unhe recover karne wali company.
* **ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount):** IPO me share apply karte samay bank khate me paisa block karne ki suvidha.

### 🔤 Section B
* **BCBS (Basel Committee on Banking Supervision):** BIS (Basel, Switzerland) me banks ke international regulatory norms banane wali committee.
* **BCSBI (Banking Codes and Standards Board of India):** Customers ke fair treatment ke liye independent watchdog.
* **BIS (Bank for International Settlements):** Central banks ka international bank (Headquarters: Basel, Switzerland).
* **BOS (Banking Ombudsman Scheme):** Customer grievances ko resolve karne wali lokpal scheme.
* **BSBDA (Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account):** Zero balance wala aam bachat khata (No minimum balance penalty).

### 🔤 Section C
* **CAD (Current Account Deficit):** Desh ke total import aur export (goods + services) ke beech ka nakaratmak antar.
* **CAMELS:** Capital, Asset quality, Management, Earnings, Liquidity, Sensitivity to market risk (Bank performance rating model).
* **CAR / CRAR (Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio):** Bank ke paas uske risky loans ke mukable kitni capital surakshit hai.
* **CASA (Current Account Savings Account):** Bank ke low-cost deposits ka ratio.
* **CBLO (Collateralized Borrowing and Lending Obligation):** Ab isse replace karke TREPS (Tri-party Repo) bana diya gaya hai.
* **CBS (Core Banking Solution):** Centralized branch software (Finacle, BaNCS).
* **CD (Certificate of Deposit):** Banks dwara short-term me issue kiya gaya negotiable term deposit (Min 7 days to 1 year).
* **CDR (Corporate Debt Restructuring):** Kist na chuka pane wali companiyon ke loan ki conditions ko aasan banana.
* **CDSL (Central Depository Services Limited):** BSE dwara promoted depository jo shares ko Demat form me rakhti hai.
* **CERSAI (Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest of India):** Property par bane sabhi equitable mortgages ka central fraud-prevention registry portal.
* **CIBIL (Credit Information Bureau India Limited):** Bharat ki sabhi borrowers ka credit score (300 to 900) maintain karne wali pehli bureau.
* **CP (Commercial Paper):** Corporates dwara short-term unsecured loan instrument (Min ₹5 Lakh).
* **CRILC (Central Repository of Information on Large Credits):** ₹5 Crore ya usse zyada ke sabhi loans ka RBI database.
* **CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio):** Bank ke NDTL ka wo percentage jo cash me RBI ke paas rakhna padta hai.
* **CTS (Cheque Truncation System):** Cheque ki digital scan image bhejkar hone wali tez clearing.

### 🔤 Section D & E
* **DICGC (Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation):** Har bank depositor ko ₹5 Lakh tak ka beema dene wali RBI ki subsidiary.
* **DII (Domestic Institutional Investors):** Bharat ke apne mutual funds, LIC, pension funds.
* **DRT (Debt Recovery Tribunal):** ₹20 Lakh ya usse zyada ke bad loans ki tez recovery ke liye special adalatein.
* **ECS (Electronic Clearing Service):** NACH aane se pehle ka purana automated bill/mandate clearing system.
* **EFT / NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer):** 24x7 batch-wise electronic fund transfer system.
* **EMV (Europay, Mastercard, Visa):** Smart chip card security ka global standard.
* **EPC (Export Promotion Council):** Bharat se exports badhane ke liye banayi gayi bodies.

### 🔤 Section F & G
* **FDI (Foreign Direct Investment):** Kisi videshi entity dwara company me 10% ya usse zyada long-term stake lagana.
* **FII / FPI (Foreign Institutional / Portfolio Investors):** Videshi funds jo share bazaar me short-term trading karte hain.
* **FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999):** Videshi mudra len-den ko manage karne wala civil kanoon.
* **FIMMDA (Fixed Income Money Market and Derivatives Association of India):** Bond market ka self-regulatory manch.
* **FINO (Financial Inclusion Network Operations):** Micro-banking aur BC network provide karne wali sanstha (ab Fino Payments Bank).
* **FRBM (Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act, 2003):** Sarkari rajkoshiya ghate (Fiscal deficit) ko kabu me rakhne ka kanoon.
* **FSLRC (Financial Sector Legislative Reforms Commission):** Justice B.N. Srikrishna committee.
* **GDP (Gross Domestic Product):** Desh ki seema ke andar ek saal me utpadit sabhi antim vastuon aur sevaon ka kul moolya.
* **G-Sec (Government Security):** Central ya State government dwara jari kiya gaya 100% risk-free sovereign bond.

### 🔤 Section H, I & K
* **HFC (Housing Finance Company):** Housing loans dene wali NBFC.
* **HNI (High Net-worth Individual):** Bada investor jiske paas karodo ka investable surplus ho.
* **IBPC (Inter-Bank Participation Certificate):** Banks ke beech short-term liquidity aur risk share karne ka certificate.
* **IFSC (Indian Financial System Code):** Bank branch ka 11-digit alphanumeric unique identifier.
* **IMF (International Monetary Fund):** BoP crisis me emergency loan dene wali global sanstha.
* **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):** NPCI ka 24x7 real-time ₹5 Lakh limit wala instant transfer.
* **IPO (Initial Public Offering):** Nayi company dwara pehli baar public ko share bechna.
* **IRAC (Income Recognition and Asset Classification):** NPA aur standard assets par RBI ke prudential provisioning norms.
* **IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India):** Beema sector ka niyamak (Headquarters: Hyderabad).
* **KCC (Kisan Credit Card):** Kisano ko 4% saste byaj par fasal loan dene wali scheme.
* **KYC (Know Your Customer):** Identity (ID) aur Address verify karne ke RBI niyam (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport).

### 🔤 Section L & M
* **LAF (Liquidity Adjustment Facility):** Repo aur Reverse Repo / SDF ke zariye daily liquidity control karne ka RBI tool.
* **LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio):** Basel III ke tahet 30 din ke cash outflows ke barabar High Quality Liquid Assets rakhna.
* **LIBOR (London Interbank Offered Rate):** London ke banks ka benchmark lending rate (ab SOFR se replace ho chuka hai).
* **LTV (Loan to Value Ratio):** Collateral property ki keemat ke mukable bank dwara diya gaya loan percentage (Margin requirement).
* **MIBOR (Mumbai Interbank Offered Rate):** Mumbai inter-bank call money market ka benchmark rate.
* **MICR (Magnetic Ink Character Recognition):** Cheque bottom strip par chapa 9-digit code (City, Bank, Branch).
* **MIGA (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency):** World Bank Group ki political risk insurance arm.
* **MMID (Mobile Money Identifier):** IMPS ke liye use hone wala 7-digit unique code.
* **MPC (Monetary Policy Committee):** Repo rate tay karne wali 6-member statutory body (*Section 45ZB of RBI Act*).
* **MSF (Marginal Standing Facility):** SLR securities dip karke raat bhar (overnight) ke liye emergency RBI loan lena.
* **MSME (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises):** Composite turnover aur investment limits ke aadhar par defined udyog.
* **MUDRA (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency):** Shishu, Kishor aur Tarun loans refinance karne wali agency.

### 🔤 Section N & O
* **NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development):** Kheti aur gramin vikas ka apex development bank (HQ: Mumbai).
* **NACH (National Automated Clearing House):** NPCI ka centralized high-volume electronic payment platform.
* **NAV (Net Asset Value):** Mutual fund ke ek unit ki daily book price.
* **NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Company):** Companies Act me registered financial lending entity.
* **NDTL (Net Demand and Time Liabilities):** Bank ke total demand aur time deposits me se inter-bank balances ghatane par bacha base jispar CRR aur SLR calculate hota hai.
* **NFS (National Financial Switch):** Desh ka shared ATM interconnection network.
* **NHB (National Housing Bank):** Housing finance ka apex refinance institution (HQ: New Delhi, 100% GoI owned).
* **NIM (Net Interest Margin):** Bank dwara loans par kamaye gaye byaj aur deposits par diye gaye byaj ke beech ka shuddh munafa percentage.
* **NPA (Non-Performing Asset):** 90 din se zyada overdue rehne wala dooba loan.
* **NSDL (National Securities Depository Limited):** Bharat ki pehli electronic share depository (1996).
* **NSE (National Stock Exchange):** Bharat ka pramukh electronic stock exchange (Nifty 50 benchmark).
* **OMO (Open Market Operations):** RBI dwara G-Secs ki khareed-farokht karke liquidity inject ya absorb karna.

### 🔤 Section P, Q & R
* **PCA (Prompt Corrective Action):** Financially kamzor banks par RBI dwara branch expansion aur dividend par rok lagane ka framework.
* **PIN (Personal Identification Number):** ATM ya card authentication ka 4 ya 6 digit secret code.
* **PMMY (Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana):** ₹10 Lakh tak ke non-corporate micro udyogo ko sasta loan.
* **POS (Point of Sale):** Merchant counter par card swipe karne wali terminal machine.
* **PPI (Prepaid Payment Instruments):** Wallets, smart cards jisme pehle se paisa load hota hai (PayTM wallet, Amazon Pay wallet).
* **PSL (Priority Sector Lending):** 40% / 75% ANBC ko agriculture aur priority sectors ko dena anivarya hona.
* **PSLC (Priority Sector Lending Certificates):** PSL target pura karne ke liye e-Kuber par trade hone wale certificates.
* **QIB (Qualified Institutional Buyers):** Mutual funds, foreign funds jinhe IPOs me direct allocation milta hai.
* **RIDF (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund):** PSL shortfall ka paisa lekar gramin sadak aur nahrein banane ke liye NABARD dwara managed fund.
* **RRB (Regional Rural Bank):** Central (50%), Sponsor (35%) aur State (15%) ownership wala gramin bank.
* **RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement):** Minimum ₹2 Lakh limit wala 24x7 continuous real-time fund transfer.

### 🔤 Section S to Z
* **SARFAESI:** *Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002*.
* **SCB (Scheduled Commercial Bank):** RBI Act 1934 ke Second Schedule me registered commercial bank.
* **SDR (Special Drawing Rights):** IMF ki international reserve basket asset ("Paper Gold").
* **SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India):** Capital market aur stock exchanges ka niyamak (Statutory 1992, HQ: Mumbai).
* **SFB (Small Finance Bank):** Usha Thorat committee dwara recommended 75% PSL target wala bank.
* **SHG (Self Help Group):** 10 se 20 garib mahilaon ka aapsi bachat aur loan group.
* **SIDBI (Small Industries Development Bank of India):** MSME ka apex development bank (HQ: Lucknow).
* **SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio):** Bank ke NDTL ka wo hissa jo bank ko apne paas liquid assets (G-Secs, Gold) me rakhna padta hai (*Section 24 of BR Act*).
* **SWIFT:** Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication (8 ya 11 characters code).
* **T-Bills (Treasury Bills):** Bharat Sarkar ke short-term discount par bikne wale zero-coupon bonds (91, 182, 364 days).
* **TReDS (Trade Receivables Discounting System):** MSMEs ke unpaid bills ko auction karke turant cash dilane wala platform.
* **UCB (Urban Co-operative Bank):** Shahari kshetra me cooperative principles par chalne wala bank.
* **UPI (Unified Payments Interface):** NPCI dwara chalaaya jane wala instant mobile payment architecture.
* **USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data):** \*99# dial karke bina internet feature phone par banking.
* **WMA (Ways and Means Advances):** Central aur State governments ke receipt-expenditure gap ko poora karne ke liye RBI dwara diya jane wala temporary short-term loan (90 days).
