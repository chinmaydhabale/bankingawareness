## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Payments Bank Kya Hai:** Ek naya differentiated bank model jiska mukhya maksad payments, remittances aur small savings ko digital banana hai.
* **Recommendation:** **Dr. Nachiket Mor Committee** (Committee on Comprehensive Financial Services for Small Businesses and Low Income Households).
* **Minimum Capital:** **₹100 Crore** paid-up equity capital. Promoter contribution kam se kam 40% initial 5 saal ke liye.
* **Do Sabse Badi Pabandi (Two Golden Restrictions):**
  1. **CANNOT give loans or advances:** Koyi bhi loan ya overdraft nahi de sakte!
  2. **CANNOT issue Credit Cards:** Sirf ATM/Debit card de sakte hain, credit card bilkul nahi!
* **Maximum Deposit Limit:** Ek individual customer ke khate me maximum balance **₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh)** ho sakta hai (Shuruat me yeh limit ₹1 Lakh thi, April 2021 me badhakar ₹2 Lakh ki gayi).
* **Funds Deployment Rule:** Demand deposits ka kam se kam **75% SLR-eligible Government Securities / T-Bills** me invest karna compulsory hai (maturity up to 1 year); baki **25%** doosre commercial banks ke paas current/fixed deposits me rakh sakte hain.
* **Pehla Payments Bank:** **Airtel Payments Bank** (January 2017 me live hua).

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Payments Bank vs Normal Bank: Airtel Payments Bank ya IPPB kaise alag hain?
* **Aam Aadmi ka Experience:** Aapka Airtel ka mobile number hi aapka account number ban jata hai. Aap usme ₹5,000 rakhte ho, recharge karte ho, dukandar ko QR code scan karke pay karte ho, ya gaon me mummy ko paise bhejte ho.
* **Lekin shart kya hai?**
  * Agar aap Airtel Payments Bank ke manager se bolo: *"Bhaiya, mujhe dukan ke liye ₹50,000 ka loan de do ya ek Credit Card de do"*, toh manager hath jod lega: *"Sir, RBI ne hamare hath baandh rakhe hain. Hum loan aur credit card nahi de sakte!"*
  * Aur agar aap bolo: *"Mere khate me ₹5 Lakh jama kar lo"*, toh wo bolega: *"Nahi sir, ₹2 Lakh se upar hum ek rupaya bhi nahi rakh sakte!"*

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Permitted vs Prohibited Activities Matrix
| Permitted Activities (Jo kar sakte hain) | Prohibited Activities (Jo BILKUL nahi kar sakte) |
| :--- | :--- |
| • Demand deposits accept karna (Savings & Current accounts). | • **Loans ya advances dena (STRICTLY PROHIBITED).** |
| • Maximum balance up to **₹2,00,000 per customer**. | • **Credit Cards issue karna (NOT ALLOWED).** |
| • ATM / Debit Cards issue karna. | • Non-banking subsidiaries banana. |
| • Net banking, mobile banking, UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS. | • NRI deposits (NRE/NRO accounts) accept karna. |
| • Domestic remittance services (gaon paisa bhejna). | • Fixed Deposits (FD) ya Recurring Deposits (RD) accept karna (sirf demand deposits allowed hain). |
| • Cross-selling: Mutual funds, pension, insurance bechna (as Corporate Agent). | • Time deposits rakhna. |

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Payments Banks were recommended by which committee?
(a) Nachiket Mor Committee  
(b) Usha Thorat Committee  
(c) Bimal Jalan Committee  
(d) Rangarajan Committee  
(e) K.V. Kamath Committee  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Payments banks ka concept **Dr. Nachiket Mor Committee (2014)** ne propose kiya tha.

#### Q2. What is the maximum balance limit that an individual customer can hold in a Payments Bank?
(a) ₹50,000  
(b) ₹1,00,000  
(c) ₹2,00,000  
(d) ₹5,00,000  
(e) No limit  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** April 2021 se RBI ne payments banks me maximum end-of-day balance limit badhakar **₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh)** kar di hai.

#### Q3. Which of the following activities CANNOT be undertaken by a Payments Bank?
(a) Issuance of ATM/Debit cards  
(b) Providing domestic remittances  
(c) Lending activities (Giving loans)  
(d) Distribution of mutual fund units  
(e) Opening savings bank accounts  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Payments banks ko **Lending (loans dena)** strictly prohibited hai.

#### Q4. What percentage of demand deposits must a Payments Bank invest in Government Securities (SLR-eligible)?
(a) 40%  
(b) 50%  
(c) 75%  
(d) 100%  
(e) 25%  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Payments banks ko apne demand deposits ka kam se kam **75%** G-Secs aur T-Bills me invest karna anivarya hota hai.

#### Q5. Which was the first Payments Bank to launch operations in India?
(a) Paytm Payments Bank  
(b) India Post Payments Bank  
(c) Airtel Payments Bank  
(d) Fino Payments Bank  
(e) Jio Payments Bank  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Airtel Payments Bank** January 2017 me poori tarah launch hone wala Bharat ka pehla payments bank tha.
