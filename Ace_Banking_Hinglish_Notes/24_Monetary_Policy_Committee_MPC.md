## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC):** Ek 6-sadasyiya statutory body jo Bharat me Policy Repo Rate tay karti hai taaki inflation target achieve kiya ja sake.
* **Kanooni Aadhar (Statutory Basis):** **Section 45ZB of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934** (Finance Act, 2016 dwara amend kiya gaya).
* **Recommendation:** **Dr. Urjit Patel Committee (2014)**.
* **Total Members:** **6 Members** (3 from RBI + 3 External Experts nominated by Central Government).
* **Chairperson:** **RBI Governor (Ex-officio Chairperson)**.
* **Meetings Frequency:** Saal me kam se kam **4 baar (Minimum 4 meetings per year)** milna compulsory hai (Usually har 2 mahine me - Bi-monthly meeting hoti hai).
* **Quorum:** Meeting ke liye kam se kam **4 members** ka upasthit hona anivarya hai.
* **Voting Procedure:** Har member ke paas **1 vote** hota hai. Agar barabari (3-3 ka tie) ho jaye, toh **RBI Governor ke paas second / Casting Vote** hota hai.
* **Inflation Target:** Headline CPI Combined Inflation = **4% (+/- 2% tolerance band: 2% se 6%)**.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. MPC se pehle vs MPC ke baad: Akele Governor ka Faisla vs 6 Logon ka Vote
* **Pehle (Pre-2016):** Repo rate badhega ya ghatega, iska aakhri faisla akele RBI Governor lete the. Ek Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) hoti thi, par Governor unki salah maanne ke liye baadhya (bound) nahi the.
* **Ab (MPC Era):** Faisla pure loktantrik (democratic) tarike se hota hai. 6 members table par baithte hain aur vote karte hain. Har member ka vote aur uska reason publically disclose hota hai.
* **Tie Breaker:** Agar 3 log bole *"Repo rate badhao"* aur 3 log bole *"Mat badhao"*, tab Governor apna **Casting Vote** use karke tie todte hain!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Composition of MPC (6 Members Breakdown)
1. **Three RBI Members (Ex-officio):**
   * **Governor of RBI:** Chairperson, ex-officio.
   * **Deputy Governor in charge of Monetary Policy:** Member, ex-officio.
   * **One Officer of RBI:** Central Board dwara nominate kiya gaya officer (Executive Director).
2. **Three External Members:**
   * Central Government dwara Search-cum-Selection Committee (Cabinet Secretary, RBI Governor, Economic Affairs Secretary aur 3 experts) ki sifarish par appoint kiye jaate hain.
   * **Eligibility:** Economics, banking, finance ya monetary policy ke reputed experts.
   * **Tenure:** **4 saal ka fixed term**.
   * **Re-appointment Rule:** External members **re-appointment ke liye eligible NAHI hote**.

---

### B. Decision Making & Minutes Publication
* **Quorum:** Kam se kam **4 sadasya**, jisme se kam se kam ek Governor ya unki gair-haziri me Deputy Governor hona chahiye.
* **Decision:** Majority vote se faisla hota hai.
* **Statement Release:** MPC ki meeting khatam hote hi usi din policy statement aur Resolution release hota hai.
* **Minutes of Meeting:** Meeting ke **14 din baad** meeting ke complete minutes (jisme har member ka vote aur reasoning hoti hai) RBI ki website par publish kiye jaate hain.
* **Monetary Policy Report:** RBI har **6 mahine me ek baar** ek detailed Monetary Policy Report publish karta hai jisme inflation ke srot aur forecast bataye jaate hain.

---

### C. Accountability & Failure to Meet Target
* **Failure Condition:** Agar average CPI inflation lagatar **teen tiwahi (three consecutive quarters)** tak target range (2% se 6%) se bahar rehti hai.
* **Report to Government:** Failure hone par RBI ko Central Government ko ek likhit report bhejni padti hai jisme batana hota hai:
  1. Target fail hone ke karan kya the.
  2. Usko theek karne ke liye kya remedial actions liye jayenge.
  3. Kitne samay me (time period) inflation wapas 4% target band me aa jayegi.

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

### 💡 Mnemonic: MPC 6-4-4-2 Rule
* **6** Members total (3 RBI + 3 Govt)
* **4** Quorum (Kam se kam 4 log meeting me)
* **4** Saal ka tenure external members ka (No re-appointment)
* **4** Baar saal me meeting compulsory (Min 4 meetings/year)
* **2**% se 6% inflation tolerance band.

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Under which Section of the RBI Act, 1934, was the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) constituted?
(a) Section 17  
(b) Section 22  
(c) Section 42  
(d) Section 45ZB  
(e) Section 49  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** MPC ka gathan **Section 45ZB of RBI Act, 1934** ke tahet kiya gaya hai.

#### Q2. How many members are there in the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)?
(a) 4  
(b) 5  
(c) 6  
(d) 7  
(e) 9  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** MPC me kul **6 members** hote hain (3 RBI se aur 3 Central Government dwara nominated).

#### Q3. Who is the ex-officio Chairperson of the Monetary Policy Committee?
(a) Finance Minister  
(b) Prime Minister  
(c) RBI Governor  
(d) Deputy Governor in charge of Monetary Policy  
(e) Chief Economic Adviser  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **RBI Governor** MPC ke ex-officio Chairperson hote hain.

#### Q4. What is the tenure of the external members of the MPC appointed by the Central Government?
(a) 2 years  
(b) 3 years  
(c) 4 years  
(d) 5 years  
(e) 6 years  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** External members ka tenure **4 saal** hota hai aur wo re-appointment ke liye eligible nahi hote.

#### Q5. What is the minimum number of times the MPC is required to meet in a year as per the statute?
(a) 2 times  
(b) 3 times  
(c) 4 times  
(d) 6 times  
(e) 12 times  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Kanoon ke mutabiq MPC ko saal me kam se kam **4 baar** milna anivarya hota hai.

#### Q6. What is the quorum required for a meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee?
(a) 3 members  
(b) 4 members  
(c) 5 members  
(d) All 6 members  
(e) 2 members  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** MPC meeting ke liye minimum quorum **4 members** ka hota hai.

#### Q7. In case of a tie of votes in the MPC meeting, who has the casting vote?
(a) Finance Minister  
(b) RBI Governor  
(c) Senior-most external member  
(d) Deputy Governor  
(e) Chief Economic Adviser  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Vote barabar hone par **RBI Governor ke paas second ya Casting Vote** hota hai.
