## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Currency Issue Authority:** **Section 22 of RBI Act, 1934** ke mutabiq Bharat me Banknotes issue karne ka sole right Reserve Bank of India ke paas hai. (Exception: **₹1 ka note aur sabhi sikke Ministry of Finance** issue karti hai).
* **Maximum Denomination:** RBI Act ke **Section 24** ke mutabiq RBI maximum **₹10,000 tak ka banknote** issue kar sakta hai. Coinage Act ke mutabiq maximum **₹1,000 tak ka sikka** dhal sakta hai.
* **Char Note Printing Presses (4 Presses):**
  * **2 Central Govt (SPMCIL) ke adheen:** **Nashik** (Maharashtra - 1928) aur **Dewas** (Madhya Pradesh - 1975).
  * **2 RBI Subsidiary (BRBNMPL) ke adheen:** **Mysuru** (Karnataka - 1996) aur **Salboni** (West Bengal - 1996).
* **Char Coin Mints (4 Taksalein):**
  * **Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Noida** (Sabhi 4 SPMCIL ke adheen hain).
* **Security Paper Mill:** **Hoshangabad (Narmadapuram, MP)** me sthit hai.
* **Clean Note Policy:** 1999 me RBI ne introduce ki taaki public ko saaf-suthre notes milein aur noton par likhna ya stapler marna prohibit kiya jaye.
* **Note Refund Rules:** Soiled notes (gande notes), Mutilated notes (tukde/fate notes) ko bank branch ke counter par bina kisi fees ke badalne ka niyam.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Apne Jeb ke Sikke (Coins) ko Pehchano: Sikke par Chapa Mint Mark!
Kya aapko pata hai ki aapki jeb me jo ₹5 ya ₹10 ka sikka hai, wo kis shahar me bana hai? Sikke ke saal (year) ke theek neeche dekho:
* **Diamond Sign ($\diamond$):** Agar saal ke neeche heera (diamond) bana hai, toh sikka **Mumbai Mint** me dhal kar aaya hai!
* **Star Sign ($\star$):** Agar paanch-kone wala sitara (star) bana hai, toh sikka **Hyderabad Mint** me bana hai!
* **Round Dot ($ullet$):** Agar ek chhota gola/dot bana hai, toh sikka **Noida Mint** me bana hai!
* **No Mark (Koyi sign nahi):** Agar saal ke neeche bilkul khali jagah hai, toh sikka **Kolkata Mint** me bana hai!

### 📌 2. Fata Hua Note: Bank Badalne se Mana Kyun Nahi Kar Sakta?
* Ramesh ke ₹500 ke note ka ek kona phat gaya ya bache ne uspe paani gira diya. Ramesh dukan par gaya toh dukandar ne lene se mana kar diya.
* Ramesh kisi bhi commercial bank ki branch me jata hai (chahe uska wahan khata ho ya na ho!). **RBI Note Refund Rules** ke mutabiq har commercial bank ko Soiled aur Mutilated notes badalkar naya note dena compulsory hai. Agar note ka area 80% se zyada salamat hai, toh poora ₹500 wapas milega; agar 40% se 80% hai toh aadha moolya milega!

### B. Security Features of Indian Banknotes
1. **Watermark:** Mahatma Gandhi portrait aur multidirectional lines, denominational numeral watermark.
2. **Security Thread:** Note ke andar windowed thread jisme *"RBI"* aur *"BHARAT"* likha hota hai. Light me dekhne par continuous line banti hai.
3. **Color Shifting Ink:** ₹100, ₹200, ₹500 ke notes par optical variable ink hoti hai jo note ko tilt karne par green se blue me rang badalti hai.
4. **See-Through Register:** Front aur back ke floral designs ko light ke samne rakhne par complete denomination number dikhta hai.
5. **Bleed Lines & Identification Marks (For Visually Impaired):**
   * ₹100: 4 angular bleed lines dono kinaro par + Triangle mark.
   * ₹200: 4 lines with 2 circles in between + Circle mark.
   * ₹500: 5 bleed lines dono kinaro par + Circle mark.
   * ₹2000 (discontinued): 7 bleed lines + Diamond mark.

---

### C. Soiled, Mutilated & Imperfect Notes
* **Soiled Note:** Aisa note jo ganda ho gaya ho ya do hisson me katkar aapas me chipkaya gaya ho, par jiske saare zaroori hisse salamat hon. Full value refund hoti hai.
* **Mutilated Note:** Jiska ek hissa gayab ho ya jo do se zyada tukdon me ho. Iska refund area-ratio ke hisab se calculate hota hai.
* **Imperfect Note:** Aisa note jiska adha hissa gayab ho, ya jo dhoondhla/wash out ho chuka ho, par jiska genuine hona confirm ho sake.

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Under which Section of the RBI Act, 1934, does the Reserve Bank have the sole right to issue banknotes in India?
(a) Section 18  
(b) Section 22  
(c) Section 24  
(d) Section 28  
(e) Section 42  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Section 22 of RBI Act, 1934** ke tahet RBI ko banknotes issue karne ka sole right diya gaya hai.

#### Q2. What is the maximum denomination of banknote that RBI can issue under Section 24 of the RBI Act?
(a) ₹1,000  
(b) ₹2,000  
(c) ₹5,000  
(d) ₹10,000  
(e) ₹50,000  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Section 24 ke mutabiq RBI maximum **₹10,000 tak ka banknote** issue karne ke liye kanoonan authorized hai.

#### Q3. Which two currency note printing presses are owned and operated by Bharatiya Reserve Bank Note Mudran Private Limited (BRBNMPL)?
(a) Nashik and Dewas  
(b) Mysuru and Salboni  
(c) Mumbai and Kolkata  
(d) Dewas and Salboni  
(e) Nashik and Mysuru  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** BRBNMPL (RBI subsidiary) ki do presses **Mysuru (Karnataka)** aur **Salboni (West Bengal)** me sthit hain.

#### Q4. A coin bearing a 'Star' mark below the year of minting is minted in which city?
(a) Mumbai  
(b) Kolkata  
(c) Hyderabad  
(d) Noida  
(e) Dewas  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Star ($\star$)** mint mark **Hyderabad Mint** dwara dhaley gaye sikkon par hota hai.

#### Q5. Coins minted at Kolkata mint have which mark below the year?
(a) Diamond  
(b) Star  
(c) Dot  
(d) No mark  
(e) Triangle  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Kolkata Mint** ke sikkon par saal ke neeche **koi mark (No mark)** nahi hota.
