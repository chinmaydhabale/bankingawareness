## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Financial Inclusion:** Samaaj ke kamzor aur kam aay wale vargon ko saste daam par basic banking, credit, insurance aur pension suvidhayein uplabdh karana (*Dr. C. Rangarajan Committee, 2008*).
* **BSBDA (Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account):** August 2012 me RBI ne purane 'No-Frills' account ko replace kiya. Zero balance khata, minimum balance penalty nahi, 4 free cash withdrawals per month, free ATM card.
* **Small Account:** Simplified KYC wala khata (Validity: 12 months, max balance ₹50,000, aggregate credits max ₹1 Lakh per year, total withdrawals max ₹10,000 per month).
* **PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana):** Launched **28 August 2014**. Slogan: *"Mera Khata, Bhagya Vidhata"*. Features: Zero balance, RuPay debit card, accidental insurance (₹2 Lakh after 28 Aug 2018; ₹1 Lakh earlier), Overdraft facility up to **₹10,000** (bina condition ₹2,000; age 18-65 years).
* **Social Security Jan Suraksha Trio (Launched 9 May 2015 in Kolkata):**
  1. **PMJJBY:** Age 18–50 yrs, Life cover ₹2 Lakh, Premium **₹436/year**.
  2. **PMSBY:** Age 18–70 yrs, Accident cover ₹2 Lakh, Premium **₹20/year**.
  3. **APY:** Age 18–40 yrs, Pension ₹1,000 to ₹5,000/month after age 60, PFRDA administered.
* **Stand-Up India:** Launched **5 April 2016**. SC, ST aur Mahila udhyamiyon ko greenfield projects ke liye **₹10 Lakh se ₹1 Crore** tak ka loan.
* **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY):** Beti Bachao Beti Padhao. 10 saal tak ki ladki ke liye, min deposit ₹250, max ₹1.5 Lakh/saal, 21 saal maturity.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. PMJDY ka Overdraft (OD) kaise kaam karta hai?
* **Example:** Gaon ki Ramkali ka Jan Dhan account pichhle 6 mahine se theek-thaak chal raha hai aur usme DBT (ration ya subsidy) ka paisa aata rehta hai. Achanak uski beti bimar pad gayi aur khate me balance ₹0 hai.
* **Bank ki Suvidha:** Ramkali bank branch jati hai. Manager uske regular transaction dekhkar usse turant ₹2,000 ka Overdraft (OD) de deta hai bina kisi sawal ke. Agar transaction history bohot achhi hai, toh Ramkali ko maximum **₹10,000 tak** ka OD mil sakta hai. Yeh ek tarah ka mini loan hai!

### 📌 2. Social Security Trio: Chai ke kharch me saal bhar ki suraksha
* **PMSBY (₹20 saal ka):** Ek cup cutting chai se bhi sasta! Saal me sirf ₹20 kat-te hain aur agar accident me mrityu ya permanent apangta ho jaye toh parivar ko ₹2 Lakh milte hain.
* **PMJJBY (₹436 saal ka):** Saal me lagbhag ₹1.20 per day. Agar kisi bhi karan se (beemari ya heart attack) vyakti ki maut ho jaye toh parivar ko ₹2 Lakh milte hain.

### C. Stand-Up India Scheme
* **Launch Date:** **5 April 2016**.
* **Target Beneficiaries:** Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST) aur **Women entrepreneurs**.
* **Nature of Project:** **Greenfield project** (naya enterprise) in manufacturing, services, agri-allied, ya trading sector.
* **Loan Amount:** **₹10 Lakh se ₹1 Crore** tak.
* **Mandate:** Har bank branch ko kam se kam **ek SC/ST borrower** aur **ek mahila borrower** ko loan dena anivarya hai.

---

### D. Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA)
* **Eligible Girl Child:** Birth se lekar **10 saal ki umar tak**. (Ek parivar me maximum 2 ladkiyan, twins/triplets par exception).
* **Deposit Limits:** Minimum **₹250 per financial year**, Maximum **₹1,50,000 (₹1.5 Lakh)**.
* **Deposit Period:** Account khulne se **15 saal tak** deposit karna hota hai.
* **Maturity:** Khata khulne ke **21 saal baad** ya ladki ki 18 saal ki umar ke baad shadi hone par.
* **Partial Withdrawal:** 18 saal ki umar ke baad higher education ke liye 50% balance nikala ja sakta hai.
* **Tax Benefit:** **EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt)** status under Section 80C.

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. When was the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) officially launched?
(a) 15 August 2014  
(b) 28 August 2014  
(c) 2 October 2014  
(d) 1 January 2015  
(e) 8 April 2015  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** PMJDY ko poore desh me **28 August 2014** ko launch kiya gaya tha.

#### Q2. What is the maximum Overdraft (OD) limit available under PMJDY?
(a) ₹2,000  
(b) ₹5,000  
(c) ₹10,000  
(d) ₹15,000  
(e) ₹20,000  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** PMJDY me Overdraft limit ko badhakar **₹10,000** kar diya gaya hai (bina kisi condition ke ₹2,000 tak milta hai).

#### Q3. What is the eligible entry age group for Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)?
(a) 18 to 40 years  
(b) 18 to 50 years  
(c) 18 to 60 years  
(d) 18 to 70 years  
(e) 21 to 55 years  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** PMJJBY me pravesh karne ki umar **18 se 50 saal** hai (risk cover 55 saal tak rehta hai).

#### Q4. What is the annual premium for Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)?
(a) ₹12 per year  
(b) ₹20 per year  
(c) ₹330 per year  
(d) ₹436 per year  
(e) ₹100 per year  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** PMSBY ka revised annual premium **₹20 per year** hai (pehle ₹12 tha).

#### Q5. What is the maximum age limit to join the Atal Pension Yojana (APY)?
(a) 30 years  
(b) 35 years  
(c) 40 years  
(d) 50 years  
(e) 60 years  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** APY me shamil hone ki umar **18 se 40 saal** ke beech honi chahiye.

#### Q6. What is the loan amount range under the Stand-Up India Scheme?
(a) ₹50,000 to ₹10 Lakh  
(b) ₹10 Lakh to ₹1 Crore  
(c) ₹1 Lakh to ₹50 Lakh  
(d) ₹25 Lakh to ₹2 Crore  
(e) Up to ₹10 Lakh  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Stand-Up India ke tehat SC, ST aur Mahila borrowers ko **₹10 Lakh se ₹1 Crore** tak ka loan diya jata hai.

#### Q7. Under Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, what is the maximum age of the girl child at the time of account opening?
(a) 5 years  
(b) 8 years  
(c) 10 years  
(d) 12 years  
(e) 14 years  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Sukanya Samriddhi Account ladki ke janam se lekar **10 saal ki umar tak** khulwaya ja sakta hai.
