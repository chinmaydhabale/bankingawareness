## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Company):** Aisi company jo Companies Act (1956/2013) ke tahet registered ho aur jiska mukhya karobar (Principal Business) loans dena, shares/stocks khareedna, leasing, hire-purchase, ya insurance karna ho. *Section 45-I(f) of RBI Act, 1934*.
* **50-50 Principle (Principal Business Test):** Kisi company ko NBFC tabhi mana jata hai jab uske total assets me se **kam se kam 50% financial assets** hon aur uski total income me se **kam se kam 50% financial income** ho.
* **Bank aur NBFC me Mukhya Antar (Difference):**
  1. NBFC **Demand Deposits (CASA - Current Account & Savings Account)** accept nahi kar sakti.
  2. NBFC **Cheques issue nahi kar sakti** aur Payment & Settlement System (cheque clearing) ka hissa nahi hoti.
  3. NBFC ke depositors ko **DICGC ka ₹5 Lakh insurance cover nahi milta**.
* **Classification:**
  * **Liability ke aadhar par:** NBFC-D (Deposit taking) aur NBFC-ND (Non-deposit taking).
  * **Size ke aadhar par:** Systemically Important (NBFC-ND-SI) jinka asset size **₹500 Crore ya usse zyada** ho.
* **Scale-Based Regulation (SBR - 4 Layers):** Base Layer, Middle Layer, Upper Layer, aur Top Layer.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Bajaj Finance ya Muthoot Finance: Bank kyun nahi hain?
* **Real-life Scenario:** Aapne showroom se ₹50,000 ka smartphone liya zero-percent EMI par. Loan kisne diya? **Bajaj Finance** ne! Ya aapne sona girwi rakhkar cash liya **Muthoot Finance** se.
* **Sawāl:** Agar yeh loan de rahe hain, toh inko Bank kyun nahi kehte?
* **Jawab:** 
  1. Kya aap Bajaj Finance me jakar apna Salary account (Savings account) khol sakte ho jahan jab marzi ATM se paise nikal sako? **Nahi!** (NBFC can't accept Demand Deposits).
  2. Kya Muthoot Finance aapko cheque book de sakta hai jisse aap kisi ko cheque kaat kar de do? **Nahi!** (NBFC can't issue cheques).
  3. Isliye yeh loan toh de sakte hain, par pure bank nahi hain — isiliye inko **Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs)** kehte hain!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Bank vs NBFC (Master Comparison Matrix)
| Parameter | Commercial Bank | NBFC |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Incorporation Act** | Banking Regulation Act, 1949 & SBI/Companies Act | Companies Act, 1956 / 2013 |
| **Demand Deposits (CASA)** | **Haan**, Savings aur Current accounts khol sakte hain. | **Nahi**, strictly prohibited. Sirf Fixed Term deposits le sakte hain (wo bhi sirf NBFC-D). |
| **Cheque Books** | **Haan**, apne naam par cheques issue kar sakte hain. | **Nahi**, self-drawn cheques issue nahi kar sakte. |
| **Payment & Settlement** | Clearing house aur RBI payment systems ka hissa hain. | Hissa nahi hote. |
| **Deposit Insurance (DICGC)** | **Haan**, ₹5 Lakh tak ka beema milta hai. | **Nahi**, koi DICGC insurance protection nahi milta. |
| **FDI Limit** | 74% (Private Banks me) | **100% FDI** allowed (Automatic route). |
| **Reserve Norms** | CRR aur SLR dono maintain karna compulsory hai. | CRR maintain nahi karna hota; NBFC-D ko liquid assets maintain karne hote hain. |

### B. Types of NBFCs (विभिन्न प्रकार की NBFCs)
1. **Asset Finance Company (AFC):** Physical assets (automobiles, tractors, generators) ki financing karne wali company (min 60% assets).
2. **Investment Company (IC):** Doosri securities aur shares ko acquire karne wali company.
3. **Loan Company (LC):** Finance/loan provide karne wali company (AFC ko chhodkar).
4. **Infrastructure Finance Company (IFC):** Total assets ka kam se kam 75% infrastructure loans me lagane wali company (Net Owned Funds min ₹300 Crore, Credit Rating 'A').
5. **Core Investment Company (CIC):** Total assets ka kam se kam 90% apni group companies ke shares/debt me invest karne wali company (Asset size $\ge$ ₹100 Crore).
6. **NBFC-MFI (Micro Finance Institution):** Kam income wale logon ko collateral-free small loans dene wali company (Qualifying assets min 85%).
7. **NBFC-Factor:** Factoring business (receivables discounting) karne wali company (Financial assets min 50%, Income min 50%).
8. **Mortgage Guarantee Company (MGC):** Minimum net owned funds ₹100 Crore, jiska 90% business mortgage guarantee ka ho.
9. **NBFC-Non-Operative Financial Holding Company (NOFHC):** Promoters dwara naya bank shuru karne ke liye banayi jane wali holding company.

---

### C. Public Deposits ke Niyam (NBFC-D Rules)
* Sabhi NBFCs ko deposit lene ki anumati nahi hoti. Sirf wahi NBFCs public deposit le sakti hain jinhe RBI se specific authorisation mili ho aur minimum investment grade credit rating ho.
* **Deposit Tenure:** Minimum **12 mahine (1 saal)** aur Maximum **60 mahine (5 saal)**. (Demand deposits bilkul allowed nahi hain).
* **Ceiling on Interest Rate:** RBI ek maximum interest rate cap fix karta hai jisse zyada byaj NBFC nahi de sakti.

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

### 💡 Mnemonic: 3 'NOs' of NBFC (No CASA, No Cheque, No DICGC)
* **No CASA:** Demand deposits nahi le sakti.
* **No Cheque:** Cheque draw nahi kar sakti.
* **No DICGC:** Doobne par ₹5 Lakh ka beema nahi milta.

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. NBFCs are registered under which Act in India?
(a) Banking Regulation Act, 1949  
(b) Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934  
(c) Companies Act, 1956 / 2013  
(d) Securities Contracts Regulation Act, 1956  
(e) Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** NBFCs ka registration **Companies Act (1956 ya 2013)** ke tahet hota hai, aur unka regulatory supervision RBI Act, 1934 ke Chapter III-B ke tahet RBI karta hai.

#### Q2. What is the minimum financial asset and income threshold (50-50 test) for an entity to be categorized as an NBFC?
(a) 40% assets and 40% income  
(b) 50% assets and 50% income  
(c) 60% assets and 60% income  
(d) 75% assets and 75% income  
(e) 90% assets and 90% income  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Principal business test ke anusar, company ke **50% assets** financial assets hone chahiye aur **50% income** financial income honi chahiye.

#### Q3. Which of the following activities CANNOT be undertaken by an NBFC?
(a) Giving loans and advances  
(b) Acquiring shares and bonds  
(c) Accepting Demand Deposits (Savings/Current account)  
(d) Hire-purchase finance  
(e) Equipment leasing  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** NBFCs ko **Demand Deposits** accept karne ki bilkul permission nahi hoti.

#### Q4. Are deposits in NBFCs insured by DICGC?
(a) Yes, up to ₹1 Lakh  
(b) Yes, up to ₹5 Lakh  
(c) No, insurance facility is not available  
(d) Yes, up to ₹50,000  
(e) Only for government NBFCs  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** NBFCs ke depositors ko **DICGC ka koi insurance cover nahi milta**.

#### Q5. What is the minimum and maximum period for which deposit-taking NBFCs can accept public deposits?
(a) 6 months to 3 years  
(b) 12 months to 60 months  
(c) 7 days to 1 year  
(d) 15 days to 3 years  
(e) 1 year to 10 years  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** NBFCs public deposits kam se kam **12 mahine (1 saal)** aur zyada se zyada **60 mahine (5 saal)** ke liye le sakti hain.

#### Q6. An NBFC-ND is classified as 'Systemically Important' (NBFC-ND-SI) if its asset size is:
(a) ₹100 Crore or more  
(b) ₹250 Crore or more  
(c) ₹500 Crore or more  
(d) ₹1,000 Crore or more  
(e) ₹5,000 Crore or more  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Non-deposit taking NBFC jiska asset size **₹500 Crore ya usse zyada** ho jata hai, use **Systemically Important (NBFC-ND-SI)** classify kiya jata hai.
