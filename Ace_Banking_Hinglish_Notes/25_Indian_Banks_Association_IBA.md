## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **IBA Kya Hai:** Bharat ke sabhi banking institutions ka ek pratinidhi manch (Representative voluntary association of Indian banks).
* **Sthapna:** **26 September 1946** ko 22 banks ke sath sthapit hua.
* **Headquarters:** **World Trade Centre Complex, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai**.
* **Members:** Public Sector Banks, Private Sector Banks, Foreign Banks operating in India, Co-operative Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) aur All-India Financial Institutions.
* **Mukhya Karya (Core Roles):**
  1. **Wage Negotiations (Bipartite Settlement):** Har 5 saal me bank employees aur officers ke salary hike aur working conditions (jaise 5-day banking) par bank unions (UFBU) ke sath samjhauta karna.
  2. **Policy Advocacy:** Sarkar aur RBI ke samne banking industry ki taraf se suggestions aur samasyaayein rakhna.
  3. **Industry Standards:** Cheque clearance, inter-bank transactions aur digital security ke model codes taiyar karna.
  4. **Co-promoter of NPCI:** RBI ke sath milkar National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) ko khada karne me key promoter role nibhana.

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Key Functions & Initiatives of IBA
1. **Bipartite Wage Settlement (द्विपक्षीय वेतन समझौता):**
   * Bank karmchariyon ki salary structure, allowances aur pensions har 5 saal me revise hoti hain. IBA banks ke management ki taraf se aur United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU) karmchariyon ki taraf se aamne-saamne baithkar yeh settlement sign karte hain.
2. **Promoting Banking Technology:**
   * SWIFT (Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication) connectivity ko India me facilitate karna.
   * NPCI ko setup karne me active role aur Indian Banks' Center for Education and Research ko badhava dena.
3. **Dispute Resolution & Legal Advisory:**
   * Member banks ke beech inter-bank disputes ko aapsi baatchit (arbitration) se suljhana.
   * Naye kanoono (jaise SARFAESI ya IBC) par banks ke liye standardized documentation taiyar karna.

#### Q1. When was the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) established?
(a) 1935  
(b) 1946  
(c) 1949  
(d) 1955  
(e) 1969  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** IBA ki sthapna **26 September 1946** ko hui thi.

#### Q2. Where is the headquarters of the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) located?
(a) New Delhi  
(b) Kolkata  
(c) Mumbai  
(d) Chennai  
(e) Bengaluru  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** IBA ka headquarter **Mumbai (World Trade Centre, Cuffe Parade)** me sthit hai.

#### Q3. Which entity conducts the industry-level Bipartite wage negotiations with bank unions in India?
(a) Reserve Bank of India (RBI)  
(b) Ministry of Finance  
(c) Indian Banks' Association (IBA)  
(d) Staff Selection Commission (SSC)  
(e) NITI Aayog  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Bank karmchariyon ke sath wage settlement ka negotiations **Indian Banks' Association (IBA)** karta hai.
