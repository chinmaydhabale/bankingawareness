## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Cheque ki Definition:** **Section 6 of Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881** ke mutabiq Cheque ek aisa Bill of Exchange hai jo hamesha kisi specified banker par draw kiya jata hai aur **hamesha demand par payable** hota hai.
* **Three Parties:**
  1. **Drawer (आहर्ता):** Jo vyakti cheque likhta hai aur sign karta hai (Account holder).
  2. **Drawee (आहार्थी):** Wo specific bank jahan khata hai aur jisse payment karne ka order diya gaya hai.
  3. **Payee (आदाता):** Wo vyakti jisko cheque ka paisa milna hai.
* **Cheque ki Validity (Vaani avadhi):** Issue date se **3 Mahine (Three Months)** tak valid rehta hai (1 April 2012 se lagu; pehle 6 mahine thi).
* **Mukhya Cheque Types:**
  * **Bearer Cheque:** Counter par jo bhi vyakti cheque present karega usse bina ID ke cash mil jayega (*"or Bearer"* word likha hota hai).
  * **Order Cheque:** Sirf named vyakti ko cash milega, verification ya endorsement zaroori hai (*"or Order"*).
  * **Crossed Cheque:** Cheque ke kone par do tirchhi lines ($//$). Counter par cash nahi mil sakta, sirf bank account me deposit hoga.
  * **Account Payee Cheque:** Lines ke beech me *"A/c Payee Only"* likha hota hai. Yeh paisa sirf aur sirf payee ke khate me hi jayega, aage endorse nahi ho sakta.
  * **Stale Cheque:** 3 mahine beet jane ke baad bank me laya gaya expired cheque (Bank payment refuse kar deta hai).
  * **Post-Dated Cheque (PDC):** Future ki tareekh ka cheque. Us tareekh se pehle bank payment nahi karega.
  * **Ante-Dated Cheque:** Pichhli tareekh ka cheque (agar 3 mahine ke andar hai toh valid rehta hai).
  * **Mutilated Cheque:** Phata hua ya kharab ho chuka cheque.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Bearer Cheque vs Account Payee Crossing: Suraksha ka Sabse Bada Khel
* **Bearer Cheque (Khatarnak):**  
  * Ramesh ne Suresh ke naam ₹20,000 ka Bearer cheque likha. Raaste me wo cheque gir gaya. Ek anjan chor (Kallu) ko wo cheque mila. Kallu bank counter par gaya aur bola: *"Yeh cheque cash kar do"*. Cashier bina koi ID maange Kallu ke hath me ₹20,000 cash thama dega!
* **Account Payee Crossing (100% Safe):**  
  * Ab socho Ramesh ne cheque ke top-left corner par do lines kheench kar likh diya **"A/C PAYEE ONLY"**.  
  * Agar yeh cheque gir bhi gaya aur chor ko mil gaya, tab bhi chor cash nahi le sakta! Yeh paisa sirf aur sirf Suresh ke verified bank account me hi transfer hoga, jisse chori asambhav ho jati hai!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Crossing of Cheques (चेक का रेखांकन)
* **General Crossing (Section 123):** Cheque ke face par do parallel transverse lines ($//$) kheench di jati hain, chahe beech me *"& Co."* likha ho ya na likha ho. Iska asar yeh hota hai ki cheque counter par cash nahi hoga, sirf bank account ke through clear hoga.
* **Special Crossing (Section 124):** Lines ke beech kisi specific bank ka naam likh diya jata hai (jaise *"State Bank of India"*). Ab yeh cheque sirf aur sirf SBI ke zariye hi collect ho sakta hai.
* **Account Payee Crossing:** Restrictive crossing hoti hai jisse instrument ki negotiability khatam ho jati hai aur funds sirf payee ke account me credit hote hain.

---

### B. CTS-2010 Standards (Cheque Truncation System)
RBI ne fraud rokne ke liye cheques me compulsory security features banaye:
1. **CTS-2010 Watermark:** Paper me bank ka standardization watermark.
2. **Void Pantograph:** Photocopy karne par copy ke upar bada-bada *"VOID"* ya *"COPY"* likha hua ubhar aata hai.
3. **Ultra-Violet (UV) Ink:** Bank logo UV light ke neeche chamakta hai.
4. **Mandatory Standard Format:** Amount in words, numbers aur signature ke liye fixed grids.

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Cheque is defined under which Section of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881?
(a) Section 4  
(b) Section 5  
(c) Section 6  
(d) Section 13  
(e) Section 138  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Cheque **Section 6 of NI Act, 1881** ke tahet define kiya gaya hai.

#### Q2. What is the legal validity period of a cheque from the date of issue in India?
(a) 1 month  
(b) 3 months  
(c) 6 months  
(d) 1 year  
(e) 15 days  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 1 April 2012 se cheque ki validity date of issue se **3 mahine** hoti hai.

#### Q3. A cheque that is presented to the bank after the expiry of 3 months from the date of issue is called:
(a) Post-dated Cheque  
(b) Ante-dated Cheque  
(c) Stale Cheque  
(d) Mutilated Cheque  
(e) Bearer Cheque  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Expire ho chuke (3 mahine purane) cheque ko **Stale Cheque** kehte hain aur bank iska payment nahi karta.

#### Q4. Which type of cheque cannot be encashed across the bank counter and must be credited to an account?
(a) Bearer Cheque  
(b) Crossed Cheque  
(c) Open Cheque  
(d) Blank Cheque  
(e) Self Cheque  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Crossed Cheque** ka payment counter par cash me nahi milta, balki use bank account me jama karna padta hai.

#### Q5. Who is the 'Drawee' in case of a cheque?
(a) The person who draws the cheque  
(b) The person in whose favor the cheque is drawn  
(c) The specified banker upon whom the cheque is drawn  
(d) The person who endorses the cheque  
(e) The bearer of the cheque  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Cheque ke mamle me **Drawee hamesha wo Bank hota hai** jahan drawer ka account hota hai aur jo payment karega.
