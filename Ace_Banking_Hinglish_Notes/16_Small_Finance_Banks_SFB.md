## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Small Finance Banks (SFBs):** Niche / Differentiated banks jo unserved aur underserved sections (chhoti industries, micro businesses, marginal farmers) ko basic banking suvidhayein (Deposit accept karna aur Loan dena) provide karte hain.
* **Recommendation:** **Usha Thorat Committee** (External Advisory Committee on SFBs).
* **Minimum Capital Requirement:** 
  * Shuruat me: **₹100 Crore**.
  * On-tap licensing guidelines me: **₹200 Crore** (aur Urban Co-op Banks ke conversion par ₹100 Crore initial, badhakar 5 saal me ₹200 Crore).
* **Promoter Contribution:** Kam se kam **40% initial 5 saal tak**, fir 12 saal me 30% aur 15 saal me ghatakar 15% karna hota hai.
* **Two Golden Regulatory Rules (Exam Favorites!):**
  1. **75% Priority Sector Lending (PSL):** Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) ka kam se kam **75%** PSL me jana chahiye (Commercial banks ke 40% ke mukable!).
  2. **25% Rural Branches:** Bank ki total branches ka kam se kam **25% unbanked rural centres** me kholna anivarya hai.
* **Loan Cap:** Bank ke loan portfolio ka kam se kam **50% loan ₹25 Lakh tak ke chhote loans** hone chahiye.
* **Pehla SFB:** **Capital Small Finance Bank** (Jalandhar, Punjab me April 2016 me shuru hua).

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. SFB aur Normal Commercial Bank me kya farak hai?
* **Commercial Bank (jaise HDFC ya SBI):** Badi companiyon ko ₹1,000 Crore ka corporate loan de sakta hai, videsh me branches khol sakta hai, aur uske liye PSL target sirf 40% hota hai.
* **Small Finance Bank (jaise AU SFB ya Equitas):** Yeh aam commercial bank ki tarah **Deposit bhi le sakta hai aur Loan bhi de sakta hai**, ATM/Debit card bhi de sakta hai. Lekin ispar RBI ne strict shart lagayi hai: *"Tum ameer corporate ko bada loan dekar aish nahi kar sakte! Tumhara 75% loan kisan, carpenter, kirana dukandar aur chhote karobariyon ko hi jana chahiye!"*

### 📌 2. Urban Co-op Bank ya MFI ka SFB banne ka fayda
* Pehle kai MFIs (jaise Ujjivan ya Janalakshmi) sirf loan baant-ti thi, public se deposit nahi le sakti thi. SFB ka license milte hi unhe aam janta se saste byaj par Savings account aur FD lene ka adhikar mil gaya, jisse unki funding cost kafi kam ho gayi!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Regulatory Guidelines & Eligibility Criteria for SFBs
1. **Eligible Promoters:** Resident individuals/professionals jinke paas banking/finance me 10 saal ka anubhav ho; NBFCs, MFIs, aur LABs (Local Area Banks).
2. **Foreign Shareholding (FDI):** Private sector banks ki tarah **up to 74% FDI** allowed hai.
3. **Prudential Norms:**
   * Commercial banks ki tarah **CRR aur SLR** dono maintain karna anivarya hai.
   * Minimum Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR): **15%** maintain karna hota hai (Tier 1 min 7.5%).
4. **Transition to Universal Bank:** SFBs jo 5 saal tak satisfactory track record maintain karte hain, wo RBI se **Universal Bank** banne ke liye apply kar sakte hain.

## 🧠 4. Kaise Yaad Rakhein? (Memory Tricks & Mnemonics)

### 💡 Mnemonic: SFB Golden Trio Numbers (75 - 25 - 50)
* **75%** = Priority Sector Lending Target
* **25%** = Branches in Unbanked Rural Areas
* **50%** = Loans must be $\le$ ₹25 Lakh  
👉 *Trick:* **75-25-50** (Pachattar, Pachchis, Pachaas).

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Small Finance Banks were recommended by which committee?
(a) Nachiket Mor Committee  
(b) Usha Thorat Committee  
(c) Bimal Jalan Committee  
(d) Urjit Patel Committee  
(e) R.H. Khan Committee  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** SFBs ki licensing ke liye recommendations **Usha Thorat Committee** ne di thi.

#### Q2. What percentage of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) must Small Finance Banks lend to the Priority Sector?
(a) 40%  
(b) 50%  
(c) 60%  
(d) 75%  
(e) 90%  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** SFBs ke liye Priority Sector Lending (PSL) target **75% of ANBC** hai.

#### Q3. What percentage of branches must a Small Finance Bank open in unbanked rural areas?
(a) 15%  
(b) 20%  
(c) 25%  
(d) 30%  
(e) 50%  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** SFBs ko apni total branches ka kam se kam **25% unbanked rural centres** me kholna padta hai.

#### Q4. Which was the first Small Finance Bank to commence operations in India?
(a) AU Small Finance Bank  
(b) Capital Small Finance Bank  
(c) Equitas Small Finance Bank  
(d) Ujjivan Small Finance Bank  
(e) ESAF Small Finance Bank  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Bharat ka pehla SFB **Capital Small Finance Bank** tha jo April 2016 me Jalandhar (Punjab) me shuru hua.

#### Q5. What is the minimum Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) that Small Finance Banks are required to maintain?
(a) 9%  
(b) 11.5%  
(c) 12%  
(d) 15%  
(e) 18%  
**Answer: (d)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** SFBs ke liye minimum Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio **15%** anivarya hota hai.
