## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Financial Market:** Wo bazaar jahan borrowers (jinhe paise ki zaroorat hai) aur lenders/investors (jinke paas surplus paisa hai) aapas me funds exchange karte hain.
* **Do Mukhya Bhaag (Two Major Segments):**
  1. **Money Market (Mudra Bazaar):** Short-term funds ka bazaar (Tenure: **Up to 1 year / 365 days**). Regulator: **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**.
  2. **Capital Market (Poonji Bazaar):** Medium aur Long-term funds ka bazaar (Tenure: **More than 1 year**). Regulator: **Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)**.
* **Money Market ke Sub-segments:**
  * **Call Money:** 1 din (Overnight) ke liye udhaar.
  * **Notice Money:** 2 din se 14 din ke liye udhaar.
  * **Term Money:** 15 din se 1 saal tak ke liye udhaar.
* **Key Money Market Instruments:**
  * **Treasury Bills (T-Bills):** Sarkar dwara 91, 182, 364 days ke liye zero-coupon discount par issue hote hain (Min: ₹25,000).
  * **Cash Management Bills (CMB):** Sarkar ki temporary mismatch ke liye, maturity **< 91 days**.
  * **Commercial Paper (CP):** Badi corporate companies dwara uncollateralised short-term loan (7 days to 1 year, Min: ₹5 Lakh).
  * **Certificate of Deposit (CD):** Banks dwara issued negotiable term deposit (7 days to 1 year, Min: ₹1 Lakh / ₹5 Lakh).
* **Capital Market ke Do Bhaag:**
  * **Primary Market:** Naye shares/securities pehli baar janta ko beche jaate hain (IPO, FPO, Rights Issue, Private Placement).
  * **Secondary Market:** Pehle se issue ho chuke shares ki khareed-farokht stock exchanges (BSE, NSE) par hoti hai.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Money Market vs Capital Market: Mandi aur Makaan ka Udaharan
* **Money Market (Dukan chalane ka rozmarra ka kharch):** Socho Ramesh ek kapde ki dukan chalata hai. Agle hafte diwali ka maal aane wala hai aur usse sirf 10 din ke liye ₹2 Lakh cash chahiye supplier ko advance dene ke liye. Wo short-term ke liye kisi se paisa leta hai aur 10 din baad maal bechkar wapas kar deta hai. Yeh **Money Market** hai (Maturity $\le$ 1 year).
* **Capital Market (Nayi Factory lagana):** Ab socho Ramesh ko kapde ki ek nayi mill lagani hai jisme ₹50 Crore lagenge aur factory 15-20 saal tak chalegi. Wo itna bada paisa 10 din ya 1 saal ke liye nahi le sakta; usse lambi avadhi (10-20 saal) ke liye equity share ya debenture issue karna padega. Yeh **Capital Market** hai (Maturity > 1 year).

### 📌 2. Call Money, Notice Money aur Term Money: Time ka Khel
Banks ke paas achanak shaam ko pata chalta hai ki unka CRR maintain karne ke liye ₹500 Crore kam pad raha hai:
* **Call Money (1 Din / Raat bhar):** SBI ne HDFC se kaha: *"Bhai, mujhe aaj raat bhar ke liye ₹500 Crore de do, kal subah 10 baje wapas kar dunga!"* (Overnight = 1 Day).
* **Notice Money (2 se 14 Din):** Agar SBI ne kaha: *"Mujhe 5 din ke liye chahiye, agle Monday wapas karunga."* (2 to 14 days).
* **Term Money (> 14 Din se 1 Saal):** Agar SBI ne kaha: *"Mujhe agle 3 mahine ke liye paisa chahiye."* (15 days to 1 year).

### 📌 3. Treasury Bill (T-Bill) kaise kaam karta hai? (Zero-Coupon Magic)
* Socho Bharat Sarkar ko 91 din ke liye paisa chahiye. Sarkar ₹100 Face Value ka T-Bill nikalegi.
* Sarkar aapse kahegi: *"Tum mujhe aaj ₹98 do (Discount price). 91 din baad main tumhe poora ₹100 wapas karungi!"*
* Yahan koi alag se byaj (coupon) nahi milta, balki issue price aur face value ka gap (₹100 - ₹98 = ₹2) hi aapka munafa hota hai. Isliye isse **Zero-Coupon Bond** kehte hain.

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Money Market vs Capital Market (Master Comparison Table)
| Parameter | Money Market (मुद्रा बाज़ार) | Capital Market (पूँजी बाज़ार) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tenure (Avadhi)** | Short-term (Up to 1 year / 365 days) | Long-term (More than 1 year) |
| **Primary Regulator** | **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** | **Securities & Exchange Board of India (SEBI)** |
| **Purpose** | Working capital, liquidity adjustment, short-term deficit | Fixed capital, infrastructure, business expansion |
| **Instruments** | T-Bills, CMB, CP, CD, Call/Notice Money, Repos | Shares (Equity/Preference), Debentures, Bonds |
| **Risk Level** | Bohat kam (High liquidity, Low credit risk) | Zyada (Market fluctuations, Price risk) |
| **Participants** | RBI, Commercial Banks, Primary Dealers, Mutual Funds | Retail investors, FIIs, DIIs, Stock Brokers, Corporates |

### B. Money Market Segments & Key Instruments

#### 1. Inter-bank Call/Notice/Term Money
* **Call Money:** Overnight borrowing/lending (1 day).
* **Notice Money:** 2 days se 14 days tak.
* **Term Money:** 15 days se 1 year tak.
* **Participants:** Scheduled Commercial Banks, Co-operative Banks, aur Primary Dealers (PDs). (Non-bank entities jaise Corporates isme part nahi le sakte).

#### 2. Treasury Bills (T-Bills)
* **Issuer:** Sirf **Central Government** (State Governments T-Bills issue nahi kar sakti; State Govts *State Development Loans - SDLs* issue karti hain).
* **Maturity Tenors:** 
  1. **91-day T-Bill** (Weekly auction)
  2. **182-day T-Bill** (Fortnightly auction)
  3. **364-day T-Bill** (Fortnightly auction).
* **Minimum Amount:** Minimum **₹25,000** aur uske multiples me.
* **Feature:** **Zero-coupon securities** (Discount par issue hote hain, Par value par redeem hote hain).

#### 3. Cash Management Bills (CMBs)
* Central Government ke short-term cash flow mismatch ko poora karne ke liye **2010** me introduce kiye gaye.
* Inki maturity **hamesha 91 din se kam** hoti hai.

#### 4. Commercial Paper (CP)
* **Pehli baar kab aaya:** **1990** me Vaghul Working Group ki recommendation par.
* **Issuer:** Highly rated Corporates, Primary Dealers (PDs), aur All-India Financial Institutions (AIFIs).
* **Nature:** **Unsecured Promissory Note**.
* **Maturity:** Minimum **7 din** aur Maximum **1 saal (365 din)**.
* **Denomination:** Minimum **₹5 Lakh** aur uske multiples me.
* **Eligibility for Corporates:** Tangible Net worth kam se kam ₹4 Crore honi chahiye aur credit rating RBI-approved agency se li gayi ho.

#### 5. Certificate of Deposit (CD)
* **Pehli baar kab aaya:** **1989** me Vaghul Committee ki sifarish par.
* **Issuer:** Scheduled Commercial Banks (RRBs aur LABs ko chhodkar) aur AIFIs.
* **Nature:** Usance Promissory note, Dematerialised form me issued term deposit.
* **Maturity:** 
  * Banks dwara: Minimum **7 din** se Maximum **1 saal**.
  * FIs dwara: Minimum **1 saal** se Maximum **3 saal**.
* **Denomination:** Minimum **₹1 Lakh** (market practice me ₹5 Lakh) aur uske multiples me.

#### 6. Collateralized Borrowing and Lending Obligation (CBLO) / TREPS
* Clearing Corporation of India Ltd (CCIL) dwara chalaaya jata hai. Ab isse replace karke **TREPS (Tri-Party Repo)** bana diya gaya hai.

### C. Capital Market: Primary Market vs Secondary Market

#### 1. Primary Market (New Issues Market)
* **IPO (Initial Public Offering):** Jab koi unlisted company pehli baar apne shares aam janta (public) ko bechti hai.
* **FPO (Follow-on Public Offering):** Jab pehle se listed company dubara naye shares market me issue karti hai.
* **Rights Issue:** Existing shareholders ko unke holding ratio ke hisab se discounted price par naye shares offer karna.
* **Private Placement / Preferential Allotment:** Public offer na karke chuninda institutional investors (QIBs) ko shares bechna.

#### 2. Secondary Market (Stock Exchanges)
* **BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange):**
  * Sthapna: **1875** (Premchand Roychand dwara *Native Share and Stock Brokers' Association* ke roop me). Asia ka **sabse purana stock exchange**.
  * Benchmark Index: **SENSEX** (Sensitive Index - Top 30 companies, Base year: 1978-79 = 100).
* **NSE (National Stock Exchange):**
  * Sthapna: **1992** (Pherwani Committee recommendation), operations shuru huye **1994** me.
  * Bharat ka sabse bada electronic stock exchange.
  * Benchmark Index: **NIFTY 50** (Top 50 companies, Base year: 1995 = 1000).

#### 3. Depositories & Demat System
* **Demat (Dematerialisation):** Physical share certificates ko electronic form me convert karna.
* Bharat me do central depositories hain:
  1. **NSDL (National Securities Depository Limited):** 1996 me sthapit (Pehli depository, Promoted by NSE, IDBI, UTI).
  2. **CDSL (Central Depository Services Limited):** 1999 me sthapit (Promoted by BSE, SBI, HDFC).

* **T**erm Money = **15 to 365** Days (Lamba Term)

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. Which market is associated with short-term financial assets that are close substitutes of money?
(a) Capital market  
(b) Commodities market  
(c) Money market  
(d) Foreign exchange market  
(e) Real estate market  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **Money Market (मुद्रा बाज़ार)** short-term funds (up to 1 year) ka bazaar hai jisme cash aur close substitutes of money trade hote hain.

#### Q2. Name the three sub-markets of the Indian Money Market based on tenure:
(a) Call Money, Notice Money and Term Money markets  
(b) Treasury bills, CDs, Commercial Papers  
(c) Primary, Secondary, Tertiary markets  
(d) Equity, Debt, Derivatives  
(e) None of these  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Tenure ke aadhar par inter-bank money market ke teen hisse hote hain: **Call Money (1 day)**, **Notice Money (2-14 days)**, aur **Term Money (15-365 days)**.

#### Q3. In Call Money market, funds are borrowed or lent for a period of:
(a) 1 day  
(b) 2 to 14 days  
(c) 15 to 30 days  
(d) Up to 1 year  
(e) More than 1 year  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Call money market me funds sirf **1 din (overnight)** ke liye borrow ya lend kiye jaate hain.

#### Q4. In Notice Money market, funds are transacted for a period of:
(a) 1 day  
(b) 2 days to 14 days  
(c) 15 days to 1 year  
(d) Exactly 1 month  
(e) 30 days to 90 days  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Notice money ka samay **2 din se 14 din** tak hota hai.
