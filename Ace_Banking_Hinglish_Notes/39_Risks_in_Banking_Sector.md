## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Banking Risk:** Koyi bhi aisi sambhavna (uncertainty) jiski wajah se bank ke aashit munafey (expected earnings) me kami aaye ya capital ka nuksan ho jaye.
* **Mukhya Categories of Banking Risks:**
  1. **Credit Risk (Default Risk):** Udhaar lene wala vyakti ya company loan aur byaj samay par na chuka sake (NPA risk).
  2. **Market Risk:** Financial markets me utar-chhadhav se securities ki value girna (Interest Rate Risk, Forex Risk, Equity Risk, Commodity Risk).
  3. **Operational Risk:** Internal systems fail hona, fraud hona, manviya laparwahi ya cyber attack hona.
  4. **Liquidity Risk:** Bank ke paas depositors ki withdrawal demands poori karne ke liye cash ya liquid assets na hona.
  5. **Systemic Risk:** Ek bade bank ke fail hone se pure desh ka banking system domino effect me collapse hona.
  6. **Reputational Risk:** Market me bank ki chhavi kharab hona jisse depositors achanak sara paisa nikalne lagein (**Bank Run**).
* **Risk Management Framework:** ALCO (Asset Liability Committee), Basel Capital Adequacy Norms, Credit Appraisal, aur Internal Risk Audits.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. Chaar Mukhya Risks ka Real-Life Masala
* **1. Credit Risk (Vijay Mallya Scenario):** Bank ne Kingfisher Airlines ko ₹7,000 Crore loan diya. Airlines band ho gayi aur paisa wapas nahi aaya. Bank ke sir par NPA ka pahad tut pada. Yeh **Credit Risk** hai!
* **2. Market Risk (Bond Price Crash):** Bank ne 10-year Government Bond khareeda tha 6% interest rate par. Agle saal RBI ne repo rate badha diya aur naye bonds 8% par aane lage. Purane 6% wale bonds ki bazaar me keemat gir gayi. Bank ko portfolio me loss book karna pada. Yeh **Market / Interest Rate Risk** hai!
* **3. Operational Risk (Nirav Modi / PNB Scam):** Branch ke clerk aur manager ne bina kisi authorization ke illegal SWIFT LOU (Letters of Undertaking) issue kar diye aur audit system pakad nahi paya. ₹14,000 Crore ka fraud ho gaya! Yeh internal failure **Operational Risk** hai!
* **4. Liquidity Risk (Silicon Valley Bank - SVB Crash):** Achanak sabhi depositors ko pata chala ki bank sankat me hai. Sabhi log ek hi din mobile app se apna saara paisa transfer karne lage (**Bank Run**). Bank ke paas asset the, par turant dene ke liye cash nahi tha. Bank doob gaya! Yeh **Liquidity Risk** hai!

## 📑 3. Detailed Theory & Har Point Deep-Dive (Zero Point Missed)

### A. Master Risk Classification Matrix
| Risk Type | Definition & Sub-components | Mitigation Method (Bachav ka Tarika) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Credit Risk** | Borrower dwara default karne ka risk.<br>• *Counterparty Risk*<br>• *Country Risk*<br>• *Concentration Risk* | Strict Credit Appraisal, CIBIL/Credit Bureau checks, Collateral mortgage, Exposure ceiling limits. |
| **Market Risk** | On and off-balance sheet positions par adverse market movement se loss.<br>• *Interest Rate Risk*<br>• *Forex (FX) Risk*<br>• *Equity Price Risk* | Duration matching, Interest rate swaps, Hedging, Forward contracts, Value at Risk (VaR) limits. |
| **Operational Risk** | Internal processes, logon, systems ya external events (fraud, fire, cyber attack) se loss. | Strong internal audit, Maker-Checker controls, Dual authorization, Cyber security firewalls, Disaster Recovery sites. |
| **Liquidity Risk** | Commitments meet karne me cash ki kami.<br>• *Funding Liquidity Risk*<br>• *Market Liquidity Risk* | LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio 100%), NSFR, Asset-Liability Management (ALM) monitoring by ALCO. |
| **Reputational Risk** | Negative publicity ya public trust tootna. | Corporate governance, Transparent disclosures, Media relations, Consumer satisfaction. |
| **Systemic Risk** | Contagion effect — ek institution ke fail hone se contagion failure hona. | D-SIB (Domestic Systemically Important Banks) extra capital buffer regulation (SBI, HDFC, ICICI). |

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. The risk of default on a debt that may arise from a borrower failing to make required payments is known as:
(a) Market Risk  
(b) Credit Risk  
(c) Operational Risk  
(d) Liquidity Risk  
(e) Sovereign Risk  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Borrower dwara loan ya byaj na chukane ke risk ko **Credit Risk (ऋण जोखिम)** kaha jata hai.

#### Q2. The risk arising from fraudulent transactions, employee errors, system crashes, or cyber-attacks is classified as:
(a) Credit Risk  
(b) Operational Risk  
(c) Market Risk  
(d) Legal Risk  
(e) Country Risk  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Internal systems, manviya galti ya fraud se hone wale nuksan ko **Operational Risk (परिचालन जोखिम)** kehte hain.

#### Q3. The risk that a bank will be unable to meet its financial commitments (such as depositor withdrawals) as they fall due is called:
(a) Solvency Risk  
(b) Market Risk  
(c) Liquidity Risk  
(d) Credit Risk  
(e) Systemic Risk  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Samay par cash withdrawals poora na kar pane ke risk ko **Liquidity Risk (तरलता जोखिम)** kaha jata hai.

#### Q4. Which committee in a bank is primarily responsible for managing asset-liability mismatch and interest rate sensitivity?
(a) Audit Committee  
(b) Asset Liability Committee (ALCO)  
(c) Risk Management Committee  
(d) Executive Committee  
(e) Credit Sanction Committee  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Bank me maturity mismatch aur liquidity monitor karne ka kaam **ALCO (Asset Liability Committee)** karti hai.
