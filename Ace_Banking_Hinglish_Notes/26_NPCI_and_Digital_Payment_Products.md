## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **NPCI Full Form:** **National Payments Corporation of India** (Bharat me retail payments aur settlement systems ko chalane wali umbrella organisation).
* **Sthapna:** **2008** me *Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007* ke tahet sthapit.
* **Corporate Structure:** Not-for-profit company under **Section 25 of Companies Act, 1956 (now Section 8 of Companies Act, 2013)**.
* **Promoters:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI) aur Indian Banks' Association (IBA) dwara 10 core promoter banks ke sath banaya gaya. HQ: **Mumbai**.
* **Key Digital Products Suite:**
  1. **NFS (National Financial Switch):** Desh ka shared ATM network (2009 me IDRBT se liya gaya).
  2. **IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):** Instant 24x7 interbank fund transfer (Launch: **22 Nov 2010**, Max limit: ₹5 Lakh).
  3. **RuPay:** Bharat ka indigenous card payment scheme (Launched 2012).
  4. **AePS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System):** Micro-ATMs par biometric finger scan se cash nikalna aur balance check karna.
  5. **APBS (Aadhaar Payment Bridge System):** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) subsidy sarkari khate se seedhe janta ke account me bhejna.
  6. **NACH (National Automated Clearing House):** ECS ko replace karne wala centralized bulk clearing system (Salaries, Dividends, EMI debits).
  7. **CTS (Cheque Truncation System):** Physical cheque ko chalaye bina digital image dwara clearing karna.
  8. ***99# (USSD):** Bina internet ke basic feature phone se mobile banking (NUUP - National Unified USSD Platform).
  9. **UPI (Unified Payments Interface):** Virtual Payment Address (VPA) par instant mobile-to-mobile transfer (Launch: **11 April 2016**).
  10. **BHIM (Bharat Interface for Money):** NPCI ka flag-bearer UPI app (Launch: **30 Dec 2016**).
  11. **Bharat BillPay (BBPS):** Bijli, pani, gas, dth ka interoperable bill payment platform.
  12. **NETC / FASTag:** RFID technology se highway toll plazas par contactless automatic toll collection.

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. UPI (Unified Payments Interface) ki Kranti (Revolution)
* **Puraana Daur (NEFT/RTGS):** Agar kisi dost ko ₹500 bhejne the, toh pehle uska 16-digit account number maango, IFSC code dhundo, NetBanking me beneficiary add karo, aadhe ghante ka cooling period intezar karo, fir OTP daalkar bhejo!
* **UPI ka Jaadu (VPA & QR Code):** NPCI ne sab aasan kar diya. Bas ek **Virtual Payment Address (VPA)** banaya (jaise `ramesh@okhdfcbank` ya mobile number). Chai ki tapri par gaye, phone se QR code scan kiya, UPI PIN daala aur 2 second me paisa transfer!
* **Global Wonder:** Aaj poori duniya (Singapore, UAE, France, Nepal, Mauritius) me Bharat ka UPI apnaaya ja raha hai!

### 📌 2. AePS: Ungli lagao, paisa paao (Biometric Banking)
* Gaon ke budurg Dadu ji jinhe na ATM card chalana aata hai, na padhna aata hai, aur na shahar ja sakte hain.
* Gaon ke chauraha par **Bank Mitra (Business Correspondent)** Micro-ATM lekar baithta hai. Dadu ji apna 12-digit **Aadhaar Number** batate hain aur scanner par **Angootha (fingerprint)** lagate hain.
* NPCI ka **AePS** server verify karta hai aur Bank Mitra unke haath me unki ₹1,000 ki vridha pension cash me de deta hai!

| **Settlement Type** | Real-time Gross | Real-time Gross | Half-hourly Batches (48 batches) | Real-time Gross (STP) |

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. NPCI was established under which Act?
(a) Banking Regulation Act, 1949  
(b) Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007  
(c) RBI Act, 1934  
(d) Companies Act, 2013  
(e) Information Technology Act, 2000  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** NPCI ki sthapna **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007** ke pravdhano ke tahet hui thi.

#### Q2. NPCI is registered under which Section of the Companies Act as a Not-for-Profit company?
(a) Section 8 (Companies Act 2013) / Section 25 (Companies Act 1956)  
(b) Section 12  
(c) Section 19  
(d) Section 45  
(e) Section 138  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** NPCI ek Not-for-Profit company hai jo **Section 25 of Companies Act, 1956 (now Section 8 of Companies Act, 2013)** ke tahet incorporated hai.

#### Q3. What is the standard transaction limit for IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)?
(a) ₹1 Lakh  
(b) ₹2 Lakh  
(c) ₹5 Lakh  
(d) ₹10 Lakh  
(e) ₹20 Lakh  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** RBI aur NPCI ne IMPS ki limit ko badhakar **₹5 Lakh** kar diya hai.

#### Q4. In which year was the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) officially launched?
(a) 2014  
(b) 2015  
(c) 2016  
(d) 2017  
(e) 2018  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** UPI ko **11 April 2016** ko Dr. Raghuram Rajan (Governor RBI) dwara launch kiya gaya tha.

#### Q5. What is the USSD code used for mobile banking on feature phones without internet?
(a) \*121#  
(b) \*99#  
(c) \*100#  
(d) \*400#  
(e) \*199#  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Feature phones par NUUP platform ke zariye mobile banking karne ke liye **\*99#** dial kiya jata hai.

#### Q6. What technology does FASTag / NETC use for automatic toll collection?
(a) Bluetooth  
(b) Infrared  
(c) RFID (Radio Frequency Identification)  
(d) QR Code  
(e) NFC only  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** FASTag gaadi ke windshield par laga hota hai aur yeh **RFID (Radio Frequency Identification)** technology par kaam karta hai.
