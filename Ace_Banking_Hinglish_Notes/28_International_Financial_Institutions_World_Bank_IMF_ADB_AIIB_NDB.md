## 🎯 1. Concept at a Glance (एक नज़र में)

* **Bretton Woods Conference (July 1944):** New Hampshire, USA me aayojit conference jisme do sansthaayein paida hui jinhe **Bretton Woods Twins** kaha jata hai: **World Bank (IBRD)** aur **International Monetary Fund (IMF)**.
* **World Bank Group (5 Institutions):**
  1. **IBRD (International Bank for Reconstruction and Development - 1944):** Middle-income deshon ko vikas hetu loan dena.
  2. **IDA (International Development Association - 1960):** 'Soft Loan Window' — Gareeb deshon ko zero/low interest aur 30-40 saal ki lambi avadhi par concessional credit dena.
  3. **IFC (International Finance Corporation - 1956):** Developing countries me private sector companies ko equity aur debt finance dena.
  4. **MIGA (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency - 1988):** Videshi private investors ko political risk (yuddh, expropriation) se insurance dena.
  5. **ICSID (International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes - 1966):** Government aur foreign investors ke vivado ko suljhana. *(Important Note: Bharat ICSID ka member NAHI hai!)*
  * **HQ:** **Washington D.C., USA**.
* **IMF (International Monetary Fund):**
  * Sthapna: **1944 / 1945**. HQ: **Washington D.C.**
  * Kaam: Global monetary cooperation, financial stability, exchange rate stability, aur **Balance of Payments (BoP) crisis** me member countries ko emergency bailout loan dena.
  * **SDR (Special Drawing Rights - 'Paper Gold'):** 1969 me banayi gayi international reserve asset. Currency basket me 5 currencies hain: **US Dollar, Euro, Chinese Renminbi, Japanese Yen, British Pound Sterling**.
* **Asian Development Bank (ADB):**
  * Sthapna: **19 December 1966**. HQ: **Mandaluyong, Manila, Philippines**.
  * Largest shareholders: **Japan aur USA (barabar share)**.
* **New Development Bank (NDB - BRICS Bank):**
  * Sthapna: **2014 (Fortaleza Declaration)**, operations shuru 2015. HQ: **Shanghai, China**.
  * Pehle President: **K.V. Kamath** (Bharat).
* **AIIB (Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank):**
  * Sthapna: **2015 / 2016**. HQ: **Beijing, China**.
  * Largest Shareholder: **China**, Doosra sabse bada shareholder: **Bharat (India)**!

## 💡 2. Easy Hinglish Explanation with Real-Life Examples (आसान भाषा में Real-Life Example)

### 📌 1. World Bank vs IMF: Asli Farak Kya Hai?
* **World Bank (Vikas ka Paisa - Highway, School, Hospital):**  
  * Socho Bharat ko bullet train chalani hai ya Ganga safai karni hai ya gaon-gaon me bijli pahunchani hai. Yeh long-term development projects hain. Iske liye sasta aur 25-30 saal ka loan kaun dega? **World Bank**!
* **IMF (ICU ka Doctor - Jab Tijori Khali Ho Jaye):**  
  * Ab socho kisi desh (jaise 1991 me Bharat ya haal hi me Sri Lanka aur Pakistan) ke paas petrol aur medicine import karne ke liye videshi currency (Dollar) bachi hi nahi (Foreign Exchange Crisis / BoP Crisis). Desh bankrupt hone ki naubat par aa gaya. Tab emergency bailout loan lekar aakhri doctor ke roop me kaun aata hai? **IMF**!

### 📌 2. SDR (Special Drawing Rights): "Paper Gold" kya hai?
* SDR koi physical currency ya note nahi hota jisse aap market me chai peene jao. Yeh IMF ki apni hisab-kitab ki artificial mudra (accounting unit) hai. Agar kisi desh ke paas dollar kam pad jaye, toh wo IMF me apne SDR ko dollar, euro ya yen me exchange karke import payment kar sakta hai!

## 📝 5. Chapter Exercise & Exam Practice (With Hinglish Solutions)

#### Q1. The Bretton Woods conference of 1944 led to the creation of which two institutions?
(a) World Bank and WTO  
(b) IMF and World Bank  
(c) UN and IMF  
(d) ADB and NDB  
(e) GATT and WTO  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** 1944 ki Bretton Woods conference se **IMF aur World Bank (IBRD)** bane the jinhe 'Bretton Woods Twins' kaha jata hai.

#### Q2. Which entity is known as the 'Soft Loan Window' of the World Bank?
(a) IBRD  
(b) IDA  
(c) IFC  
(d) MIGA  
(e) ICSID  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** **IDA (International Development Association)** gareeb deshon ko bina byaj ke concessional loans deti hai, isliye isse World Bank ki 'Soft Loan Window' kehte hain.

#### Q3. India is a member of all the World Bank Group institutions EXCEPT:
(a) IBRD  
(b) IDA  
(c) IFC  
(d) MIGA  
(e) ICSID  
**Answer: (e)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** Bharat **ICSID (International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes)** ka member nahi hai.

#### Q4. Where is the headquarters of Asian Development Bank (ADB) located?
(a) Tokyo, Japan  
(b) Beijing, China  
(c) Manila, Philippines  
(d) Jakarta, Indonesia  
(e) Bangkok, Thailand  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** ADB ka headquarter **Mandaluyong, Manila (Philippines)** me sthit hai.

#### Q5. Who was the first President of the New Development Bank (NDB) established by BRICS?
(a) K.V. Kamath  
(b) Raghuram Rajan  
(c) Dilma Rousseff  
(d) Marcos Troyjo  
(e) Bimal Jalan  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** NDB ke pehle President Bharat ke **K.V. Kamath** the.

#### Q6. Which country is the second largest shareholder in the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) after China?
(a) Russia  
(b) India  
(c) Germany  
(d) Japan  
(e) South Korea  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Explanation (Hinglish):** AIIB me China sabse bada shareholder hai aur **Bharat (India)** doosra sabse bada shareholder hai.
